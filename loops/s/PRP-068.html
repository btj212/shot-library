Prompt

Reference mapping

- @Image1 is the five-product complexion board: VEIL, TRACE, SHADE, HALO and LUME.
- @Image2 is the five-product color board: FLUSH, SLIP, GLAZE, LASH and FRAME.
- @Image3 is the five-product finish and fragrance board: CLOUD, QUAD, SKIN, AFTER and POCKET.
- @Image4 is the five-product body and hand board: RINSE, SOFT, MILK, HANDS and SHEEN.

Each product board contains five distinct SKUs. Preserve every shown product’s exact shape, scale, material, color, closure, applicator and package lettering; never merge products or identities.

Global brief
Create one exact 30-second, 16:9 product-launch film performed by four adult friends. A brushed-silver MIRRO launch case arrives while they are getting ready; the new collection moves with them from apartment to elevator, taxi, launch venue bathroom and dawn kitchen. Every product change happens through a completed human action, a physical handoff or a motivated match cut. Products never float. The film announces a real launch through story, behavior and VO rather than on-screen text.

Visual and art style
Clean Y2K beauty editorial with candid human energy: pearl-white bathroom tile, brushed stainless steel, powder blue, transparent cherry red, shell pink and smoky plum; fitted knits, low-slung tailored trousers, silver jewelry and small shoulder bags. Warm matte skin, bright eyes, readable faces, no oily highlights or dark eye sockets. Alternate restrained 35–50mm ensemble coverage, crisp direct-flash 85mm reactions and tactile 100mm product macros. Mild 35mm grain, hard side light, precise shadow edges and occasional early-digital flash bloom.
