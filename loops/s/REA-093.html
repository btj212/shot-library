Prompt

One 30-second 16:9 photorealistic observational macro nature film. Use the nine supplied images as ordinary visual references, not time-locked keyframes. Preserve the same resting dragonfly: pale-yellow body with bold black stripes, large pale-green compound eyes, thin dark legs gripping a slender grass stem, four transparent finely veined wings bearing clear dew. Soft green meadow, shallow depth of field, natural morning light with a gentle cool-to-golden progression. Keep the insect and the same meadow coherent; no sudden takeoff, extra insects, deformed anatomy, artificial sparkles, species or location labels. Quiet breeze through grass and soft natural insect ambience throughout, no dialogue, no narration, no music.
