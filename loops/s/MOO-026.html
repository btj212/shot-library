Standing Still While the World Moves — Seedance 2.5

A young woman with long, straight pastel-pink hair and soft bangs stands perfectly still in the middle of a busy city street during golden hour. She wears a beautiful orange satin mini dress with delicate ruffled hems and black ankle boots. Her expression is calm, slightly melancholic, and emotionally distant, as if she feels left behind while the entire world moves forward.

Around her, yellow taxis, cars, and city traffic rush past in both directions with realistic motion blur, creating a powerful contrast between her stillness and the fast-moving world. The cars pass close to her without touching her. Her hair and dress move gently in the breeze while she remains completely motionless, looking straight ahead.
