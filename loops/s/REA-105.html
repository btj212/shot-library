Prompt

Create a high-energy, realistic 15-second User Generated Content (UGC) product advertisement featuring a Jamaican-Canadian man in his mid-to-late 20s unboxing and testing a premium smart bike light and computer set.

The video should feel like authentic smartphone footage captured at home, not a traditional commercial. Use rapid jump cuts, satisfying ASMR sounds, natural reactions, realistic movements, and cinematic product close-ups.

PRODUCT: CYCLEBEAM X2 SMART BIKE LIGHT.

A premium rechargeable bike light featuring a matte-black aluminum body with brushed neon-yellow accents, a high-intensity LED lens with a wide beam pattern, a built-in ambient light sensor, a single illuminated power button, a small LED battery indicator, a quick-release handlebar mount with a rubber strap, a USB-C charging port, a rear companion tail light, and a soft zippered carrying pouch.

SHOT 1: THE ARRIVAL (0-2s)

[Medium shot, eye-level]

The man sits on his apartment floor with a small delivery box branded "CYCLEBEAM X2" in front of him, his bike leaning against the wall behind him. He tears the sealing tape open in one satisfying motion.

SFX: Loud, crisp tape ripping sound.

SHOT 2: THE REVEAL (2-4s)

[Medium shot, slightly low angle]

He lifts the lid, pulls the light and tail light from the carrying pouch, and reveals the matte-black body and neon-yellow accents. He looks genuinely impressed.

Dialogue: "Okay, this finally came!"

SFX: Cardboard movement, pouch zipper opening, soft packaging rustling.

SHOT 3: THE CLOSE LOOK (4-7s)

[Full shot, clean jump cut]

He holds the CYCLEBEAM X2 up to the camera, runs his thumb along the neon-yellow trim, and clicks it into the quick-release mount. He taps the power button, which glows, and the LED battery indicator lights up.

Dialogue: "Look at this! It's so small but feels so solid."

SFX: Mount clicking, soft button click, power-on chime.

SHOT 4: THE BEAM TEST (7-11s)

[Close-up, 45-degree angle]

He straps the light onto the handlebar, clips the tail light under the saddle, and dims the room. He presses the button to cycle through modes: steady, pulse, and flash. A bright, wide beam cuts across the wall, and the tail light blinks red. He raises his eyebrows, clearly impressed by the brightness.

SFX: Rubber strap tightening, soft button clicks, mode-change beeps.

SHOT 5: THE FINAL EXPERIENCE (11-13s)

[Medium shot, eye-level]

He wheels the bike outside at dusk and the light automatically brightens as the sky darkens. He gives a thumbs-up and smiles directly at the camera.

Dialogue: "Wait... I'm riding at night now!"

SHOT 6: HERO PRODUCT SHOT (13-15s)

[Slow cinematic close-up]

The CYCLEBEAM X2 sits mounted on the handlebar, glowing softly beside its open box, tail light, and carrying pouch. Warm evening light reflects across the matte-black body and neon-yellow accents.

Final dialogue: "This is officially my new favourite upgrade."
