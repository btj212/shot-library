A cinematic Halloween night scene: a massive gothic stone castle perched on a rocky cliff under a huge full moon and stormy clouds. Lightning flashes across the sky. A winding cobblestone path lined with glowing jack-o’-lanterns leads up to the castle’s huge wooden doors.
The doors slowly open, releasing thick purple mist. Out step three characters who begin an energetic synchronized dance: a pale vampire in a black formal suit with a flowing black cape lined in red, a skeleton wearing a black hoodie and matching pants with bold orange graphic designs (stylized lettering and splatters), and a witch in a dramatic purple Victorian dress, corset, boots, and pointed hat.
They dance through the grand candlelit castle hall — black-and-white checkered marble floors, crystal chandeliers, tall arched windows, and pumpkins scattered around. The scene shifts to the castle battlements under the full moon, with bats flying and purple lightning in the sky. They continue the choreography, then finish outside the castle entrance surrounded by dozens of floating and ground-level glowing jack-o’-lanterns, purple fog, autumn leaves, and warm lantern light.
