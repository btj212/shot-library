Prompt

CAMERA:
DV tape camcorder handheld aesthetic. A woman films herself directly by hand in selfie-cam and first-person style through her first gym session after months away. Keep realistic hand shake, slightly crooked framing, delayed autofocus, awkward zoom-ins and zoom-outs, occasional motion blur, and small framing mistakes where part of her face briefly slips out of frame. Mix handheld selfie footage with fixed external shots. ABSOLUTE RULE: no camera, camcorder, phone or recording device ever appears in the frame, in her hands, on any surface, or in the mirror reflection — her hands are always empty of equipment. Everything is seen from the recording camera's own point of view. When the shot becomes hands-free, do NOT show her putting anything down: the IMAGE ITSELF swings, tips and settles on a surface, and we understand what happened purely from what the picture does. When switching from handheld to a fixed external angle, use a clean jump cut as if the camera had already been positioned before the shot began. The camcorder itself is never visible on screen.

LOOK:
Soft digital tape camcorder look with subtle vintage DV character. Slight blur, faint tape noise, mild highlight bloom under the overhead lights, subtle auto-exposure flicker, muted contrast, realistic skin texture, believable indoor lighting. The room is an ordinary everyday gym and nothing in it is designed or boutique — the colour does all the work: warm sodium overhead strip light, one crimson exercise mat on black rubber flooring, cobalt-blue padded equipment, a single bright yellow towel. Saturated primaries sitting inside a completely plain room.

STYLE:
A realistic late-night gym vlog. The tone is dry, sheepish, slightly breathless and very natural. She is charismatic and funny about herself, but it must read as a genuine self-recorded vlog and never as a commercial or a fitness advert. Clean jump cuts, strong continuity, natural body language, no dead moments.

The woman in @Image1. Keep her face, hair and wardrobe exactly as shown: a French-Algerian woman in her mid-thirties, warm olive skin, a sharp jaw, a small scar through one eyebrow, deep-set dark eyes, hair scraped back into a low ponytail, no makeup, visible skin texture, wearing a plain black long-sleeve athletic top and black leggings, white trainers, and a bright yellow towel over one shoulder. The same woman remains fully consistent in every shot: same face, same low ponytail, same black top and leggings, same yellow towel. No duplicated limbs, no broken hands, no broken gym equipment, no impossible movements. The water bottle stays on the bench until she picks it up at cut 7. STORYBOARD (30 seconds total, 10 cuts):

1. (~3s, arm's-length selfie) She walks in filming herself, shoulders slightly hunched, sheepish. Dialogue: "Right. First time back in… we don't say the number out loud."

2. (~3s, handheld pan across the room and back to her) The camera drifts across the dumbbell rack, the mirror wall and the empty floor, then returns to her face. Dialogue: "Nobody here. Which is exactly how I planned it."

3. (~4s, handheld arm's-length selfie at the mirror) She stops at the mirror holding the camera herself at arm's length, reaches up with her free hand and tightens the ponytail, and catches her own eye in the glass. Then, on the last word, the IMAGE ITSELF swings down, tips over and settles at a wonky angle resting on the end of the bench, still running — she has freed up both hands. No camera is ever seen. Dialogue: "I look like someone about to make a mistake."

4. (~3s, from the wonky resting angle on the bench) Continuing from where the image came to rest. She steps back into frame, picks up two dumbbells and tests the weight in one hand before committing. No dialogue.

5. (~4s, same resting angle) She performs a short set of presses. It is visibly harder than she expected: the first rep is clean, the second slows, and the third she has to grind out, arms shaking slightly at the top. She drops the weights down to her chest and then to the floor, doubles over her knees and laughs at herself while completely out of breath. The line is broken up by real effort — grunting, gasping and a breathless laugh between the words, not delivered cleanly. Dialogue, breathless and grunting through it: "Whoo— (grunts) okay. Okay, that's— (huffs) that's it. Too much. (breathless chuckle) Whew."

6. (~3s, handheld walking selfie) Back in selfie mode, towel over one shoulder, walking it off, breathing noticeably harder. Dialogue: "My arms have filed a complaint."

7. (~3s, tight handheld close-up) She picks up the water bottle, drinks, lowers it and exhales in relief. Dialogue: "This is the part I'm actually good at."

8. (~3s, fixed external wider shot facing the mirror) Jump cut to a fixed shot. She does a short stretch, catches her own reflection, and laughs at herself, looking away from the lens to do it. Dialogue: "Stretching. So it counts as a real session."

9. (~3s, handheld close selfie, extreme close on her face) She is visibly worked: skin shining with sweat, a damp hairline with short strands stuck to her temples and the back of her neck, deep colour high in her cheeks, chest still moving with her breathing, one bead of sweat at the jaw. She pushes a stuck strand of hair off her forehead with the back of her wrist, tired and quietly pleased. Dialogue: "I said twenty minutes. It's been fifty."

10. (~3s, arm's-length selfie ending) She walks out through the corridor still filming herself, still flushed and damp, and gives a small genuine smile straight into the lens. She stays in frame for the whole shot — the film ends on HER face, never on an empty room. Dialogue: "Same time Thursday. Probably. Night."

ACTING NUANCE:
The sheepishness at cut 1 lives in her shoulders, not in her voice. At cut 4 she genuinely weighs the dumbbell in one hand before she commits to it. Cut 5 is the honest beat of the whole film: the effort is real and it beats her, her arms shake on the last rep, and the breathing is LOUDER than the words — she is fighting to get the sentence out between gasps rather than speaking over the top of them. The laugh at the end of it is genuine self-mockery, aimed at nobody. At cut 8 the laugh is at herself and she breaks eye contact with the lens to do it. At cut 10 the smile is small and real, not a performed sign-off. The sweat and the flush do not disappear between cuts 9 and 10 — she is still recovering right through the last frame.

Do not add any instrumental score, soundtrack or background music.

FINAL INSTRUCTION:
The result must feel like a real self-recorded late-night gym vlog in an ordinary gym. Prioritise realistic handheld motion, strong continuity, believable pacing, natural breathing and subtle imperfection. The ending is complete, not cut off: she delivers the final line to the lens, holds her own gaze for a beat, and the recording ends on her face. This is a selfie vlog, so it never cuts away to an empty room. No subtitles.
