[ACTION & CAMERA]
0–5s: Wide, low, front-facing tracking shot. Seo-yeon walks slowly down the stone staircase with the closed umbrella in her right hand, its steel tip tapping each step. Seven opponents wait in the courtyard below. Opponent 7 (charcoal coat) says in a low, amused tone: "You walked into the wrong market tonight." She tilts her head slightly and answers calmly: "Then I'll leave it a little tidier." Opponents 1 and 2 charge. She steps off the last stair, flicks the umbrella around so the brass hook catches Opponent 1's ankle, and pulls sharply. He drops flat on the wet stone with a splash. Camera drops with the pull.

5–10s: Opponent 2 swings a wild haymaker. She ducks and snaps the umbrella open right in his face. The canopy bursts open with a spray of rainwater and blinds him. From behind the canopy she lands a spinning heel kick on his shoulder, sending him sliding across a puddle. She snaps the umbrella shut in one motion. Camera orbits 180° around the canopy burst in one smooth arc, catching water droplets in the air.

10–15s: Opponents 3 and 4 flank from the market stalls. She burst-steps forward in one explosive sprint, a streak of water spraying from her sneakers, and reappears between them. A short jab with the steel tip into Opponent 3's raised forearm, then the brass hook catches Opponent 4's collar and she swings him sideways into a stack of bamboo crates, which tumbles lightly. Camera whip-pans left, then right, and settles behind her shoulder.

15–20s: Opponent 5 (rust coverall) charges like a bull. She runs at the plaster wall, takes two steps up it, flips backward over his head, and opens the umbrella mid-flip so she drifts down behind him in a soft, controlled landing. She closes it as her feet touch and drives the shaft into the back of his knees. He falls forward into a canvas stall, which sags and splashes rainwater from its tarp. Camera follows the wall-run in a tight vertical tilt, then drops to the landing.

20–25s: Opponent 6 (olive bomber) hurls a wooden stool at her. She snaps the umbrella open as a shield and the stool bounces off the canopy and clatters away. She spins the open umbrella once, flinging a wide arc of trapped rainwater into Opponents 6 and 3's faces, then snaps it shut and blocks Opponent 6's punch with the shaft held crosswise in both hands. She twists and flicks her away with a sharp one-handed rotation. Opponent 6 slides back across the wet stone. Camera pushes in tight on the shield-open, then pulls wide.

25–30s: Seo-yeon sprints and slides on her knees across a long puddle, umbrella held flat overhead, passing under a swinging blow from Opponent 1. She springs up at the stone well in the center of the courtyard, steps onto its rim, and plants the closed umbrella's tip beside her foot. Water streams off the canopy. Opponents 7 and 1 stand ahead of her, breathing hard, while the others recover behind. She is breathing steadily, eyes locked forward. HOLD this exact final frame for the last half second.

[ACTION & CAMERA]

Opponent 1 lunges. She plants the umbrella's steel tip on the well rim and uses it like a vaulting pole, swinging over his lunge and landing behind Opponents 1 and 4 as they collide. Camera follows the vault in a slight upward arc.

The camera slowly pulls back and rises to a wide shot of the whole courtyard with lanterns glowing in the drizzle. She climbs the first few steps and disappears into the lantern-lit lane under the open umbrella. Hold the empty wide shot for the last second.
