Prompt

Create a 15-second vertical 9:16 Hollywood-style live-action fantasy comedy in one continuous static shot. Use Image 1 as the visual reference for the woman, ghost, refrigerator, kitchen, lighting, camera angle, and props.

A woman in blue pajamas opens her refrigerator late at night. A tiny, cute, slightly translucent white ghost is sitting on the middle shelf, secretly eating caramel pudding.

As the door opens, the ghost is revealed naturally. The woman looks surprised, and the ghost immediately panics. It drops the spoon into the pudding cup, awkwardly hides the cup behind its body, and quickly looks left and right to check if anyone is watching.

Then the ghost looks at the woman and raises one finger to its mouth, asking her to keep the secret. She smiles, understands the situation, and shushes back.

They share a funny secret moment before she gently closes the fridge door completely.

Visual style: Realistic live-action, natural facial expressions, believable physics, detailed textures, warm nighttime kitchen lighting contrasted with cool refrigerator light, and seamless VFX integration.

Strict rules: One fixed camera, one continuous take, no cuts, no zoom, no camera movement, no morphing, no teleportation, no extra characters, no object clipping, and no cartoon-style rendering. Preserve character and prop consistency throughout.

Audio: Quiet kitchen ambience, refrigerator hum, subtle spoon movement, and a soft fridge door close. No dialogue or background music.

The goal is simple: make the ghost feel real, the comedy feel natural, and the entire scene flow smoothly from beginning to end.
