Prompt

Create a 30-second, 1080p video that looks like genuine live-action footage captured inside a real luxury sleeper train during a catastrophic event. The footage must resemble a scene filmed on a physical movie set with practical effects, real human performances, realistic environmental details, and restrained cinematic color grading. It must NOT look like animation, a video game, a CGI showcase, or glossy AI-generated footage.

SCENE:
Inside a modern luxury overnight train traveling through a real metropolitan city on a cloudy afternoon. A young adult East Asian woman with natural skin texture, slightly messy black hair, and a simple cream-colored sleeveless top is sitting on a large bed beneath a thick ivory duvet. She is holding a ginger cat against her chest. The cabin has realistic wood veneer, ordinary brushed-metal fixtures, warm practical reading lights, slightly wrinkled bedding, and a large panoramic train window. Everything should feel physically constructed and functional, not futuristic or excessively luxurious.

CAMERA:
One continuous interior shot from a natural observer's perspective near the foot of the bed. A subtle handheld camera movement, realistic autofocus behavior, minor exposure adjustments, and natural motion blur. The camera never passes through objects or moves impossibly. The window frame remains fixed, and the outside environment moves consistently with the train's direction. Avoid dramatic artificial camera sweeps.

ACTION AND TIMING:

0–7 SECONDS:
The woman casually strokes her cat while looking out of the window. The train gently vibrates as buildings, railway infrastructure, and occasional commuters pass outside. Her breathing, blinking, hand movements, and posture are natural and imperfect. Nothing feels staged.

7–13 SECONDS:
She notices people running along a nearby station platform. One commuter briefly looks toward the train with a frightened expression before disappearing behind a passing structural column. The woman stops stroking the cat and leans forward slightly, trying to understand what is happening. Her reaction is subtle and believable, not theatrical.

13–20 SECONDS:
Beyond the platform, a large crowd suddenly runs away from something farther down the railway corridor. In the distance, a huge, indistinct humanoid figure moves between buildings through rain and grey urban haze. Show it partially obscured by concrete pillars, passing structures, and smoke. Its size becomes apparent gradually through comparison with real buildings and people. Do not reveal a detailed fantasy monster or use exaggerated supernatural movement.

20–25 SECONDS:
The woman notices the figure approaching the tracks. A heavy impact or distant explosion makes the train windows vibrate slightly. She instinctively pulls the cat closer and shifts backward on the bed. Her eyes widen, her breathing quickens, and she glances between the window and the cabin door. Keep her physical reaction spontaneous and restrained.

25–30 SECONDS:
The train passes behind a row of concrete support columns, temporarily obscuring the view. When the city becomes visible again, the figure is gone, but smoke rises from a distant street and people continue running. The woman remains still, holding the cat tightly, listening to the rumbling train. End on her unsettled expression as the train continues forward.

REALISM REQUIREMENTS:
Photographic skin texture with visible pores and natural imperfections. Realistic human anatomy, eye movements, hand positioning, cat behavior, and fabric physics. Accurate window reflections, subtle glass vibration, naturally moving city backgrounds, believable perspective, consistent train speed, and realistic daylight exposure. Use muted grey skies, natural urban colors, restrained contrast, and subtle sensor grain. The creature must be integrated into the actual environment through atmospheric perspective, realistic scale, partial occlusion, and consistent lighting.

The scene should resemble live-action footage from a serious disaster thriller, with realism comparable to a professionally photographed feature film. Prioritize believable everyday details and natural performances over spectacle.

AVOID:
Plastic-looking skin, artificial facial beauty filters, oversharpening, excessive HDR, neon lighting, impossible camera movements, floating debris, distorted architecture, rubbery creatures, exaggerated facial expressions, overly smooth motion, video-game graphics, fantasy concept art, excessive slow motion, jump-cut montages, subtitles, logos, and watermarks.
