Prompt -

15s, 9:16 vertical, photorealistic premium American TV drama. Use Image 1 and Image 2 as sequential references for continuous action. Keep the same two adults, faces, hair, proportions, clothing and apartment.

An American couple argues intensely at night. He thinks his joke was harmless; she feels humiliated. Keep the argument fast, emotional and natural. Use only the dialogue below.

[0–3s] Start from Image 1. Medium two-shot. She sits on the sofa:
“Seriously? That was your idea of funny?”
He replies: “Oh, come on. It was a joke.”

[3–5s] She stands naturally and moves into the open living room:
“You embarrassed me!”
He immediately replies: “I didn’t mean—”

[5–7s] Continue seamlessly into the positions shown in Image 2. No cut or jump. Medium-wide two-shot. They face each other.
She: “You never do!”
He: “So now I can’t joke around?”

[7–10s] Naturally transition into medium shot-reverse-shot. Hold on him:
“Making this a huge deal?”
Cut to her matching medium shot:
“You think this is about the joke?”
She continues: “It’s about you not caring how I feel.”
Keep eyelines consistent.

[10–12.5s] Return to a medium two-shot. She says:
“You still don’t get it!”
As she speaks, she walks directly past the man toward the entrance behind him. She does not turn around first. Let her finish the line while walking.

[12.5–15s] She reaches the door behind him, opens it, storms out and slams it hard. He stays in place, turns his head and follows her with his eyes. After the door closes, hold briefly on his medium shot, his anger shifting to shock and disbelief.

Cinematic handheld camera with natural transitions. No extreme close-ups. Preserve the apartment layout, 180-degree rule and spatial continuity. The entrance door is directly behind the man.

Natural acting and realistic movement. No added dialogue, repeated turning, touching, pushing, chasing, sudden repositioning, teleportation, duplication, face or clothing changes, exaggerated acting, subtitles, text, music or extra people.
