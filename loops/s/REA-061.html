Prompt

SCENE CONTEXT A 15 second comic scene inside a vintage car actively traveling along a country road at 40 km/h. The car is already moving in the first frame and continues moving through the final frame. A terrified blonde passenger helps steer while the composed brunette driver applies lipstick. ACTIVE REFERENCE : starting-frame reference for both adult women, their exact faces, hairstyles, white clothing, seating positions, vintage car, and warm film look. Blonde passenger on screen left; brunette driver on screen right. Match their appearance 100% to the reference. FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING Begin with the reference composition already in motion. The blonde leans across with her hand gripping the upper-left steering wheel. The brunette holds red lipstick against her lower lip, with her other hand resting on the lower-right wheel. Both faces remain clearly visible. CAMERA One continuous shot, 63° field of view, matching the reference perspective. Camera mounted outside on the front of the moving car, facing inward through the windshield. Its position stays fixed relative to the car while the landscape moves continuously outside. Gentle chassis vibration passes through the camera. VISIBLE CAR MOVEMENT Through the side windows, nearby fence posts, grass, and trees sweep backward with clear parallax: nearby objects pass faster than distant fields. Through the rear window, the road visibly recedes. Moving tree reflections slide over the windshield. Bands of sunlight travel across the women’s faces and clothing. The rearview mirror trembles slightly; earrings and loose hair sway with the car’s vibration. Maintain these movement cues throughout every line of dialogue and the ending. ACTION AND DIALOGUE 0.0–3.0 seconds: The blonde tightens her grip, staring forward with wide eyes. BLONDE, sharply: “Watch the road!” The brunette finishes a short lipstick stroke and moves the lipstick away from her mouth. 3.0–6.0 seconds: Still inspecting herself in the rearview mirror, the brunette answers casually. BRUNETTE: “You are. I’m multitasking.” She presses her lips together once. 6.0–9.0 seconds: The wheels cross a small uneven patch. Both women bounce gently with the suspension. The blonde makes a small steering correction, shoulders tense. BLONDE: “We’re going to die!” 9.0–12.0 seconds: The brunette holds the lipstick beside her cheek and studies her reflection with a tiny head tilt. BRUNETTE, completely composed: “Does this shade wash me out?” 12.0–14.0 seconds: The blonde briefly gives her an incredulous sideways look. BLONDE: “The funeral lighting might!” 14.0–15.0 seconds: The brunette slowly lowers the lipstick, shifts her eyes to the road, and tightens her grip on the wheel. The blonde faces forward with her jaw clenched. Hold their contrasting reactions as scenery continues sweeping past. PERFORMANCE / PHYSICS Natural English conversation, one speaker at a time, with synchronized mouth movements. Blonde voice: breathless, tense, increasingly incredulous. Brunette voice: relaxed, dry, matter-of-fact. Allow brief reaction pauses between lines. Preserve realistic skin texture, eye movements, finger anatomy, and continuous hand contact with the wheel. Steering corrections stay small and physically plausible. LIGHTING / COLOR Warm directional afternoon sunlight, fixed 5600K white balance. Preserve the reference’s yellow-green vintage grade, golden skin highlights, creamy white lace, vivid red lipstick, and deep teal-green cabin shadows. Soft highlight bloom and fine photographic grain. AUDIO Clearly audible continuous engine rumble, tire-on-road hiss, wind through a slightly open window, and faint dashboard rattling. A soft suspension thump accompanies the bump at 6 seconds. Mix dialogue clearly above the environmental sounds. The soundtrack consists of conversation and location sounds. OUTPUT 15 seconds, 16:9 landscape, photorealistic, real-time motion, one uninterrupted take. CONTINUITY LOCKS Same two women, faces, wardrobe, car interior, and prop positions throughout. The brunette retains the lipstick in her original hand. Both women retain their respective steering-wheel grips. Keep the car visibly traveling forward for the entire 15 seconds, including the final reaction.
