Prompt

Create a photorealistic comedic pet-bartender video using Reference Image 1 as the ONLY visual reference.

Keep the same white fluffy dog, facial features, navy bartender apron, dark walnut counter, warm amber-lit bar background, and object positions throughout.

Scene Breakdown (9 Seconds):

Ready to Work (0–0.5s): A serious-looking fluffy dog bartender stands behind the counter, looking at an open cocktail shaker.

Seal the Shaker (0.5–2.1s): The dog uses its natural front paws to place the lid securely on the shaker.

Shake It (2.1–4.7s): The dog energetically shakes the metal cocktail shaker with both paws while maintaining a focused expression.

Pour the Cocktail (4.7–7.3s): The dog tilts the shaker and pours a realistic, continuous stream of transparent liquid into a stemmed cocktail glass.

The Final Touch (7.3–8.3s): The dog picks exactly one green olive from a dish containing three olives and drops it into the cocktail.

Funny Ending (8.3–9.1s): The dog looks directly into the camera and gives a subtle sideways head tilt with a serious bartender expression.

Visual Requirements:
Vertical 9:16 format.
Photorealistic cinematic quality.
Consistent character and environment.
Realistic paw movements and object interactions.
Natural liquid physics and gravity.
Static frontal camera with clean hard cuts.
Silent video with no music or sound effects.

Negative Prompt: No cartoon effects, human hands, extra paws, duplicated objects, changing backgrounds, floating liquid, disappearing olives, distorted anatomy, text, logos, or watermarks.
