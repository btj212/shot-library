Grok Imagine as a viewfinder: orbit a Super 16 insert, extend to finish the 180, then crop a still

CAM-126Camera moves / shotsGrok Imagineby @mackaybell2026-10-27

A working-director trick from a Grok Imagine feature: to find the right insert angle on the actor's sneakers, generate a slow circling move around the tight shot ("Sneakers do not move"), extend it ("Camera keeps moving quickly to complete the 180"), pick the best frame, then crop it with an image prompt and use that as the seed frame. Every prompt starts with the stock line "Super 16 250D grain".

Prompt

Super 16 250D grain. Camera slowly circles around tight shot of sneaker to see the other side of the alley. Sneakers do not move.

Here's the prompt for the extension which found the right angle.

Camera keeps moving quickly to complete the 180.

And here's the prompt to crop the shot in Grok Image:

Super 16 250D grain. Crop the shot to a slight tighter view of the faded white sneakers.
