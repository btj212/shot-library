Prompt (truncated at source)

Photorealistic Hollywood fantasy comedy, 16:9, 4K, shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with 35mm anamorphic lenses, 24fps cinematic film look, night scene lit only by a warm campfire and soft blue moonlight, clean low-noise image, creamy shallow depth of field with oval anamorphic bokeh, ultra-sharp detail, natural film grain, rich color grade, smooth stable camera, fast comedic hard cuts. Setting stays the same in every shot: a forest clearing at night with a ring of stones around a small campfire, a canvas tent, two wooden logs to sit on, a hanging lantern, a tall pine tree with one cone on a low branch, a starry sky, and no one else. Match Daisy and giant dragon references. The dragon is the same size in every shot, about as tall as a two-story house, much taller than the tent and the pine's lowest branches; show its full body, wings, legs and tail in wide shots. The dragon is huge but easily scared, sweet and clumsy. Daisy wears a woolly hat and a cloak over her usual dress. Natural English dialogue for Daisy only, accurate lip-sync. The dragon NEVER speaks; it only makes giggles, gulps, whimpers, squeaks and purrs. Only Daisy and the dragon appear: no animals, no insects, no extra characters, and nothing stuck on the dragon's face.

0-3s: Hook, slow dolly-in at eye level: Daisy kneels in front of a stack of unlit logs and points at them, while the dragon sits opposite her with huge sparkling eyes. "Rule number one: a tiny flame. Gently."

3-6s: Macro close-up on the dragon's nostrils: it exhales one perfectly tiny flame and the logs catch fire, warm light spreading across both faces. Cut to a medium shot: the dragon looks at Daisy with proud eyes. She gives a thumbs-up. "Perfect!"

6-9s: Medium shot: Daisy sits on a log and holds the lantern under her chin so her face glows eerily. She whispers, the dragon leaning in closer and closer, its eyes growing wider. "And then... the door creaked open... slowly..."

9-12s: Wide static shot: the pine cone falls from the low branch and lands on the dragon's head with a tiny "tock". The dragon leaps up in terror with its wings spread wide, flies backward a few steps, and hides behind Daisy, who sits perfectly still while its huge head peeks over her shoulder. Daisy, deadpan: "It was a pine cone."

12-15s: Low tracking shot at ground level: the dragon tries to hide in the tent, pushing only its head inside while its huge body stays outside; the tent lifts off the ground like a hat and swings from side to side as it shakes with fear. Daisy bursts into laughter.

15-18s: Medium shot: a twig snaps off-screen with a quiet crack. The dragon grabs Daisy in both claws and hugs her tightly against its chest like a teddy bear, eyes shut. Daisy, squished, with only her face showing: "I... can't breathe..."

18-21s: Medium shot: Daisy wriggles free, sets the lantern on a log and the dragon's huge shadow stretches across the tent wall. The dragon sees the shadow and freezes, trembling, eyes wide, covering its snout with both claws.

21-24s: Close shot: Daisy, calm, points at the shadow. "That's YOU." The dragon slowly lowers its claws, looks at the shadow, waves one claw, and the shadow waves back. It giggles and strikes silly poses with its wings, the shadow copying every move.

24-27s: Medium shot: Daisy holds a long stick with a round loaf of bread over the fire, and the dragon gently breathes one tiny flame to toast it perfectly. She tears the bread in two and gives the bigger half to the dragon, which holds it between two claw tips like a jewel. They eat happily.

27-30s: Wide shot at night, slow dolly-out: the dragon curled around the campfire like a big cat with Daisy sitting against its chest under the stars. Daisy, to the camera, deadpan: "Tomorrow... we tell a happy story." The last pine cone falls on the dragon's head with a tiny "tock". It freezes with wide eyes. Daisy, without turning: "Pine cone." Freeze on both of them. Hold this shot until the end.
