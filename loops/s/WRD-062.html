Prompt

Create a 30-second, 16:9 high-fashion Neo-Chinese fashion advertisement. The entire film features only one adult female model, and the model must appear in every shot.
Strictly separate the functions of the reference images:
@Image1 simultaneously and exclusively defines the model’s identity, face shape, facial features, skin tone, original freckle density, original makeup, lip color, refined center-parted black hair, low looped bun, and the large asymmetric hand-blown glass headpiece and nails on the same side. However, the nails should preferably remain understated and not overly visible.
Preserve the face and original makeup from @Image1 completely. Do not add green or pink eyeshadow, intensify the blush, or change the eyebrows, eye shape, or lip color. Do not copy the clothing or background from @Image1.
@Image2 exclusively defines the complete outfit and footwear: a straight black velvet Neo-Chinese qipao, high Mandarin collar, black frog closures, asymmetric dark-green jacquard overlay and draping, restrained water-lily embroidery, and black velvet square-toe flats. Lock the exact same garment structure, embroidery placement, draping, dress length, and shoes throughout the film.
@Image3 exclusively defines the minimalist contemporary Chinese courtyard.
@Image4 exclusively defines the small circular platform in the lake and its surrounding rocky landscape.
@Image5 exclusively defines the overhead water-lily pond, including the water surface, flower shapes, and evenly spaced arrangement.
@Image6 exclusively defines the realistic three-dimensional structure, thickness, curled edges, and moist material of the giant water-lily interior.
The environmental reference images must not alter the model’s identity, makeup, hairstyle, headpiece, nails, or clothing.
The large glass headpiece must retain exactly the same shape, scale, position, and materials throughout the film: translucent smoky-green tendrils, clear curved spikes, pale tea-amber droplets, dark-red glass spheres, trapped air bubbles, and molten folds. The headpiece must always remain clearly visible, but it must never cover the eyes, nose, or mouth.
Editing language: powerful yet restrained high-fashion advertising. Use direct hard cuts between scenes, alternating extreme wide shots, medium shots, facial close-ups, fabric macro shots, and embroidery macro shots. Use fast but controlled handheld tracking, short push-ins, 20–30-degree short-arc orbits, and low-angle upward camera movement.
Avoid prolonged exploratory camera movement, bullet time, freeze frames, and flash transitions. The model performs clear, independent, readable editorial cover poses. She does not dance or perform a continuous series of strange poses. Every shot must clearly display at least one of the following: the clothing, headpiece, nails, or footwear.
Editing language: forceful high-fashion advertising. Use direct hard cuts between scenes, alternating extreme wide shots, medium shots, facial close-ups, fabric macro shots, and embroidery macro shots. Use fast but controlled handheld tracking, whip pans, rapid push-ins, short parallax orbits, and low-angle rush-ins.
Do not use empty establishing shots. The model must already be visible in the first frame of every new scene. Do not use freeze frames, rigid pauses, dissolves, flash transitions, or slow continuous exploratory camera movement. The model performs clear, independent, readable editorial cover poses. She does not dance or perform a continuous series of strange poses. Every shot must show the clothing, headpiece, nails, or shoes.

Visual style
A Hasselblad medium-format 120-film fashion editorial. Slightly underexposed with clean, restrained contrast and fine, irregular film grain.
Creamy ivory highlights, warm beige stone walls, gray sage-green and dark-green midtones, and dense black velvet shadows. The skin, glass, water, embroidery, transparent nails, and fabric textures must appear physically realistic.
The overall rhythm is fast while keeping the clothing information clear and readable.
Music
Restrained modern minimalist electronic music at 110 BPM.
Soft low-frequency pulses, sparse wooden percussion, airy analog synthesizer drones, and very occasional short glass-like tones.
The rapid editing may follow the beat subtly, but do not add a strong accent to every hard cut.
No guqin, traditional Chinese opera melodies, grand orchestral music, or vocals.
Environmental sound
Extremely subtle water ripples, velvet and jacquard fabric movement, flat shoes contacting the ground, delicate resonance from the glass headpiece, and soft moist friction as the petals open.
The sounds should feel intimate, restrained, and realistic.
No exaggerated impacts, enormous whooshes, dramatic risers, or dialogue.
Avoid
Empty shots, additional people, duplicated models, outfit changes, missing or reduced headpiece, headpiece covering the face, inconsistent nails, bare feet, furniture, malformed hands, duplicated limbs, slow motion, dancing, magical particles, freeze frames, text, subtitles, or a generated logo.
