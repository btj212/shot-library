Prompt

Create a photorealistic Korean zombie horror sequence set inside a nearly empty Seoul subway station late at night. Cinematic 4K, 16:9 widescreen, 24 fps, realistic Korean thriller cinematography, cold fluorescent lighting, deep shadows, natural skin textures, handheld camera movement, and subtle film grain.

A young Korean woman with long black hair, wearing a fitted gray cardigan, a black mini skirt, and white sneakers, stands on the subway platform checking her phone. She smiles politely at an elderly passenger before suddenly freezing mid-motion. Her smile slowly widens unnaturally, her eyes turn completely milky white, and faint dark veins emerge beneath the skin around her temples and jaw. She begins making a quiet, broken giggling sound that echoes through the empty station.

A station employee approaches to check on her. Without warning, she twists her head toward him with a sharp, unnatural movement and sprints down the platform on all fours. The employee stumbles backward as an approaching train enters the station. She suddenly stops at the edge, staring motionlessly through the train windows as passengers inside begin noticing her.

The doors open. A frightened young commuter steps out and sees her standing perfectly still in the middle of the doorway. Her expression changes instantly, and she lunges forward, forcing the commuter and two other passengers to flee into the station's narrow service corridor.

The survivors barricade themselves inside a staff room. Through a small wired-glass window, they watch her walk slowly past the door, dragging one hand along the wall. The footsteps stop. Silence. A faint scratching sound begins above them. The ceiling panels tremble as she crawls across the narrow maintenance space overhead.

One survivor quietly unlocks the emergency exit, but the door alarm suddenly sounds. The woman drops into the corridor behind them, landing awkwardly before rising with a disturbing, jerky movement. Everyone runs toward the escalators as the station lights shut off one section at a time.

FINAL SHOT: An empty subway train departs into the darkness. Through its last illuminated window, the infected woman is visible sitting alone among the empty seats, staring directly into the camera with glowing pale eyes as the train disappears underground.

STYLE: Photorealistic Korean zombie thriller, realistic acting, eerie laughter, subtle facial transformation, believable body movement, practical-looking horror effects, cold fluorescent lighting, atmospheric shadows, escalating tension, natural station ambience, train brakes, footsteps, ventilation noise, and distorted PA announcements. Use continuous spatial geography, varied camera angles, close-ups, and fast cuts during the chase.

NEGATIVE: Cartoon, anime, obvious CGI, excessive gore, exaggerated supernatural powers, inconsistent faces or clothing, teleportation, impossible body movements, subtitles, text overlays, logos, or watermarks. No background music or narration.
