Prompt

Create a premium cinematic commercial storyboard poster for a Coca-Cola Original Taste campaign titled “BREAK THE ICE”.

FORMAT:
Vertical 9:16 editorial advertising storyboard sheet, high-end beverage commercial aesthetic, dark icy atmosphere, cinematic red and black color palette, ultra-realistic product photography, dramatic studio lighting, glossy commercial finish.

HEADER:
Top left: Coca-Cola Original Taste branding.
Top center: large icy metallic 3D title “BREAK THE ICE”, frozen texture, frost, glowing highlights.
Subtitle underneath: “COLD UNLOCKS A BRIGHTER FEELING”.
Top right:
“A 15-SECOND
COMMERCIAL STORYBOARD”
with smaller tagline:
“SAME GREAT TASTE
BOLDER MOMENTS”

LAYOUT:
Create 8 cinematic storyboard panels arranged in a clean 2-column × 4-row grid.
Each panel contains:
- red numbered scene label
- exact timecode
- bold cinematic scene title
- short descriptive copy
- large photorealistic cinematic product image
- bottom technical strip containing CAMERA, TRANSITION, and SFX
- small hand-drawn storyboard camera-direction diagram on the right

SCENE 01 — 00:00.0–00:01.6
Title: “FROZEN INSIDE”
Show a Coca-Cola Original Taste red can completely trapped inside a massive transparent block of ice, surrounded by broken ice chunks and frost, cold mist, dramatic blue-black background.
Camera: 50mm, slow push-in.
Transition: Fade in.
SFX: Low rumble, ice cracking.

SCENE 02 — 00:01.6–00:03.2
Title: “FIRST CRACK”
Extreme macro close-up of the frozen Coca-Cola can as the ice suddenly develops dramatic cracks spreading across the surface, condensation and tiny ice particles flying.
Camera: 100mm macro, tracking.
Transition: Hard cut.
SFX: Sharp ice crack and crystalline fracture.

SCENE 03 — 00:03.2–00:04.9
Title: “PRESSURE BUILD”
Extreme macro shot from above of the Coca-Cola can top, condensation, frost and ice surrounding the aluminum lid, subtle pressure building underneath, tiny bubbles and fizz.
Camera: 100mm macro, push-in.
Transition: Match cut.
SFX: Rising tension, faint fizz.

SCENE 04 — 00:04.9–00:06.8
Title: “BREAK THE ICE”
Hero action shot: the Coca-Cola can explosively breaks through the surrounding ice, hundreds of crystalline ice fragments flying outward, red can perfectly visible in the center, powerful cinematic energy.
Camera: 35–50mm, orbit + tracking.
Transition: Impact cut.
SFX: Explosive ice break, BOOM.

SCENE 05 — 00:06.8–00:08.5
Title: “ICE SPIN”
The Coca-Cola can rotates 360 degrees in mid-air, surrounded by floating ice cubes and sparkling particles, condensation covering the cold red aluminum, dramatic rim lighting.
Camera: 50mm, orbit.
Transition: Speed ramp.
SFX: Whoosh, moving ice particles.

SCENE 06 — 00:08.5–00:10.2
Title: “THE POP”
Extreme macro shot of the Coca-Cola can opening. The pull tab snaps upward, carbonation erupts dramatically, tiny droplets and mist suspended in the air, intense red cinematic lighting.
Camera: 100mm macro, push-in.
Transition: Match cut.
SFX: Metallic click, carbonation “PSSSHHH”.

SCENE 07 — 00:10.2–00:12.7
Title: “ICE-COLD PAYOFF”
Coca-Cola Original Taste being poured into a crystal-clear glass filled with ice. Dark sparkling cola cascades into the glass, bubbles, splash crown, condensation and droplets captured with ultra-high-speed commercial photography.
Camera: 85–100mm macro, tracking.
Transition: Smash cut.
SFX: Liquid rush, ice clink, fizz crescendo.

Title: “FINAL HERO”
