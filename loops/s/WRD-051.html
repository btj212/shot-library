Prompt

Create an ultra-photorealistic luxury fashion commercial in a pastel-beige studio with cinematic lighting and smooth transitions.

Look 1- A royal-blue belted shirt dress, brown boots, and beige handbag float in mid-air. A black-gloved hand swipes left, making the outfit disappear with a dramatic whoosh.

Look 2- A cream oversized sweater, terracotta wide-leg trousers, blue heels, and matching handbag fly in. Another swipe makes everything vanish.

Look 3- Transformation: A cobalt-blue fitted top, terracotta wrap miniskirt, pale-yellow cardigan, cream heels, and brown handbag enter from the right. A gloved finger selects the outfit, which magically transforms a brunette model wearing a beige base dress. Every garment fits naturally and seamlessly.

Fashion Showcase: Smooth 180° and 360° camera orbits, low-angle shots, close-ups, flowing fabric details, and full-body reveals. The model walks, turns, smiles, and winks naturally.

Final Shot: A stunning full-body three-quarter pose, held perfectly still for the last second.
