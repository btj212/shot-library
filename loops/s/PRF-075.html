Prompt

The Wrong Delivery – Seoul Street Revenge Comedy

Create an ultra-realistic live-action action-comedy scene set in a crowded outdoor shopping district in Seoul, South Korea. Cinematic handheld camera work, natural daylight, authentic Korean street atmosphere, realistic human movement, expressive reactions, and perfectly timed physical comedy. No animation or video-game aesthetics.

MAIN CHARACTER:
A stylish Korean man in his late 20s, athletic build, short textured black hair, wearing a dark oversized hoodie, beige cargo trousers, and white sneakers. Maintain his exact facial features, hairstyle, clothing, and body proportions throughout the entire sequence.

SECONDARY CHARACTER:
A confident Korean woman in her 20s, wearing a fashionable long coat, black trousers, and boots. She carries a large takeaway shopping bag.

SCENE AND ACTION:

The man stands outside a busy Seoul convenience store, happily eating a large takeaway burger while checking his phone. He places his food on a nearby public bench for a moment to answer a message.

A woman walks past, accidentally bumps into the bench, and his drink spills onto his trousers. He looks up in disbelief.

She barely acknowledges him and continues walking.

The man picks up his half-eaten burger, notices the woman has mistakenly taken his identical takeaway shopping bag, and immediately rushes after her.

He catches up and politely taps her shoulder. She turns around, assumes he is bothering her, and dismissively pushes his hand away.

He points toward the bag, trying to explain. She rolls her eyes, opens it, and discovers that it contains a ridiculously large birthday cake instead of her shopping.

The man starts laughing uncontrollably.

Embarrassed and annoyed, she suddenly grabs the cake box and accidentally drops it. The cake slides out and lands directly on the man’s hoodie.

He freezes, looking down at the mess.

A brief silence follows.

He slowly looks up at her with an exaggerated serious expression. Without saying a word, he takes a small handful of frosting from his hoodie and gently places it on the tip of her nose.

She stares at him in disbelief.

A nearby street performer notices the situation and starts laughing. The woman tries to maintain her dignity but accidentally slips on a fallen piece of cake, landing safely on a pile of empty cardboard delivery boxes.

The man bursts into laughter, picks up his burger, takes another bite, and walks away triumphantly.

FINAL SHOT:
The camera follows him walking confidently through the crowded shopping street, eating his burger with a satisfied expression. Behind him, the woman gets up, notices frosting on her nose, and shouts at him while pedestrians laugh.

SOUND DESIGN:
Authentic Seoul street ambience, passing traffic, footsteps, convenience-store door chime, food-wrapper sounds, drink splashing, surprised reactions, laughter, cake hitting clothing, cardboard boxes collapsing, and natural Korean street chatter. No overpowering background music.

VISUAL REQUIREMENTS:

* The man must remain the central character throughout.
* Maintain consistent faces, outfits, and physical proportions.
* Show every action clearly in chronological order.
* Realistic food physics and believable physical interactions.
* Natural handheld camera movement with cinematic close-ups.
* Expressive facial reactions and excellent comedic timing.
* Use continuous shots where possible to preserve action continuity.
* Photorealistic live-action quality, detailed textures, natural lighting, cinematic 4K.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
No animation, cartoon effects, video-game visuals, blood, serious injuries, supernatural movements, exaggerated physics, distorted faces, character morphing, disappearing props, sudden outfit changes, subtitles, logos, or watermarks.
