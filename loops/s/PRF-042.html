Prompt

Long-lens and locked-off, the sculler glides straight toward camera through the golden mist. She draws the oars through the water in one long stroke, the hull surging forward, then feathers the blades and slides forward on her seat as beads of water drip glittering from the oar tips. Mist curls slowly off the river surface and the trees behind remain hazy and still. Sound: the clunk of oarlocks, dripping water, birdsong across the river.
