Prompt

[Generation Goal]
Generate an intimate wildlife sequence in eight shots joined by hard cuts. The central subject is one tiger, and the primary event is the tiger walking through a sunlit jungle while the camera explores it in extreme close-ups.

[Reference Material Roles]
<<<image_1>>> defines the tiger and the look: its face, vivid orange fur, crisp black stripes, white cheeks and amber-gold eyes, the deep green jungle, the clear side light, and the sharpness of every hair and whisker.

[Subjects and Relationships]
There is exactly one tiger and it is the only animal in the film. It is calm, powerful and unhurried, a photoreal living animal with believable weight and muscle. The jungle is empty of people.

[Event Script]

[Stage 1] Opening state: the composition of <<<image_1>>>, the tiger's face filling the frame, looking into the lens. Primary event: it blinks slowly, its ears turn, and it begins to walk forward. Camera: a steady close-up moving backward at the tiger's walking pace. <a deep slow breath> <leaves rustling> <distant insects>

Hard cut.

[Stage 2] Primary event: extreme close-up of one amber eye; the pupil tightens as a shaft of sun crosses it, the fibres of the iris and the fine lashes sharp. Camera: a macro shot level with the eye, moving with the tiger. <soft footfalls> <a low rumble in its chest>

Hard cut.

[Stage 3] Primary event: a huge paw lowers onto wet earth and moss, the toes spreading, tiny grains of soil pressed outward, a claw tip just showing. Camera: a ground-level macro shot beside the paw, then the paw lifts and leaves the frame. <a soft heavy pad on damp earth> <a twig cracking>

Hard cut.

[Stage 4] Primary event: close-up along the tiger's shoulder and flank as it walks, the muscles sliding under the striped fur, each hair catching the sun, a few water droplets shaking loose. Camera: a side-on close-up travelling with the shoulder. <fur brushing against leaves> <steady breathing>

Hard cut.

[Stage 5] Primary event: close-up of the nose and whiskers; the pink nose twitches as it scents the air, the white whiskers tremble, and its lips part to show the edge of its tongue. Camera: a tight three-quarter close-up from just below the muzzle. <a sharp sniff> <a soft huff>

Hard cut.

[Stage 6] Primary event: the tiger pushes through large wet leaves, which bend around its face and spring back, droplets flying off in sharp sparkling beads. Camera: a close front shot from inside the foliage, leaves parting toward the lens. <wet leaves slapping and dripping>

Hard cut.

[Stage 7] Primary event: the tiger lowers its head to a clear stream and drinks, its tongue lapping, rings spreading across the water, its reflection trembling. Camera: a low shot at water level from the far bank, the eyes just above the surface. <rhythmic lapping> <trickling water>

Hard cut.

[Stage 8] Primary event: the tiger lifts its head, water dripping from its chin, and looks straight into the lens, then gives one long slow blink. Camera: a slow move closer to its face, ending on both eyes. End state: the tiger's eyes filling the frame, held. <water dripping> <a deep contented rumble> <jungle ambience>

[Maintain Consistency]
Keep the tiger's stripe pattern, eye colour and fur identical in every shot. Motion is real-time. No on-screen text.

The visuals feature extreme photorealism with ultra-high-definition detail: tack-sharp focus, crisp micro-contrast, every hair, whisker and water droplet finely resolved, clean noise-free image, vivid orange, amber and deep green, brilliant highlights and clean deep blacks.
