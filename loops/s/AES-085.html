Prompt

Create a 30-second cute, polished 3D animated short film with a charming family-friendly 3D animation aesthetic, soft rounded shapes, detailed textures, expressive character animation, pastel colors, and cinematic lighting.

CHARACTER:
A tiny lavender penguin with a white belly, orange beak and feet, round dark eyes, and a mint-green knitted scarf. Keep its design identical throughout.

SETTING:
A miniature ice cream shop inside a transparent snow globe. The shop has a pastel pink roof, wooden counter, ice cream freezer, and a window overlooking a tiny snowy village with three little cottages. The globe rests on a circular wooden base.

STORY:
The village lights have gone out in the cold snow. The penguin makes a magical glowing ice cream to bring the village back to life.

SCENE 1 (0–5s):
Inside the shop, the penguin looks through the window at the three dark cottages outside. Snow falls steadily. The penguin notices the unlit cottages, looks concerned, then turns toward the ice cream freezer with an idea.

SCENE 2 (5–10s):
The penguin opens the freezer and takes out a small tub of pale yellow ice cream. It scoops one round ball into a waffle cone, then adds a second smaller pink scoop on top. It carefully places the cone into a small golden serving holder on the counter.

SCENE 3 (10–16s):
The penguin sprinkles tiny golden sugar crystals over the ice cream. The crystals glow softly. The ice cream begins emitting a warm golden light. The penguin's eyes widen with delight. Keep the cone stable in the holder.

SCENE 4 (16–22s):
The penguin carries the cone outside the shop to a small stone pedestal in the snowy village square. It gently places the glowing cone upright on the pedestal. The golden light spreads across the snow-covered path toward the three cottages. One by one, the cottage windows light up warmly. Snow continues falling.

SCENE 5 (22–30s):
The penguin steps back and watches the village glow. The cottages are now warmly lit, and the snow-covered square has a cozy golden atmosphere. The penguin smiles proudly. The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the entire miniature winter village inside the transparent snow globe, with the glowing shop and cottages visible.

CONTINUITY:
One penguin, one shop, one snow globe, one ice cream cone, and exactly three cottages. The cone must remain the same waffle cone with pale yellow and pink scoops. The penguin must carry the cone from the counter to the pedestal. The cone stays upright on the pedestal. The golden light spreads from the cone to the cottages in sequence. No teleporting, duplicate objects, random transformations, or sudden scene changes.

AUDIO:
Soft original whimsical instrumental music, gentle snowfall ambience, freezer opening, ice cream scooping, delicate sparkling sounds, and warm magical chimes as the cottage lights turn on. No dialogue or lyrics.
