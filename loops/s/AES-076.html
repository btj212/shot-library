Prompt

Create a 30-second cute, polished 3D animated short film titled "The Cloud Delivery Service."

ART STYLE:
Charming stylized 3D animated movie aesthetic, soft rounded shapes, adorable character design, expressive facial animation, fluffy clouds, pastel colors, beautiful lighting, detailed miniature environments, and smooth cinematic animation. Whimsical, warm, and visually delightful. Not photorealistic or anime.

MAIN CHARACTER:
A tiny cloud delivery worker with a round cream-colored body, short arms and legs, rosy cheeks, large expressive eyes, a small blue delivery cap, and a bright yellow backpack. Maintain exactly the same appearance throughout.

SETTING:
A magical sky village above the clouds. Small colorful houses sit on separate floating islands connected by tiny bridges. The sky is bright blue with fluffy white clouds and warm sunlight.

SCENE 1 (0–5s):
Wide shot of the floating village. The tiny delivery worker stands beside a giant folded paper airplane at a small delivery station. They check a little parcel, place it carefully inside the yellow backpack, and climb aboard the airplane.

SCENE 2 (5–11s):
The paper airplane glides smoothly between floating houses. The worker steers with excitement, waving to a tiny character on a balcony. The camera follows alongside the airplane as clouds drift past.

SCENE 3 (11–17s):
The worker arrives at a small pink house on a floating island. They land gently, take out the parcel, and leave it beside the front door. A tiny fluffy resident opens the door and happily receives it. The worker waves goodbye and returns to the airplane.

SCENE 4 (17–23s):
As the airplane flies toward the next island, the backpack suddenly glows. The worker looks confused, opens it, and discovers a tiny glass container with a miniature rainbow swirling inside. Its colorful light reflects across their face.

SCENE 5 (23–30s):
The container opens slightly. A beautiful glowing rainbow grows out of it, stretching across the sky and connecting the floating islands. The village residents look up in amazement. The delivery worker smiles proudly as the paper airplane glides through the rainbow. End with a beautiful wide shot of the colorful sky village.

CONTINUITY:
One delivery worker, one paper airplane, one yellow backpack, and one rainbow container. Maintain consistent character design, clothing, and proportions. Keep the village layout coherent. The airplane must remain the same throughout. No duplicate characters, random objects, sudden transformations, or unexplained scene changes.

CAMERA:
Smooth cinematic tracking, wide establishing shots, expressive character close-ups, and gentle aerial movements. Keep the important actions clearly visible.

AUDIO:
Cheerful original orchestral-style instrumental with gentle bells and playful percussion. Soft wind, paper airplane flutter, and magical sparkling sound effects. No dialogue, lyrics, or recognizable existing music.
