Prompt

Create a photorealistic Hollywood-style Halloween fashion comedy in one continuous 10-second shot, with no cuts.

Scene 1 (0–2.5s): A man walks toward the camera on a modern city sidewalk. The camera tracks backward smoothly, maintaining full-body framing.

Scene 2 (2.5–5.5s): He stops, smirks, and snaps his fingers. Aged ivory linen bandages with frayed edges and antique-gold details spiral up from his boots, gradually wrapping his entire body. The city transforms into a Halloween wonderland with glowing pumpkins, warm orange lights, spiderwebs, autumn leaves, and ground mist.

Scene 3 (5.5–10s): The fully transformed mummy attempts a glamorous fashion runway walk but moves like a stiff, awkward zombie. It takes uneven shuffling steps, tries an elegant leg cross, wobbles, recovers dramatically, and strikes a ridiculous model pose with a completely serious expression. End with one final zombie step and a crooked runway pose, staring directly into the camera.

Visual style: Premium live-action realism, realistic anatomy, detailed linen textures, cinematic orange lighting, wet reflective pavement, and Hollywood-quality Halloween atmosphere.

Camera: One continuous shot, smooth backward tracking, full-body framing. The character always moves forward. No sliding feet or walking in place.

Audio: Playful Halloween fashion music with pizzicato strings and marimba, one crisp finger snap, and delicate magical chimes. No dialogue or mechanical sounds.

The key: Make the mummy look like a stiff zombie who genuinely believes it is a supermodel. Bold physical comedy, realistic movement, and perfectly timed poses.
