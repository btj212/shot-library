Prompt

SCENE
A tiny seaside café just after sunrise. Faded blue chairs, cream walls and an open window facing the water.

ACTION
A hand places a white espresso cup on a wooden table. Steam rises. A curtain moves gently in the breeze.

CAMERA
One continuous shot. Start close to the cup, then slowly pull back to reveal the sea through the window.

LOOK
1970s travel-film feel. Warm, slightly faded colours, fine film grain and soft morning light.

KEEP IT SIMPLE
One cup. One hand. No cuts, captions, logos or extra people.
