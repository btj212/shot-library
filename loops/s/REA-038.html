The three women in floral chadors move slowly along the row, bent low, their fingers plucking purple crocus blossoms and dropping them into their palms. The grandmother at the front straightens slightly, the heavy basket on her back shifting, and smiles. Frost glimmers on the field as the pink dawn glow brightens. The low camera tracks gently right along the line of pickers. Sound: soft rustle of petals, quiet footsteps on frosty ground, a distant rooster.
