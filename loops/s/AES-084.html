A polished feature-animation-style short: at a sunrise dock, a plump cartoon pelican scoops a tiny pufferfish, its throat pouch balloons into a translucent spiky ball, it coughs the fish out, and ends wet with a slack, wrinkled pouch while the pufferfish floats away. The 15,000-character prompt (written with Opus 5.5, per the creator) is a lesson in state locking: every character has named states (pouch normal / stretched into a ball / stretched out and saggy; pufferfish normal / puffed) with the exact shots where each applies and a rule that the pouch never returns to normal. It also specifies water physics ("No jelly-like slabs of water… no slow-motion splashes"), quiet background life, no music or speech, and bans live-action, cel-shaded or game looks. Characters and setting:
Pelican — a stylized cartoon big white pelican, eighty centimeters tall when standing, round and plump, white feathers all over with black wingtips; a thick, long neck; a small tuft of messy feathers sticking back on top of its head; round yellow eyes with black pupils, a ring of pink skin around each eye; a very long beak, the upper beak flat and grey-pink with a small hook at the tip, and an orange-yellow throat pouch hanging under the lower beak — the pouch is a thin, stretchy layer of skin; short orange legs and very large webbed feet. It is the same pelican throughout the film, with its pouch in three states:
  Pouch normal — the pouch is tucked up against the underside of the lower beak. This is the state in Shots 1 to 3.
  Pouch stretched into a ball — the pouch is stretched by the pufferfish inside into a perfectly round ball bigger than the pelican's head, hanging in front of its chest, the skin thin, tight, translucent and shiny, with a dozen or so small points poking out from inside across the surface; the beak is forced open and cannot close; what is inside cannot be seen, only the points poking out. This is the state from Shot 4 until it coughs out the pufferfish in Shot 8. The ball is the pouch skin itself stretched out, attached to the lower beak — it is not an object held in the mouth.
  Pouch stretched out and saggy — the pouch has been stretched loose into a big slack, wrinkled sheet of orange-yellow skin, deflated and full of folds, hanging from the lower beak down past the chest; all its feathers are wet, clumped in strands and beaded with water. This is the state from after it coughs out the pufferfish in Shot 8 through Shot 12; the pouch never goes back to normal.
Pufferfish — a stylized cartoon little pufferfish, the same one throughout the film, in two states:
  Normal — only the size of a ping-pong ball, short and round; pale sandy-yellow back with small dark-brown round spots, creamy white belly; smooth skin with no raised spines; two very large round eyes, a little dopey; a tiny pouting mouth showing two little buck teeth; small translucent fins.
