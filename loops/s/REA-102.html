TITLE: THE CROISSANT INCIDENT

MODEL: Seedance 2.5
DURATION: 15 seconds
FORMAT: 9:16 vertical
STYLE: Ultra-realistic amateur iPhone footage
CAMERA: ONE continuous handheld shot — no cuts

[STYLE + CAMERA + ATMOSPHERE]

Authentic amateur smartphone footage recorded by a friend on an ordinary Parisian street.

The video must feel spontaneous, unplanned and captured in real life, not directed or professionally produced.

Natural handheld micro-shakes, imperfect framing, subtle autofocus adjustments, automatic exposure changes, realistic smartphone motion blur and compression.

Natural overcast daylight. Ordinary pedestrians, café terraces and everyday street activity.

The person filming is reacting to the situation, not anticipating it.

[IMAGE REFERENCE]

"YOUR REF IMAGE" — Main character identity reference.

Faithfully preserve the man's facial features, hairstyle, facial proportions and recognizable identity throughout the entire video.

Natural casual clothing, realistic skin texture, subtle facial expressions and relaxed body language.

[TIMELINE]

00:00–00:04

The man stands outside a small bakery, holding a takeaway coffee and a fresh croissant. His friend films him casually as he prepares to take a bite.

00:04–00:07

An ordinary pigeon suddenly flies into the frame and steals the croissant directly from his hand.

The camera operator reacts slightly late, instinctively moving the phone to follow the pigeon.

00:07–00:11

The man freezes in disbelief, staring at his empty hand before looking directly at his friend.

He says naturally: "Seriously?"

00:11–00:15

The camera operator starts laughing, causing subtle camera shaking. The man looks toward the departing pigeon, shakes his head and takes a resigned sip of coffee.

The recording ends abruptly, like an ordinary smartphone video.

[PHYSICAL REALISM]

Realistic gravity, bird anatomy, wing movement, physical contact, object interaction, environmental shadows and consistent spatial relationships.

Natural human reaction times. No exaggerated acting. The character never anticipates the unexpected event.

[SOUND DESIGN]

Authentic smartphone microphone recording, distant traffic, footsteps, street conversations, pigeon wing flapping and spontaneous laughter.

No background music. No narration. No artificial sound effects.

[STYLE & QUALITY BOOSTERS]

Photorealistic everyday life, authentic smartphone recording, natural imperfections, consistent character identity, realistic human behavior, believable environmental interaction.

Absolutely no cinematic camera movements, professional lighting, artificial bokeh, beauty filters, dramatic color grading, slow motion, CGI appearance, exaggerated acting, subtitles or transitions.
