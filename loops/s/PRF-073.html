Prompt

Girl = a calm capable young woman around 26, dark hair pulled back, sharp steady eyes, a faint brow scar, a dark fitted jacket, a single white earbud. Controlled and secretly dangerous. Face per reference. Appearance only.
Attackers = attackers disguised as airplane cabin crew in flight-attendant uniforms with cold menacing faces, advancing down the aisle with concealed weapons drawn. Appearance only.
Passengers = ordinary airplane passengers of various ages in casual travel clothes, calm at first then frightened. Appearance only.
Planeext = a large passenger airplane flying through a dark night sky among moonlit clouds, blinking navigation lights, deep blue-black night, moonlit fuselage, calm high altitude. Environment only.
Planeint = the interior cabin of a passenger airplane at night, rows of seats down a narrow aisle, dim overhead lighting and reading lights, closed window shades, overhead bins, a galley at the front, a calm night-flight atmosphere that turns tense. Environment only.

SCENE: a tense cinematic thriller aboard a night flight. A passenger plane cruises calmly through a dark cloudy night sky; inside, passengers sit quietly, among them a calm young woman with an earbud in. Suddenly the plane shakes and the cabin lights flicker and dim, and attackers disguised as cabin crew emerge and advance down the aisle with weapons. The woman rises and fights them. Moody, high-tension, intense.

TECHNICAL: 16:9, cinematic thriller grade, cool moody blue-black night palette, dim flickering cabin light, dynamic handheld and confined-space camera angles, shallow depth of field, motion blur, fine film grain, photorealistic, high-tension quality.

CUTS:
CUT 1 (0-5s): EXTERIOR — a wide cinematic shot of the passenger plane cruising calmly through the dark moonlit clouds at night, navigation lights blinking. Peaceful, vast, still. Slow drift.
CUT 2 (5-9s): INTERIOR — the camera moves through the dim calm cabin, passengers seated quietly, settling on the young woman in her seat with an earbud in, eyes half-closed, relaxed. Ordinary night flight.
CUT 3 (9-14s): Suddenly the plane SHAKES — a jolt. The cabin lights flicker, dimming and brightening unevenly. Passengers stir, alarmed, gripping armrests. The woman's eyes open, instantly alert. Tension spikes. Handheld unsteady shake.
CUT 4 (14-19s): From the galley at the front, attackers disguised as cabin crew emerge, cold-faced, drawing concealed weapons, and advance down the aisle. Low-angle shot making them menacing. Passengers recoil in fear. The woman stands, calm and ready.
CUT 5 (19-26s): The fight erupts in the cramped aisle — the woman takes on the attackers with fast skilled close-quarters combat, using the seats and tight space, striking and dropping them one by one. The lights flicker through the chaos. Dynamic confined-space angles, intense.
CUT 6 (26-30s): The last attacker drops. The woman stands alone in the dim shaking aisle, breathing hard, as frightened passengers stare. The cabin lights steady. Cool, tense final frame. END.

RULES: References are appearance only, do not recreate. Keep the woman consistent across every cut. Action is intense but not gory — no blood or graphic wounds, impacts sold through motion and body mechanics in the confined cabin. Cool moody night grade with flickering dim cabin light throughout. Follow the sequence: plane exterior calm → interior and the girl → the shaking and dimming lights → attackers emerge → she fights and wins. Sound design: the low hum of the plane, calm cabin ambience, the sudden jolt and rattle, electrical buzz of flickering lights, panicked passenger gasps, brutal impact hits and grunts in the fight, a tense driving score. Final frame cool and steady, clean.
