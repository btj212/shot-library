Prompt

Create a 30-second cute, polished 3D animated short film titled "The Mushroom House Makeover."

ART STYLE:
Charming high-quality 3D animated movie aesthetic. Soft rounded shapes, adorable character design, expressive facial animation, rich pastel colors, detailed miniature environments, warm sunlight, and smooth cinematic animation. Cozy, whimsical, and visually delightful. Not photorealistic or anime.

MAIN CHARACTER:
One tiny woodland builder with a round cream-colored body, short arms and legs, rosy cheeks, large expressive eyes, a small green leaf-shaped hat, and a brown tool belt. Maintain exactly the same design and proportions throughout.

SETTING:
A peaceful woodland clearing with soft green grass, colorful wildflowers, tiny mushrooms, and tall trees. In the center stands one oversized red mushroom with a wide spotted cap and a plain cream-colored stem. Keep the mushroom in exactly the same position and shape throughout.

SCENE 1 (0–5s):
Wide shot of the clearing. The tiny builder walks toward the large ordinary mushroom carrying a little wooden toolbox. They look up at it, rub their chin thoughtfully, then smile and open the toolbox.

SCENE 2 (5–11s):
The builder places a small round wooden door at the front of the mushroom stem, carefully fits a tiny circular window above it, and adds two miniature wooden steps leading to the entrance. Show each item being carried into place and attached by hand. The mushroom itself does not change shape.

SCENE 3 (11–17s):
The builder climbs a small ladder and carefully attaches two glowing windows to the mushroom cap. They add a tiny chimney on the left side of the cap and a little wooden balcony beneath it. The builder climbs down and proudly examines the progress.

SCENE 4 (17–23s):
The builder decorates the entrance with colorful flowers, places two small lanterns beside the steps, and lays a winding stone pathway through the grass toward the door. Each decoration is placed deliberately. The house now looks warm and inviting.

SCENE 5 (23–30s):
As evening light begins to glow, the builder switches on the lanterns. Warm golden light shines through the windows. The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the finished mushroom cottage surrounded by glowing fireflies and colorful flowers. The builder sits happily on the front step, admiring the completed home. End on a beautiful wide shot.

CONTINUITY:
Exactly one builder and one mushroom house. Preserve the mushroom's original size, red spotted cap, and position. All additions must be physically carried and installed by the builder. Once placed, doors, windows, chimney, balcony, steps, flowers, and lanterns remain in their exact positions. No disappearing objects, duplicate characters, sudden transformations, or random decorations.

CAMERA:
Smooth cinematic camera movements, wide establishing shots, medium construction shots, and expressive close-ups. Clearly show the builder performing each task. Keep the mushroom house visible during the makeover.

AUDIO:
Soft woodland ambience, birds chirping, gentle footsteps, tiny hammer taps, wood placement sounds, and magical sparkling effects when the lights turn on. No dialogue, lyrics, or recognizable existing music.
