Prompt

COLOR RIOT — 30-SECOND PREMIUM EXPERIMENTAL SKATEBOARD COMMERCIAL

Create a 30-second premium experimental sports-fashion commercial featuring a beautiful adult Asian woman, approximately 23 years old, performing aggressive, highly acrobatic freestyle skateboarding in a colorful early-2000s-inspired urban environment. Combine ultra-realistic live-action, comic-book animation, handcrafted stop-motion, pencil sketch, colorful acrylic painting, pop-art graphics, multiple exposure, paper collage and experimental VFX. Every style transition is triggered by her tricks, rotations, impacts or movement. Maintain character, skateboard, stunt trajectory and spatial continuity through all transformations.

The film must feel like a premium sports-fashion campaign: rebellious, playful, energetic, nostalgic, colorful, chaotic but sophisticated, artistic and visually unforgettable. All characters, props, locations, artwork and designs must be original and free of logos, brands, trademarks or copyrighted IP.

## MAIN CHARACTER

A beautiful adult Asian woman, approximately 23 years old, athletic and lean, strong legs, toned arms, natural proportions, expressive eyes, realistic facial features, natural skin texture, visible pores and subtle imperfections. Dark hair in a loose messy ponytail, strands moving naturally during tricks. Confident, playful, rebellious attitude.

**Wardrobe:** oversized cyan-and-orange windbreaker, plain white fitted tank top, loose baggy cargo pants with lime-green and muted-purple color blocking, generic thick-soled skate shoes with colorful laces, fabric wristbands and optional generic headphones around her neck. Authentic early-2000s skate fashion, completely unbranded.

Maintain identical face, hair, body, outfit, shoes, accessories and proportions throughout all styles.

## SKATEBOARD

One original professional freestyle skateboard with colorful abstract deck artwork using cyan, magenta, orange, yellow and cobalt blue. No letters, logos, brands or recognizable artwork. Slightly worn deck edges, visible wood layers, black grip tape, metallic trucks, realistic wheels and bearings.

Maintain the exact skateboard design and artwork through every style. Realistic board flex, wheel rotation, truck movement, ground contact, momentum and mechanics.

## ENVIRONMENT

A fictional early-2000s urban environment: colorful concrete skatepark, parking structure, rooftop, painted alley, wide stairs, metal rails, ramps, quarter pipes, vintage-looking vending machines, abstract original wall art and distant skyline.

Bright afternoon/sunset sunlight, long shadows, vibrant urban colors. Palette: electric cyan, hot pink, orange, yellow, lime green, cobalt blue, purple and turquoise. Nostalgic early-2000s skate culture without recreating recognizable real locations, advertisements, graffiti, brands or copyrighted imagery. No modern smartphones, electric vehicles or contemporary signage.

## VISUAL STYLES

**LIVE-ACTION:** Ultra-realistic cinema photography, authentic human anatomy, natural skin, fabric, hair, skateboard mechanics, realistic shadows, film grain and cinematic lighting.

**COMIC / POP-ART:** Thick ink outlines, halftone dots, vibrant color blocks, speed lines, dynamic panels, graphic impact shapes, cyan/magenta/yellow/orange, frame-breaking effects. No readable text or speech bubbles.

**STOP-MOTION:** Handcrafted miniature skatepark, tactile miniature buildings, painted surfaces, tiny rails, physical textures, imperfect frame-by-frame movement and practical miniature lighting. Sophisticated artistic stop-motion, never childish.

**PENCIL / CHARCOAL:** Graphite and charcoal drawings on textured paper, visible strokes, rough outlines, anatomical action studies, multiple poses, wheel circles, trajectory lines and sketch marks. Progressively transform into colored pencil and live-action.

**PAINTING:** Vibrant acrylic painting, visible brush strokes, canvas grain, thick paint, flowing cyan, magenta, orange, yellow and purple. Environment and rider become painted forms while maintaining recognizable anatomy and skateboard geometry.

**MULTIPLE EXPOSURE:** Sequential rider silhouettes showing different phases of a trick, photographic layers, motion trails, geometric shapes and abstract graphic effects.

**PAPER COLLAGE:** Layered cutout paper, physical edges, colorful fragments, textured backgrounds, kinetic paper animation and geometric shapes.

Transitions must be motivated by movement, takeoff, rotation, board contact or impact. Use match cuts, frame breaks, morphing environments and graphic explosions. Never apply random filters.

# EXACT 30-SECOND CHOREOGRAPHY

### 0:00–0:03 — SKETCH TO REALITY

Begin on textured white paper. A graphite sketch reveals a female skateboarder, skateboard and urban skatepark. Pencil strokes animate as she pushes off. Wheels begin rotating. Graphite transforms into colored pencil: cyan, hot pink, yellow, orange and lime green spread across the paper. The drawing seamlessly becomes photorealistic live-action.

Low ground-level tracking shot beside the skateboard. Real wheels hit concrete. She pushes powerfully and accelerates toward a ramp.

### 0:03–0:07 — COMIC BREAK

She approaches a ramp at high speed, performs a powerful ollie, kickflip and high aerial rotation over a colorful obstacle. At the apex, freeze-frame and transform into a vibrant comic panel: thick ink outlines, halftone, speed lines, cyan, pink, yellow and orange.

Three sequential panels briefly show takeoff, board rotation and board catch. She breaks through the comic borders. Panels shatter into colorful graphic fragments and ink splashes.

Return to live-action just before landing. She catches the board, lands hard but controlled, knees compress naturally, wheels hit concrete, and she continues riding.

Camera: low-angle tracking, brief side shot clearly showing the trick.

### 0:07–0:11 — MINIATURE MADNESS

She accelerates toward a metal rail and launches into an aggressive rail trick. The environment transforms into a handcrafted miniature skatepark with tiny buildings, sculpted concrete, miniature rails and physical textures.

Rider and skateboard move in deliberate frame-by-frame stop-motion. She performs a high ollie onto the rail, board slide, fast rotation out and clean landing. Miniature dust, paper confetti and tiny fragments burst outward.

As the skateboard rolls forward, the miniature environment grows larger and seamlessly transforms back into realistic live-action. She continues without stopping.

### 0:11–0:15 — PAINT IN MOTION

Side-tracking shot follows her racing through a colorful alley. She approaches a curved ramp and performs an aggressive aerial trick with a full-body rotation and board flip.

At takeoff, the environment transforms into vibrant acrylic painting. Buildings become thick brush strokes, ground becomes canvas, and colors smear across the image. Cyan paint trails behind the wheels, while hot-pink, orange and yellow brush strokes follow her rotation.

Rider and skateboard briefly become painted forms but maintain correct anatomy and board geometry. At landing, paint splashes outward. Brush strokes sharpen into realistic concrete, fabric, hair and skateboard. Return to live-action.

### 0:15–0:20 — MULTIVERSE FLIP

She races toward a rooftop gap between parking-structure levels. Low rear three-quarter tracking shot. She launches into a massive aerial combination: powerful ollie, board flip, full-body rotation, second board rotation and precise board catch.

Multiple exposure silhouettes appear simultaneously, showing sequential positions of the same trick. Some silhouettes are photorealistic, others comic ink, pencil sketches, colored-paper cutouts and painted shapes.

Wheel rotation becomes abstract graphic circles. Rooftop transforms into layered paper collage, skyline into cutout shapes. As she catches the skateboard, all layers converge into a single rider.

She lands on the rooftop with realistic impact and wheel vibration. The collage collapses and becomes real again.

### 0:20–0:26 — STYLE COLLISION / FINAL STUNT

Wide shot reveals a huge concrete staircase, metal rail and colorful quarter-pipe. She races toward it at maximum speed, crouches low, pushes aggressively and launches into an enormous aerial trick over the staircase.

Visual styles change in synchronization with the stunt:

**TAKEOFF:** Ultra-realistic live-action. Wheels leave concrete; impact creates colorful geometric shapes.

**FIRST ROTATION:** Comic-book style, thick outlines, halftone, cyan/magenta panels. She breaks through a panel border.

**SECOND ROTATION:** Pencil and charcoal sketch. Multiple action-study poses, trajectory lines, wheel circles and hand-drawn motion strokes.

**BOARD FLIP:** Acrylic painting. Skateboard rotates through thick paint strokes and vibrant brush textures.

**BOARD CATCH:** Handcrafted stop-motion miniature. Rider and skateboard briefly become tactile miniature objects.

**FINAL DESCENT:** Multiple exposure. Sequential silhouettes merge as she aligns the skateboard.

**LANDING:** Sudden return to photorealistic live-action. She lands hard but successfully, knees compress deeply, board flexes naturally, wheels maintain ground contact. Dust and colorful fragments erupt around her.

She rides out without falling. Styles explode outward into ink, paint, pencil strokes, paper cutouts and geometric shapes, then disappear, leaving pure reality.

The trick must be spectacular, extremely acrobatic and physically readable. Clearly show takeoff, rotation, board catch and landing.

### 0:26–0:30 — COLOR RIOT HERO ENDING

Pure photorealistic live-action. She rides through the colorful skatepark at sunset. Low-angle tracking beside the skateboard. Warm sunlight, long shadows, vibrant painted ramps and geometric walls.

She performs a final stylish pop trick, catches the skateboard and rolls toward camera. Controlled final powerslide. Wheels skid across concrete; dust and small colorful fragments move naturally.

She stops with one foot on the skateboard and the other touching ground. Looks directly at camera with a confident, playful, rebellious expression and subtle smile.

Her original colorful skateboard artwork is visible. Slow camera push-in. Premium hero composition with clean negative space for title. Hold briefly, cut to black.

Optional title after black: **COLOR RIOT**. Original minimal bold typography only, no branding.
