Prompt

Format & duration: archived 2003 network reality-TV broadcast footage, shot handheld on a consumer MiniDV camcorder. 4:3, standard definition, interlaced. 30 seconds, fast-cut multi-location montage, cut on the music beat.

Image references: Image1 — the only recurring character. No location references; every location is built from this prompt.

Scene geography: six public locations in a sunny American city, one per segment. A mall with a skylit escalator. A supermarket aisle under fluorescent tubes. A stone plaza fountain in harsh midday sun. A city bus stop on a bright street. A grassy park with a jogging path. An electronics store with a wall of chunky CRT TVs. Each location is shown wide enough to read in its first second.

Immutable character facts: Image1 is a heavyset adult man, 173 cm tall, with exactly the build and proportions on his reference sheet. Short curly dark hair, flushed cheeks, grey crew-neck tee, navy bike shorts, dark grey sneakers. Same face, hair, body and outfit in every location. He is the only person who appears in more than one segment.

Dramatic goal: one fearless guy brightens an ordinary day by turning every public place into his stage. Strangers go from staring to laughing along with him. The comedy comes from his total commitment, never from his body.

Continuous timeline:
0:00–0:04.3. Mall. Image1 rides the down escalator backwards, arms flung wide like an opera singer, holding one long note. Shoppers on the up escalator twist to stare; one woman covers her mouth laughing.
0:04.3–0:08.1. Supermarket. He shoves a shopping cart into a sprint, hops onto the rear bar gripping the handle, and glides down the aisle arms out. A stock clerk in a green apron flattens against the cereal shelf and cracks up.
0:08.1–0:11.6. Fountain. He sits on the stone edge, both feet in the water, shoes beside him, and kicks a SPLASH toward a college couple who shriek and run off laughing.
0:11.6–0:16.8. Bus stop. Down on one knee with a silver boombox on his shoulder, he serenades a grey-haired woman holding a newspaper. She stares flat for two beats, then cracks and SWATS his shoulder with the rolled paper.
0:16.8–0:21.5. Park. He races a jogger in a headband across the grass, dead serious, then DIVES into a belly slide over a chalk finish line while a group of college-age friends cheers.
0:21.5–0:26.4. Electronics store. He robot-dances in front of the CRT wall. An employee in a red polo glances around, then joins in, and they lock eyes doing the same arm-pop.
0:26.4–0:30. Back outside. The camera operator's hand reaches into frame; Image1 SMACKS a high five, then leans into the lens, out of breath and grinning.

Dialogue, spoken exactly as written:
Supermarket clerk: "Whoa whoa whoa!"
Image1, at the bus stop: "Ma'am, this one's for you."
Image1, at the end: "Same time tomorrow?"

Micro-expressions & performance: Image1 plays everything completely straight — eyes squeezed shut on the escalator note, cheeks puffed on the cart, tongue between his teeth on the splash, hand on heart and pleading eyebrows at the bus stop, jaw set and fists pumping in the race. The joke lands because he never breaks until the last shot, where he finally grins wide and sweaty. Every bystander is a different adult with a different reaction: stunned stare, shriek, deadpan that breaks, reluctant joining-in.

Camera workflow: one handheld operator running alongside him, part of the fun, not a crew. Operator stumbles backward down the escalator filming upward. Whip pan with a late reframe in the supermarket. Snap zoom across the plaza with autofocus hunting through the spray. Crouched low and tilting at the bus stop. Bouncing run-along in the park. Snap zoom in the store. Final shot pushes past the lens's limit into blurry digital zoom on his face, with a one-frame stutter on his grin.

Look & artifacts: low dynamic range, so skies, skylights and water clip to flat white and shadows crush to black. CCD vertical smear — a white streak straight up and down the frame — from the skylight, sun glints and the fountain. Heavy saturated colour with slight bleed on reds and blues. Interlace combing on whip pans. Exposure pumps when the camera swings from shade into sun. Fluorescent white balance lags blue in the store, then corrects. Scan-line flicker on the CRT wall.

Sound: original 2003-style pop-punk instrumental, fast palm-muted guitar and snare, dropping under every spoken line. Built-in camcorder mic throughout — clipped shouts, wind buffeting outdoors, handling thumps, his escalator note cracking, cart wheels rattling, the splash, boombox hiss, the landing thump, CRT whine, the operator laughing.

Hard prohibitions: no text, subtitles, timestamps, REC icon or logos. No smartphones, LED screens or anything made after 2006. No minors. No jokes about his weight or body. No property damage and no one getting hurt. No change to Image1's face, hair, build or outfit. No gimbal smoothness, no stabilisation, no film look, no shallow depth of field, no modern colour grade.
