Prompt

Seattle — Overlook Walk Tourist Photo Scene
Dreamina Seedance 2.5 | 12 Seconds | 3:4 Vertical
Create a 12-second hyper-realistic cinematic replacement scene for the existing Seattle video. Three distinct shots with hard cuts, natural American English dialogue, realistic physical interactions, and precise object continuity.
CHARACTER AND LOCATION REFERENCES

Use the original female streetwear reference image and existing Seattle video to preserve Woman 1's exact facial identity, black knit beanie, long dark wavy hair, beige puffer vest, fitted top, oversized blue jeans, belt, and accessories.

Use "OVERLOOK WALK.png" as the architectural and environmental reference.

The setting is Seattle's Overlook Walk, featuring landscaped terraces, wooden seating, pedestrian walkways, metal railings, and Elliott Bay in the background. Natural late-afternoon autumn lighting.

Woman 1 carries a small bouquet of yellow flowers in her LEFT hand throughout the entire scene. Her RIGHT hand is free to receive, operate, and return the tourist's smartphone.

Man 1, her male friend from the original Seattle video, remains nearby with consistent appearance.

The tourist couple consists of two American adults with consistent facial identities and clothing across all shots.

SHOT 1 — THE OFFER | 0:00–0:04

Medium-wide three-quarter camera angle showing Woman 1 approaching the tourist couple, who are struggling to frame a selfie with the waterfront behind them.

Woman 1 notices and gestures toward their phone with her empty right hand.
Woman 1: "You want the skyline in it too?"

Tourist: "Please."

The tourist extends the smartphone toward Woman 1.

Woman 1 takes the phone with her RIGHT hand. The handoff is clearly visible and physically completed before the cut.

Her yellow flowers remain securely in her LEFT hand.

Hard cut.

SHOT 2 — THE PHOTOGRAPH | 0:04–0:08

Over-the-shoulder camera angle from slightly behind Woman 1, looking past her toward the tourist couple and Seattle waterfront.

Woman 1 holds the smartphone upright in her RIGHT hand, camera facing the couple.

The couple stands together with Elliott Bay behind them, smiling naturally.
Woman 1 adjusts the framing slightly.

Woman 1: "Okay, got it."

She visibly taps the phone's camera shutter button with her right thumb.

The photograph is actually taken. The couple relaxes their pose.

The yellow flowers remain in her LEFT hand, clearly separate from the phone.

Hard cut.

SHOT 3 — THE PAYOFF | 0:08–0:12

Medium conversational shot showing Woman 1 handing the smartphone back to its original owner using her RIGHT hand.

The tourist receives the phone and looks at the photograph on its screen.

Tourist: "That's actually perfect."

Woman 1: "I charge by the compliment."
The couple laughs. Woman 1 smiles, turns, and begins walking away with Man 1.

The bouquet remains in her LEFT hand.

End with a natural walking movement suitable for cutting into the next Seattle waterfront scene.

CINEMATOGRAPHY

Hyper-realistic live-action photography with authentic skin pores, natural facial expressions, believable fabric movement, and accurate human anatomy.

Natural handheld cinematography with three distinct camera perspectives:

Shot 1: Three-quarter medium-wide interaction

Shot 2: Over-the-shoulder photograph

Shot 3: Medium conversational payoff

Use hard cuts only. No morphing, dissolves, or continuous camera orbit between shots.

Natural waterfront ambience, wind, footsteps, distant pedestrians, and casual conversation. No background soundtrack.

SUBTITLE DESIGN
All spoken dialogue uses Poppins SemiBold, weight 600, pure white #FFFFFF over a dark charcoal #17212B rounded rectangle at 60% opacity.
Dialogue font size 36–42 px at 1080 × 1440 resolution. Bottom-center, approximately 8–10% above the lower edge. Background padding 12 px vertical and 22 px horizontal, 10–12 px corner radius.

Synchronize subtitles precisely with the spoken words. Maximum two lines. No opening location title.

CRITICAL CONTINUITY AND ACTION RULES

The yellow flowers remain in Woman 1's LEFT hand from beginning to end. They never teleport, switch hands, disappear, or change appearance.

The smartphone begins in the tourist's possession, transfers visibly into Woman 1's RIGHT hand, remains in her right hand while she takes the photograph, and transfers visibly back to the original tourist.

The phone must never appear simultaneously in two people's hands except during the physical handoff.

Woman 1 must actually take the photograph. Show the phone aimed at the couple and a deliberate shutter-button press.

The tourist couple must remain stationary and visibly pose during the photograph.

Only the designated speaker delivers each line. Accurate lip synchronization, no overlapping or duplicated dialogue.

Maintain consistent character identities, clothing, props, background architecture, lighting, and spatial orientation across all three shots.

No extra characters entering the interaction, no duplicated people, no extra hands, no distorted phones, no unnatural gestures, and no unexplained object transfers.

Prioritize the physical sequence: offer → receive phone → photograph → return phone → joke → walk away.

The finished scene should feel like a spontaneous, slightly humorous interaction captured during a real afternoon in Seattle.
