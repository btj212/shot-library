Prompt

Create a 15-second, 4:3 French New Wave café scene with a vintage 35mm color-film look. Use @Image1 for the woman and @Image2 for the man, preserving their faces, hairstyles, and outfits.

They sit opposite each other at a small marble table in a Parisian café, with espresso cups, blue wooden window frames, and soft afternoon light.

0–4s: Centered medium close-up of the woman. She catches him watching her and smiles slightly, a little shy.
Woman: “Don’t look at me like that.”

4–6s: Cut to a centered close-up of the man. He gives her a small, curious smile.
Man: “Like what?”

6–10s: Cut back to the woman. She briefly looks down at her cup, then back at him.
Woman: “Like you’re happy just sitting here.”

10–12s: Cut to the man. He answers warmly and simply.
Man: “I am.”

12–15s: Cut to a balanced side-on two-shot, with the woman on the left and the man on the right. They meet each other’s eyes, then both smile shyly, one reacting to the other. No hand-holding.

Keep the camera still and the characters centered, with their heads naturally turned toward each other. In close-ups, the woman looks slightly off-camera right and the man slightly off-camera left. Keep their eyes aligned naturally with their faces.

Understated acting, small smiles, natural conversational timing, and accurate English lip sync. Fine 35mm grain, soft highlights, warm skin tones, muted reds and blues. Quiet café ambience. No music or subtitles.
