【0–2.5 seconds】

The shot begins with movement already in progress: a luxurious private jet cabin with symmetrical composition along the central axis. The female protagonist walks quickly forward alone along the central aisle, looking natural but slightly alert. The camera smoothly tracks backward, maintaining distance from her. The cabin is illuminated by stable, warm golden artificial lighting, appearing bright, warm, and luxurious. Outside the windows are gloomy blue-gray clouds, with no direct sunlight.

As she walks forward, the cabin ceiling lights and wall lights suddenly begin flickering continuously. The entire cabin alternates between bright and dim due to the electrical malfunction, causing the woman's face and the seats to repeatedly shift between light and shadow. She immediately stops walking and looks up at the lights, her expression instantly becoming alert.

The next moment, the aircraft suddenly jolts violently. Inertia throws her body forward, causing her to stumble one step. She immediately reaches out and grabs the seat beside her. A transparent glass tumbler on the table responds to the aircraft's movement with realistic inertia, sliding across the tabletop toward the edge before naturally falling off and shattering. The glass must maintain continuous, physically realistic movement, without floating, teleporting, or suddenly disappearing.

Hearing the abnormal loud noise, the woman immediately looks toward the cockpit, realizing something is wrong. As the aircraft experiences its first violent shake, the camera suddenly accelerates backward, instantly establishing a strong sense of impact.

【2.5–4.5 seconds】

The aircraft violently loses control again, and the cabin suddenly tilts to one side. The woman immediately turns and rushes toward the cockpit, but after running only a few steps, a violent lateral jolt strikes without warning. She loses her footing, her body is thrown sideways, and she crashes heavily onto the aisle floor.

She does not stop. She immediately reaches out and grabs the base of the nearest seat, trying to get back up, but the aircraft tilts again, throwing her back down through inertia. The camera rapidly tracks backward with slight shaking, continuously following her struggle.

【4.5–7.5 seconds】

Realizing she can no longer stand normally, the woman immediately switches to crawling along the floor toward the cockpit. She alternately grips the bases and armrests of the seats on both sides with her hands, struggling to move forward while being pulled and thrown around by the aircraft's continuous shaking.

A violent jolt makes her slide backward a short distance. She grips the seat tightly to stabilize herself, then continues forward. The aircraft briefly returns to a level position. She immediately pushes herself up, staggers forward, rushes to the cockpit door, and forcefully pushes it open.

The camera follows rapidly from behind her at a low angle, producing realistic handheld shake as she crawls, gets up, and sprints. The cockpit doorframe quickly sweeps across the frame and completely obscures the camera, creating a seamless spatial transition through occlusion rather than using a conventional hard cut.

【7.5–10 seconds】

The camera seamlessly enters the cockpit from behind the door, following the woman from behind. She collapses into the left pilot's seat and grips the control yoke tightly with both hands.

The cockpit combines warm artificial lighting with the cold blue environment outside. Churning black thunderstorm clouds fill the windshield. Instrument warning lights begin flashing. She pulls the control yoke sharply, trying to control the aircraft, but the plane continues tilting violently.

The camera rapidly pushes in from over her shoulder, briefly entering her point of view: thunderstorm clouds rush closer at high speed as the aircraft shakes violently.

【10–14 seconds】

Cut to a close external aerial shot of the aircraft.

The same private jet suddenly loses altitude, and its nose begins pointing noticeably downward. The camera tracks parallel to the aircraft, clearly capturing the complete progression from an uncontrolled tilt into a steep dive.

The aircraft continues descending at high speed, with the dive angle becoming increasingly steep. The wings shake violently in the powerful airflow, and the fuselage visibly jolts up and down. Do not make the aircraft suddenly disappear or crash instantaneously.

Keep the entire aircraft visible in the frame while slightly tracking downward, allowing the audience to clearly feel that the plane is rapidly falling. The thunderstorm clouds remain in the distance as a dark background, with occasional lightning flashes across the sky, but the aircraft must remain clearly visible and must not enter the clouds.

The main focus of this section is to convey realistic high-speed loss of control and a strong sense of vertical descent.

【14–20 seconds】

Instantly return to the cockpit.

Red emergency warning lights continue flashing, with red light repeatedly sweeping across the woman's face. The light fixtures remain intact, with no explosions, shattering, or exaggerated visual effects.

Extreme close-up of her face: her hair is disheveled, her breathing is rapid, and her expression gradually changes from panic to genuine despair. She desperately pulls the control yoke, but the aircraft still does not regain control.

She finally realizes that the aircraft is continuing to descend and that she can no longer save it. She stares forward, briefly losing focus, as all hope disappears from her eyes.

Amid the violent shaking, she screams one desperate “NO!” The scream continues and gradually becomes uncontrollable. The red warning lights repeatedly sweep across her face, and her hair moves slightly with the shaking. She keeps looking forward, unable to do anything, forced to watch helplessly as the aircraft continues descending.

For the final approximately 2 seconds, hold on an extreme close-up of her desperate, terrified face. Let the scream end naturally, then cut off abruptly.
