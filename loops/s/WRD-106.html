Prompt

Style: Ultra-realistic cinematic fashion commercial, magical Christmas atmosphere, luxury lifestyle aesthetic, spontaneous reactions, realistic motion physics, continuous handheld camera movement.
Character: A beautiful young woman with long, voluminous blonde curls, soft glamorous makeup, rosy cheeks, glossy pink lips and expressive eyes. She wears an ivory floral corset top with sheer, flowing bell sleeves, a matching layered lace mini skirt and carries a small black luxury handbag.
Location: An elegant European city street at night, surrounded by grand historic buildings decorated with warm golden Christmas lights, illuminated trees, vintage streetlamps and snow-covered pavements.

00:00–00:02:
The camera follows the woman from behind as she casually walks along the snowy pavement. Suddenly, Santa Claus's luxurious red-and-gold sleigh rushes past her on the left, scattering snow into the air. She quickly turns around in surprise, her blonde curls swinging naturally.

00:02–00:04:
Santa throws a large pastel-pink luxury shopping bag with elegant rope handles and “WAVESPEED” branding toward her from the moving sleigh. She opens her mouth in shock and instinctively reaches forward, catching the bag with one hand while holding her black handbag in the other.

00:04–00:06:
The sleigh disappears down the illuminated street. The woman stares after it with wide eyes and an astonished expression before looking at the unexpected Christmas gift.
00:06–00:08:
Her shocked expression transforms into a delighted smile. She proudly lifts the pink shopping bag toward the camera, admiring her surprise delivery. The camera gently moves closer, capturing her joyful expression and the sparkling Christmas lights behind her.

Sound design: Festive sleigh bells, rushing wind, soft snow crunching, realistic shopping bag rustling, a surprised gasp and playful Christmas instrumental music.
Important: Maintain strict facial identity, hairstyle, outfit and body proportions throughout. Realistic hair and fabric movement, accurate hand-object interaction, natural facial expressions, believable bag trajectory, consistent environment and lighting. One seamless continuous shot, no abrupt transitions, no face morphing, no cartoon physics and no black-screen ending.

The sound design and technical settings are suggested additions for recreating the reference video.
