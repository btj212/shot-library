Prompt

A 15-second single continuous take in 9:16 vertical at 30fps: a photoreal 3D automotive render with the energy of a video game's garage customisation reel.

Image 1 is the car. Recreate the exact supercar from the object sheet: gunmetal grey paint that flips to mauve-rose in the light, carbon aero and trim, white turbine wheels with black centres, red calipers, slim white LED headlight strips, a full-width red taillight bar, a rear wing, red bucket seats and a small gold M emblem. Keep its proportions and identity locked in every frame.

Image 2 is only a layout guide for the room's geometry. The car sits on a circular turntable in the centre of the garage, ringed with a glowing cyan light. The far wall has a wide metal shutter with a bright white LED ceiling light box above it. Behind the camera is a glass entry ramp. Along the left wall are a tool pegboard with a holographic screen and a robotic arm on a floor rail. Tall black cabinets fill the far corners, a wheel rack stands in the near right corner, and white EV charger pods and a glowing glass panel sit on the right wall. Every wall, the floor and the ceiling are clean blank surfaces, with plain polished concrete underfoot and zero lettering, signage or overlays anywhere in the room.

The look is a clean, clinical, high-tech garage. Cool white light falls from overhead and the turntable ring and wall panels add cyan accents. The polished grey concrete floor has glossy wet reflections and a faint haze drifts across it. Long white light streaks slide over the car's paint. The image is crisp and high contrast, with a cool palette in which the car's red and gold are the only warm colours.

The camera is a handheld-feel FPV with wide fisheye barrel distortion. It uses aggressive whip pans, crash zooms and fast orbits, with heavy motion blur on every move. Speed ramps slow down on each upgrade and snap fast between them. Every transition is a camera move inside one unbroken take.

The shot opens on a locked wide hero frame from the glass entry ramp, looking toward the shutter. The car faces camera on the slowly rotating turntable, its cyan ring glowing and the ceiling light box blazing behind it. The camera whips in with a crash zoom down onto the hood, wobbling through the fisheye, as the robotic arm swings in from the left wall. The hood transforms in beats: grey paint, then raw exposed carbon weave, then a vented carbon hood with a thin mauve centre stripe, with small sparks on each swap. The camera dives in a fast orbit to the front wheel, with the wheel rack visible behind it. The rims swap on the beat, going from white turbine to gloss black mesh to satin gold forged 6-spoke with a polished lip, and the red calipers glow brighter. A low tracking slide along the side skirt follows as a livery wipes across the doors: angular mauve and deep red pencil-stroke graphics, pure shapes only. The camera swings to the rear three-quarter view. The red taillight bar flares, and the rear wing rises higher on carbon swan-neck struts and locks with a hydraulic hiss. The camera swoops over the roof and whips down past the gold wheel in a motion-blur smear. It then pulls back and settles into the same wide hero frame as the opening. The underglow ignites and cycles from cyan to red to white, the headlight strips flash on, and the turntable completes its turn as the car holds, fully built and gleaming.

Audio: a hard-hitting phonk drift beat at around 140 BPM, with a cowbell melody and distorted 808s. Every upgrade lands on a downbeat with a bass impact. Layer in pneumatic hisses, robotic arm servo whirrs, ratchet clicks, carbon panel clunks and a turntable motor hum, and end on an engine rev blip as the underglow fires.
