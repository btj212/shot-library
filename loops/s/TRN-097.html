Sunday workout in 15 shots: a shot list that writes the transition into every line ("Match cut into", "Whip pan transition", "Rhythmic cut into")

TRN-097TransitionsSeedance 2.5by @AIwithSynthia2026-11-21

A Seedance 2.5 (ElevenLabs Creative) lifestyle montage written as one line per shot in the form "SHOT n: size, lens + move / action / SFX". Each line names how it joins the previous one (match cut into the water-bottle fill, whip pan out of the apartment door, rhythmic cut to the squat rack, match cut into the mirror selfie), so the edit rhythm is directed in the prompt. Useful as a template for an athleisure or fitness-app spot.

Prompt

SHOT 1: ECU, 85mm slow push-in / A smartphone alarm lights up on the bedside table as morning sunlight spills across the bedroom. A young woman reaches out from under the blanket, silences the alarm, and stretches lazily with a sleepy smile / SFX: alarm vibration, soft birdsong, bedsheet rustle.

SHOT 2: MS, 35mm handheld follow / Cut to her sitting on the edge of the bed, tying her hair into a high ponytail. She wears a stylish fitted black gym crop top, high-waisted leggings, and white sneakers, getting ready for her Sunday workout / SFX: hair tie snap, fabric movement, sneaker tap.

SHOT 3: MCU, 50mm tracking shot / Match cut into her filling a transparent water bottle, adding a few ice cubes, and taking a quick sip before grabbing her gym bag / SFX: water pour, ice clink, zipper pull.

SHOT 4: WS, 24mm gimbal tracking / Whip pan transition as she walks confidently through the apartment entrance and heads outside into bright Sunday morning sunlight, gym bag over her shoulder / SFX: door click, footsteps, distant traffic.

SHOT 5: MS, 35mm tracking / Cut to her entering a modern gym with glass walls, mirrors, and fitness equipment. She scans the room with an excited smile before walking towards the workout area / SFX: gym ambience, footsteps, equipment clank.

SHOT 6: MCU, 50mm mirror composition / Match cut into her standing in front of a full-length gym mirror, holding her smartphone and taking a stylish mirror selfie. She adjusts her ponytail, smiles confidently, and captures the perfect shot / SFX: camera shutter, phone tap.

SHOT 7: WS, 35mm low-angle tracking / Rhythmic cut into her walking towards the squat rack, placing her water bottle nearby, adjusting her leggings, and positioning herself under the barbell with focused determination / SFX: metal bar clink, sneaker shuffle, gym ambience.

SHOT 8: MS, 50mm smooth lateral slide / Cut into a series of controlled squats. She lowers into a deep squat with proper form, pauses briefly, and pushes back up, repeating the movement with focused expressions. Natural muscle movement and realistic body mechanics / SFX: controlled breathing, barbell movement, shoe grip.

SHOT 9: CU, 85mm cinematic close-up / Match cut into sweat glistening naturally on her forehead as she finishes her final repetition. She exhales, smiles proudly, and reaches for her towel / SFX: heavy breathing, weights settling.

SHOT 10: MCU, 50mm handheld / Rhythmic cut as she wipes sweat from her forehead, cheeks, and neck with a soft gym towel. She looks into the mirror with a satisfied smile / SFX: towel rustle, soft breathing, distant weights.

SHOT 11: Insert shot, 85mm macro / Object pass into her hand peeling a fresh banana. She takes a bite while sitting on a gym bench, with her water bottle beside her / SFX: banana peel movement, soft bite, bottle cap click.

SHOT 12: MS, 35mm slow push-in / Cut to her sitting comfortably on the workout bench, eating the banana and scrolling through her phone, occasionally smiling at the selfie she just captured / SFX: phone taps, gym ambience.

SHOT 13: CU, 85mm mirror reflection / Match cut into her standing before the gym mirror again. She gently fixes her ponytail, gives herself a confident smile, and adjusts her gym top / SFX: fabric rustle, subtle camera shutter.

SHOT 14: MCU, 50mm direct-to-camera push-in / She notices the camera, smiles playfully, raises her hand to her lips, and blows a sweet flying kiss directly towards the lens before giving a little wink / SFX: soft kiss, playful giggle.

SHOT 15: WS, 24mm cinematic pull-out / Final shot of her picking up her gym bag and water bottle, waving goodbye to the camera, and walking confidently out of the gym into beautiful Sunday morning light / SFX: footsteps, gym door opening, upbeat music fading out.
