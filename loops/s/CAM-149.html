Prompt

15s cinematic coral reef expedition, 16:9, 24fps. In a single continuous shot, single unbroken scene, no scene cuts. Dynamic continuous forward camera glide navigating through an underwater reef canyon. [0-5s] Tight probe-camera motion skimming inches above vibrant brain corals, purple soft sea fans, and bright orange clownfish hiding inside swaying sea anemones within a narrow reef crevice. [5-10s] The camera smoothly accelerates forward, threading cleanly between two towering limestone coral pinnacles covered in giant sea sponges and darting neon wrasses. [10-15s] The canyon suddenly opens into a vast, sun-drenched turquoise lagoon shelf, revealing a solitary reef shark patrolling quietly in the distance beneath shimmering volumetric caustic webs. Pristine water clarity, organic fluid sway, rich oceanic colors, authentic 35mm lens optics. Sound design: muffled aquatic hum, gentle water flow, faint wave echoes. Exclude: scene cuts, divers, bubbles on lens, cartoon, CGI gloss, watermark.
