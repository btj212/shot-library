Prompt

Create a 15-second cinematic 16:9 New York City intro. A seamless one-take vintage paper collage animation inspired by classic  travel books.

Use realistic magazine photo cutouts mixed with hand-drawn illustrations, torn paper edges, paper grain, halftone dots, gouache textures, pastel colors, and playful stop-motion movement. Warm off-white background, elegant negative space, smooth camera tracking, and layered parallax.

0–4 sec: New York Harbor appears with a paper-cutout Statue of Liberty, tiny boats, yellow taxis, and the Brooklyn Bridge unfolding in the background.

4–8 sec: Camera tracks across the Brooklyn Bridge toward the Manhattan skyline. Yellow taxis bounce through the foreground while coffee cups, pretzels, and hot dog carts briefly pop into view.

8–12 sec: Transition into stylized Times Square with abstract colorful billboard clippings and a pop-up Empire State Building. No readable signs or real brands.

12–15 sec: The city collage smoothly transitions into a peaceful Central Park-inspired landscape with green paper trees, a small bench, and tiny walking figures. All elements settle into a beautiful vintage paper composition, revealing the letters “NY” in the center, designed in a charming handmade paper-cutout style. End with generous off-white negative space.

Visual style: Vintage New York travel poster, handmade paper collage, gouache and pastel textures, warm colors, charming stop-motion animation, premium cinematic motion graphics.
