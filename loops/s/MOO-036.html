Prompt

On a long compressed lens the camera creeps in very slowly. The thick white cloud pours over the flat summit and tumbles down the cliffs in slow ribbed cascades, curling and dissolving into vapour halfway down. Below, the coastal city stays bright in hard afternoon sun, and small white lines of surf roll in along the shore of the deep blue sea. The motion is calm and steady, like a slow-motion waterfall. Sound: steady ocean wind, distant surf, faint city hum.
