Prompt

The jumping spider holds still on the dewy stem, then tilts its head slightly, its big glossy front eyes catching a shifting glint of dawn sky. Its fuzzy pedipalps twitch and bob, and one front leg lifts and resets on the stem. A tiny dewdrop on its head trembles and rolls off. The camera creeps in a few millimetres with shallow focus. Sound: near silence, a faint dawn birdsong, a soft breeze.
