Prompt

Create a premium Hollywood-style live-action cinematic sequence featuring two versions of the same woman.

15-second scene breakdown:
0–2s: A woman in black desperately climbs a steep, wet volcanic cliff above a raging ocean.
2–4s: A massive wave crashes over her. She loses her grip and struggles to survive.
4–6s: Exhausted, trembling, and close to giving up, she struggles to climb again.
6–8s: The camera reveals another version of herself in ivory white, standing higher up the cliff.
8–11s: The rescuer reaches down. Their left hands meet in a dramatic cinematic close-up, and she pulls the climber upward.
11–13s: The woman in black gets completely onto the platform. Once safe, they embrace tightly.
13–15s: An emotional close-up captures their reunion as the final subtitle appears:

"Only you can save yourself."

Visual direction: Premium Hollywood realism, realistic ocean physics, natural human movements, dramatic camera choreography, consistent character identities, cinematic lighting, emotional storytelling, and powerful background music.

Important: Maintain anatomical accuracy, realistic hand contact, continuous rescue movement, and consistent spatial positioning. No teleportation or unexplained cuts.

The goal is to make every frame feel like a scene from a real Hollywood survival movie.
