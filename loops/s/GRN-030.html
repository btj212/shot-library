Create one continuous six-second Western shot of the brim lift: an extreme low-angle close-up of the station keeper against the pale sky, her hat brim cropping the top of frame, as she tips it up at the first sight of the rider's dust. Calm, luminous, natural.
A lone 1870s horse-relay station on a high bleached mesa top at noon: cracked bone-tan clay, grey-green sage, silvered juniper snags, far pale ochre mesas under a faded sky-blue sky. An adobe station house with a sagging grey-timber porch, a split-rail corral, a tarred plank trough and a crooked cedar telegraph pole line. Everyone wears patched, sun-rotted cotton, buckskin, canvas and wool, sweat-stained felt hats, dust in every seam; brass is tarnished, iron hand-forged. Dry tumbleweeds bounce across the open ground on a hot wind. Nothing is new: every board, rope, saddle and cloth is decades-worn and mended. Every person is an original fictional adult.
At the porch rail, filling the lower frame against the sky, stands Honora Coyle, a fictional 44-year-old widow who keeps the relay station: strikingly beautiful in a natural classic-film-star way, fair skin with a soft natural tone and only a light dew of sweat, strong cheekbones, calm grey-green eyes with deep squint lines, tall and straight-backed, dark auburn braid tied with a frayed faded-blue ribbon. She wears a sun-bleached, patched blue calico work dress with rolled sleeves, a stained tan canvas apron, a sweat-stained bone felt hat and cracked brown boots. A sun-greyed porch post stands soft in the near right foreground.
Camera: a low dolly on a short track below the porch rail, looking steeply up at her face against the sky. 50mm lens impression, no wide-angle distortion. One very slow forward creep of forty centimetres. Because the camera inches closer from below, the post slides out of the right edge and her brim comes to crop the top of frame as a hard horizontal line. Not a zoom, not a tilt, not handheld.
Timing: seconds zero to two, Honora stands still against the faded sky, the brim's shadow soft across her brow, skin natural and matte with a faint dew at the temple. Seconds two to four, her eyes shift far off camera-left. Seconds four to five, with two fingers she tips the brim up a finger's width and her eyes narrow. Final second, she holds still, brim edge level across the top of frame. End on her eyeline.
