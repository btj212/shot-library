Prompt

A bold full-bleed risograph motion poster, NO frame, NO border, NO paper edges: the artwork fills the whole 16:9 screen edge to edge, exactly the riso grain, flat ink colours and illustration style of Image 1. Start almost empty and assemble the poster of Image 1 with BIG, playful motion, ending exactly on its composition. 0-3 s: a flat blue sky with the city skyline; the giant black-and-white cat drifts down from the top of frame like a fluffy cloud and settles onto the rooftops, eyes closed. 3-6 s: the letters of SUNDAY slam up from behind the skyline one by one with a squash-and-stretch bounce; spell it exactly. The cat stays IN FRONT of the letters, its head poking out between them exactly like Image 1. 6-9 s: the cat yawns hugely, stretches one paw across the letters, and the letters wobble; then it snuggles back down. 9-12 s: tiny details come alive: windows light up, a small plane loops behind the letters, the cat's tail flicks and curls over the skyline. 12-15 s: confident slow camera push-in toward the cat's sleepy face as it opens one eye, winks, and closes it again; SUNDAY stays perfectly still and sharp behind it. Sound: a lazy lo-fi Sunday-morning piano loop, a big yawn, soft bouncy thumps on each letter, gentle purring. No other text.
