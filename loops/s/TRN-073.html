Prompt

15 shots across 30 seconds, montage, every shot 2.0s: S1 0–2s, S2 2–4s, S3 4–6s, S4 6–8s, S5 8–10s, S6 10–12s, S7 12–14s, S8 14–16s, S9 16–18s, S10 18–20s, S11 20–22s, S12 22–24s, S13 24–26s, S14 26–28s, S15 28–30s. Hard cuts. Real-time, no slow motion.

Style: 8K photoreal live-action fantasy comedy, large-format film, organic grain. NOT a 3D render, NOT a game engine, NOT a game cutscene, NOT a cartoon. Pore-level skin. 24fps, 180-degree shutter, real motion blur, no warping, no morphing.

NO ON-SCREEN TEXT — CRITICAL: no captions, subtitles, titles, logos or watermarks at any point.

THE SCRIPT — CRITICAL: only these words are spoken, by the woman, nothing improvised: S7: "Ooohh... Nooo!!". S8: "Am I dreaming??". S13 and S14: a wordless scream only. Nobody else speaks.

THE TONE — CRITICAL: gentle comic fantasy. Her reactions are real, never mugging; the waiter stays dead serious and elegant.

LIVING SKY — CRITICAL: the bed floats in open sky surrounded by gathered white clouds, drifting slowly downward but never falling. Clouds drift at different speeds, vapor slides over the bed edge, wind gusts in uneven pulses lifting the duvet hem, her hair and the waiter's coat tails.

ASSETS:

@Image 1 = the woman, 100% match to reference face, hair and wardrobe.

@Image 2 = the waiter, full formal restaurant uniform: black tailcoat, white shirt, black bow tie, white gloves, slim black backpack with a pull cord on its left side.

GEOMETRY: a white-sheeted double bed centred in frame, sky and clouds on all sides, sun upper left. The waiter lands on the foot of the bed, she sits at the head. Birds travel screen-right to screen-left. S14–S15: she sits in a window seat, window on screen-left, passengers on screen-right.

FIRST FRAME: tight close-up on her sleeping face, no empty hold.

ACTION (montage, every cut on the 2-second mark):

S1 (18°/100mm close-up, gentle handheld): asleep, uneven breathing; birdsong grows loud off-frame, her brow knits, she groans and turns her head on the pillow, eyes shut.

S2 (29°/85mm, side angle): a wind gust whips her hair across her face; eyes still closed, she swats it away.

S3 (34°/65mm, high angle over the bed): she yanks the duvet up to her chin; warm sunlight crawls onto her face and she squeezes her eyes tighter, annoyed.

S4 (crash zoom): she sits bolt upright, duvet sliding off; the lens snap-zooms into an extreme close-up of her eyes flying wide open.

S5 (zoom out to 63°/30mm medium wide): reveal the bed floating in the middle of the clouds, golden sun flaring soft.

S6 (84°/22mm wide): a flock of birds enters frame-right, chirps loudly at centre frame and exits left; her head follows them, mouth open.

S7 (47°/50mm medium, handheld): she grabs her head with both hands: "Ooohh... Nooo!!"

S8 (18°/100mm close-up): utterly bewildered, head tilting, eyes darting around the clouds, lips parted, she mutters: "Am I dreaming??"

S9 (63°/30mm medium wide): a waiter drops from above in freefall and lands on the foot of the bed, mattress compressing.

S10 (47°/50mm): he lifts a polished chrome dome off a plate like a grand banquet course: just a plain sandwich. He pulls the cord on his backpack side and is yanked straight up out of frame top.

S11 (47°/50mm medium): still wrapped in the duvet, she bites the sandwich, chews, and smiles wide, savouring it.

S12 (84°/22mm wide, low behind her shoulder): a deep roar; a passenger jet bursts through the clouds, its nose filling the frame, heading straight at her.

S13 (fast horizontal orbit ending at 47°/50mm): the camera swings around her and lands on her face as she screams.

S14 (match cut, 47°/50mm, aircraft cabin): same head position, same angle, same screaming expression, now in casual travel clothes in a window seat. The scream carries over the cut; nearby passengers jolt and turn to stare.

S15 (47°/50mm): she freezes, cheeks flushing, covers her face with both hands and turns toward the window.

PHYSICS: real gravity on landings, mattress compressing and rebounding, coat tails lagging, the cord yank jolting his body upward. Only the bed floats.

ACTING: she is invested in one task: figure out where she is. Her eyes hunt the sky, the birds and the waiter, checking if this is real; in S14–S15 they dart to the staring passengers, then escape to the window. Natural blinks, never a frozen stare. The waiter keeps polite focus on her.

LIGHT & COLOUR: warm morning sun upper left, bright cloud bounce. ~70% soft white and sky blue, ~25% warm sun gold on skin, ~5% chrome glint from the dome. S14–S15: soft cool daylight through the cabin window.

AUDIO: birdsong, wind gusts, duvet rustle, mattress creak, chrome dome lift, cord snap and whoosh, jet roar, her lines, cabin hum and murmurs. No music, no score, no swell, no drone.

LOCKS: order holds — sleep, disturbed, sits up, birds, disbelief, waiter's sandwich, she savours it, jet, match cut to plane, hides face. Same face and hair throughout. No CGI look, no gimbal glide, skin never plastic.
