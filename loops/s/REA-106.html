"Tonal response" spec prompt: a backlit library reading room that reads as real cinematography, no reference image (Seedance 2.5)

REA-106RealismSeedance 2.5by @NEXUS_TO_NOVA2026-11-27

A text-only Seedance 2.5 test that tries to kill the "AI look" by writing the prompt like a cinematographer's exposure report instead of an adjective list: SCENE CHARACTERISTICS, then numbered sections such as TONAL RESPONSE ("VISUAL SYNTAX: high-contrast daytime interior... near-white window regions contrast with a substantially darker room"), how highlights clip, how mid-tones sit on the face, motion blur on a foreground passer-by, and a long NO-list of temporal artefacts (exposure pumping, texture crawling, light following subjects). The result is a restrained, underexposed reading room with silhouettes crossing tall windows that looks like real 35mm b-roll. Reuse the section structure for any interior where you need believable light, e.g. a bank, hotel lobby or museum spot.

Prompt

SCENE CHARACTERISTICS:

Daytime interior of a large architectural

reading room or institutional lounge.

Tall, vertically proportioned windows with

dark mullions occupy the background.

Long beige-brown curtains frame the windows.

Dark walnut-colored wooden partitions,

deep charcoal upholstered seating,

radiators beneath the windows, and small

warm amber practical lamps occupy the room.

Strong exterior daylight enters through

the windows, producing a high-contrast

backlit interior.

A woman wearing a pale, desaturated blue

dress crosses the middle ground.

A darker foreground figure passes across

the frame with pronounced motion blur.

The visual aesthetic is naturalistic,

subdued, and photographically restrained.

1. TONAL RESPONSE

VISUAL SYNTAX:

High-contrast daytime interior photography.

Extremely bright, near-white window regions

contrast with a substantially darker room.

Exterior daylight is partially overexposed,

especially around the upper and central

windowpanes.

Some exterior architectural geometry and

greenery remain visible through selected

lower and right-side windows.

Interior exposure remains relatively low.

Dark wooden surfaces fall into deep brown

shadows, while upholstered seating approaches

near-black charcoal.

The woman's face, hair, shoulders, and pale

blue clothing occupy comparatively brighter

middle tones.

Soft daylight creates gradual tonal

transitions across her face and clothing.

Dark figures near the edges of the frame

appear partially silhouetted against the

bright windows.

Small practical lamps introduce localized

yellow-amber highlights within the darker

interior.

CONSTRAINTS:

NO artificial HDR balancing;

NO uniformly illuminated interior;

NO excessive shadow recovery;

NO flattened contrast;

NO completely black, featureless room;

NO artificial glowing outlines;

NO dark nighttime exposure;

NO dramatic sunlight beams;

NO exaggerated cinematic bloom.

2. CHROMATIC RESPONSE

VISUAL SYNTAX:

Dominant color palette:

Soft daylight white;

cool grey-blue exterior surfaces;

muted beige and taupe curtains;

deep walnut brown wooden partitions;

charcoal-black furniture;

soft golden-amber practical lights;

pale, desaturated powder-blue clothing.

The dominant illumination is natural

daylight entering from the background.

Exterior daylight appears relatively

neutral to slightly cool, with bright white

highlights and subdued blue-grey tones.

The wooden interior introduces restrained

warm brown undertones.

Three visible practical lamps emit localized

yellow-orange illumination beneath their

shades.

Their amber light remains concentrated

near the fixtures rather than washing the

entire room in orange.

Skin tones retain subdued natural warmth

against the cooler daylight environment.

The woman's pale blue clothing provides

a distinct cool-color accent within

the otherwise neutral-brown interior.

Overall saturation is low to moderate.

CONSTRAINTS:

NO nighttime blue color cast;

NO exaggerated teal-orange color grading;

NO saturated orange interior;

NO cyan shadows;

NO neon-colored illumination;

NO unnatural skin saturation;

NO uniform warm filter;

NO aggressive color contrast;

NO strongly saturated furniture or walls.

3. IMAGE MICROSTRUCTURE

VISUAL SYNTAX:

Natural photographic image texture.

Moderately soft image detail without

excessive digital crispness.

The woman's face and clothing retain

recognizable surface details.

Fine hair strands catch the window light,

producing subtle luminous separation

against the dark background.

Wooden partitions preserve broad grain

patterns and muted surface reflections.

Dark upholstery appears matte and absorbs

much of the available light.

The bright window regions contain soft

tonal transitions around the edges of

their structural mullions.

Overall image rendering is restrained,

with gentle microcontrast and subtle

photographic softness.

The blurred foreground figure exhibits

reduced spatial detail due to movement,

while stationary architectural features

remain comparatively defined.

CONSTRAINTS:

NO hyper-sharpened textures;

NO plastic-looking skin;

NO exaggerated clarity;

NO heavy synthetic film grain;

NO artificial sharpening halos;

NO excessive halation;

NO CGI-like surface reflections;

NO artificial texture enhancement.

4. OPTICAL GEOMETRY

VISUAL SYNTAX:

Horizontal 16:9 composition.

Eye-level camera perspective looking

laterally across the interior.

Medium-wide environmental framing.

A moderately wide-to-normal spherical

lens perspective, approximately 35–40mm

full-frame equivalent as a prompting choice.

Architectural windows form strong,

repeated vertical lines across the

background.

Tall rectangular windowpanes establish

rhythmic spatial divisions.

Wooden partitions frame the far left

and far right sides of the composition.

Dark seating occupies the lower foreground

and middle ground.

The woman in pale blue occupies the

middle-left portion of the composition.

A motion-blurred person crosses the

central foreground, partially obscuring

the woman's body and adjacent architecture.

A dark-clothed man remains near the left

partition.

Another figure is partially visible

at the right edge.

Foreground occlusion creates layered depth

without requiring exaggerated shallow

depth of field.

The architectural background remains

recognizable and spatially coherent.

CONSTRAINTS:

NO fisheye distortion;

NO exaggerated perspective stretching;

NO extreme wide-angle convergence;

NO artificial portrait-mode background blur;

NO floating foreground elements;

NO inconsistent architectural geometry;

NO perfectly centered subject framing;

NO excessive optical distortion.

5. TEMPORAL IMAGE BEHAVIOR

VISUAL SYNTAX:

Naturalistic live-action motion rendering.

A woman moves through a stationary

daylit architectural interior.

The windows, curtains, wooden partitions,

seating, and practical lamps maintain

stable spatial positions.

A foreground passerby crosses the camera's

field of view, producing pronounced

directional motion blur.

The moving figure partially overlaps

the woman and the surrounding furniture.

Motion blur is concentrated on the

fast-moving figure rather than uniformly

applied across the image.

The woman's facial features and clothing

remain comparatively readable.

The background architecture retains

structural definition throughout

the foreground movement.

Window illumination remains bright

and temporally consistent.

The scene maintains its characteristic

contrast between bright exterior daylight

and dark interior surfaces.

The small amber lamps maintain a consistent

warm color and localized intensity.

Moving subjects transition naturally

between brighter window-facing regions

and darker interior areas.

CONSTRAINTS:

NO temporal flickering;

NO exposure pumping;

NO sudden daylight color changes;

NO artificial nighttime transitions;

NO excessive motion trails;

NO ghosted duplicate bodies;

NO inconsistent shadow directions;

NO moving architectural structures;

NO fluctuating practical-lamp brightness;

NO artificial light following subjects;

NO unstable foreground-background occlusion;

NO texture crawling.
