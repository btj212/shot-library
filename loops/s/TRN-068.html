Prompt

0–3 seconds — Moroccan Desert: She films herself walking through breathtaking golden sand dunes at sunset, wearing a flowing terracotta outfit with a lightweight scarf fluttering in the breeze. The warm orange sky, distant hot-air balloons, and endless dunes create a spectacular backdrop. Her long hair moves naturally in the wind as she smiles charmingly into the camera. Realistic handheld selfie footage with gentle walking movements.
3–5 seconds — Phone Drop Transition: While turning to show the scenery, her phone suddenly slips from her hand and tumbles toward the sand. The footage shakes naturally, briefly capturing her hand reaching for it before the lens becomes completely dark as the phone lands. Realistic motion blur, muffled impact, and natural camera movement.
5–7 seconds — Hidden Transformation: During complete darkness, execute a perfectly concealed match cut. The phone is now lying on a cobblestone street in a stunning snow-covered European village. The same woman picks it up, now wearing an elegant ivory winter coat, burgundy knitted scarf, and stylish winter boots. Snowflakes drift past the lens as the camera rises naturally.
7–10 seconds — Alpine Fairytale Reveal: She lifts the phone into the exact same selfie angle and continues walking as if nothing happened. Behind her are charming snow-covered wooden chalets, glowing festive lights, towering Swiss Alps illuminated by a pink sunset, and a horse-drawn sleigh passing through the village. She looks around in delighted amazement before turning back to the camera with her beautiful, captivating smile.
CRITICAL RULES: The phone drop must be the ONLY transition mechanism. Hide all location, outfit, weather, and lighting changes during the completely dark frame. Maintain the woman's exact facial identity, natural beauty, hair consistency, realistic expressions, and identical selfie framing before and after the transition. No visible cuts, morphing, portals, magical effects, flickering, or camera-angle inconsistencies.
Ultra-realistic 4K travel-vlog footage, natural smartphone camera shake, authentic human movement, cinematic lighting, realistic skin texture, detailed scenery, believable environmental sounds, smooth motion, no text, no subtitles, no logos.
