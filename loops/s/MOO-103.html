"The Color of You": 60 s 2D love story where colour spreads from every touch, a grey-to-warm palette arc in one Seedance 2.5 prompt (Magnific)

An official Magnific short: a grey foggy town, a girl in a mustard coat who leaves colour wherever her fingertips land, and a boy who follows the trail through a bridge, stairs, a bakery and a flower cart to a night fountain. The prompt is a model of long-form structure: VISUAL STYLE, CHARACTERS with locked clothing, a SEQUENCE written as connected camera beats (crane down, side-track, track behind), DIRECTION rules ("motivated cuts between locations, never abrupt cuts within an action", keep the flower and sketchbook continuous) and a colour plan from cool grey with mustard accents to blue night with amber lamps to a warm finale. Swap the touch-colour gag for a paint, fabric-dye or phone-screen brand story.

Prompt

Create a complete cinematic animated short film, approximately 60 seconds, in 16:9. Tell the following love story through connected scenes, with clear visual continuity and a gentle, flowing pace

VISUAL STYLE
Stylized 2D feature animation, angular characters with elongated proportions, fine colored linework, no black outlines. Flat cel shading, subtle dry-brush textures, geometric architecture and layered white fog. Fluid hand-drawn motion. Keep character designs, clothing and props consistent throughout

CHARACTERS
Boy: curly black hair, glasses pushed onto his head, forest-green cardigan, white shirt, grey trousers, sketchbook
Girl: auburn bob, oversized mustard-yellow coat, charcoal trousers, brown ankle boots
Baker: short, round, small moustache, blue jacket, red scarf
Florist: elderly woman, silver bun, spectacles, dusty-rose cardigan

SEQUENCE
Open on a wide view of a grey, foggy town. Crane down toward the Boy sitting alone and sketching the colorless buildings. Move closer as he pauses, looking quietly disappointed

Cut to a side-tracking shot of the Girl walking across a bridge. She brushes her fingertips along a grey bench. Mustard yellow spreads smoothly across the wood from the exact point of contact. She continues into the mist

Show the Boy discovering the yellow bench. He touches it, looks toward the departing mustard coat and runs after her. Let his expression change naturally from surprise to curiosity

Follow him up a narrow stone staircase beneath washing lines. Yellow laundry marks her trail. Keep the ascent brief and purposeful, then cut to the bakery interior

The Boy is already inside, catching his breath. The Baker holds a plain paper bag with a baguette, smiles and points through the window toward the Girl walking away. The Boy follows his gesture

Outside, an elderly Florist takes a single yellow flower from her grey flower cart and places it in his hand. He accepts it gently, then follows the direction she indicates

Transition softly into night. Track behind him as amber streetlamps illuminate the route toward a fountain. The Girl stands beside it. She turns and smiles shyly as he approaches

Move into an intimate two-shot. He offers the flower; she accepts it. He opens his grey sketchbook toward her. Her fingertips touch the drawing and warm colors spread across the page. Hold their shared smile

Pull back as they sit together on the fountain’s edge. Color gradually spreads across the cobblestones and surrounding buildings. Rise into a wide view of the transformed town and fade gently to black

DIRECTION
Preserve complete gestures and gradual changes of expression. Use motivated cuts between locations, never abrupt cuts within an action. Keep the yellow flower and sketchbook continuous between shots. Begin with cool greys and mustard accents, shift to blue night and amber lamps, then reveal warm color in the finale

AUDIO
Natural foley only: pencil during visible drawing strokes, footsteps matching footfalls, light paper rustling, a brief breath inside the bakery and soft fountain water. No door sound inside the bakery. No music or spoken dialogue
