Using the attached image as reference, prompt 1:

Generate a 30-second epic contestant introduction package for a high-stakes metalworking competition. Core subject is the large man from <<image1>>. Main event is his slow dramatic entrance into the workshop, multiple intermediate shots of presence and environment, on-camera self-presentation with text overlays, and separate interview segment.

<<image1>> provides the man's face, mullet hairstyle, thick mustache, aviator sunglasses, overall powerful build and proportions. Do not use the original clothing or background from this image.

The man wears a heavy black leather blacksmith apron over a soot-stained open red plaid flannel shirt with cut-off sleeves, thick leather gloves tucked into his belt, dark work jeans, and steel-toe boots. His arms and apron show fresh soot and heat marks.

Language: American English. All voiceover and dialogue use the contestant's own deep, gravelly Southern American accent.

0-3s
Start state: Extreme wide of a dim industrial workshop, orange forge glowing at the far end, sparks drifting, anvil and tools silhouetted. The man stands small in the distance, backlit.
Main event: He begins walking slowly toward camera. Low, rising cinematic score starts under heavy forge ambience.
End state: He is still far but clearly advancing.

3-6s
Continuing: Same man, same clothing and lighting direction.
Main event: Cut to low-angle tracking shot following his boots and powerful stride on the concrete floor, sparks falling around him. 
Voiceover (his own voice): {They told me a man from the hills had no place at a forge this big.}
End state: Boots fill the lower frame as he continues forward.

6-9s
Continuing: Identity, clothing, and spatial direction locked.
Main event: Cut to medium side angle as he walks past hanging hammers and chains. Camera drifts with him. Close detail of soot on his forearm and leather apron.
End state: He passes the midground tools, still advancing.

9-12s
Continuing: Same continuous walk energy.
Main event: Cut to medium-wide frontal as he approaches. Camera slowly pushes in. Forge light rims his mullet and shoulders. He looks straight ahead.
End state: He fills more of the frame, roughly halfway to camera.

12-15s
Continuing: Same man and environment.
Main event: Quick cut to extreme close-up of his face and sunglasses reflecting the forge glow, then back to medium as he stops and plants his feet. Simple white text fades in lower third: 【Buck Harlan】.
End state: He stands still, text visible, looking into camera.

15-18s
Continuing: Clothing, identity, and workshop locked.
Main event: Cut to medium close-up. He speaks directly. Text updates to 【Buck Harlan · West Virginia】.
Dialogue (his own voice): {Name's Buck Harlan. Been swinging hammers since I could stand.}
End state: He holds the line, slight nod.

18-21s
Continuing: Same setup.
Main event: Cutaway to close-up of his gloved hand resting on the anvil, then back to his face as he continues.
Dialogue: {Today I came to prove the steel remembers who shaped it.}
Camera holds with a very slow push. Distant hammer strikes under the score.
End state: Text remains, he finishes the sentence.

21-24s
Clean hard cut to separate interview setup. Same man, same clothing, now against a darker workshop wall with soft key light and practical forge glow deep in background.
Main event: Medium shot of him standing or seated, looking slightly off-camera as if speaking to an interviewer. 
Dialogue (same voice): {I don't care about the cameras.}
End state: He begins the next thought.

24-27s
Continuing: Same interview lighting and identity.
Main event: Cut to tighter close-up on his face and sunglasses. Natural hand gesture enters frame.
Dialogue: {I care about the heat, the iron, and whether the piece holds when it cools.}
Camera drifts slowly in.
End state: He delivers the line with weight.

27-30s
Continuing: Same.
Main event: Final cut to medium close-up. Slight confident smirk as he finishes. Text overlay locks lower third: 【Buck Harlan · West Virginia】.
Dialogue: {That's all that matters.}
End state: Frame holds on his face, text, and forge glow in background. Score and ambience settle.

Consistency lock
Keep the man's identity, mullet, mustache, sunglasses, build, clothing, soot marks, and voice identical across every shot. Maintain consistent workshop lighting direction and spatial orientation. No additional people. No subtitles except the specified on-screen text. No background music that overpowers dialogue.
