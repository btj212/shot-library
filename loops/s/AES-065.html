Prompt

15 seconds, 16:9. An original anime action sequence with a bold mixed-media aesthetic: hand-drawn cel animation, photographic city textures, torn-paper collage, halftone printing, charcoal smears, and expressive painted effects. The central gag: a small, scrappy fighter punches a gigantic robot so hard that it briefly flies through different animation materials before crashing back into its original world. Energetic, funny, spectacular, and visually coherent. Exactly two characters, one location, one punch, one clear payoff. No dialogue, captions, logos, or existing franchise characters.

The setting is a rain-soaked rooftop above a dense futuristic city at dusk. Photographic concrete and puddles sit beneath an illustrated violet sky. Windows appear as pasted scraps of luminous yellow paper. Wind pushes loose newspaper across the roof. Maintain consistent geography: the fighter stands screen-left, the robot screen-right, with an empty rooftop water tank behind the robot.

The fighter is a young adult woman with short black hair, a bright red oversized bomber jacket, black trousers, battered white trainers, and yellow boxing wraps. Her silhouette remains immediately readable. Her opponent is a towering, bulky security robot with a tiny rectangular head, enormous shoulders, chipped ivory armour, and a single amber eye. Its movements are ponderous and overconfident.

0–3 seconds: Open in motion. The robot’s huge metal fist slams into the rooftop where the fighter stood a moment earlier. She slides beneath its extended arm toward camera, one palm skimming a puddle. The splash becomes a fan of white paper cutouts, then resolves into water. A low tracking camera follows her slide. She plants one trainer, stops inches from the lens, and looks up at the robot with an irritated, almost bored expression. Her jacket settles a beat after her body.

3–6 seconds: Cut to a tight side view as she stands and draws back her wrapped right fist. The robot leans down, presenting its enormous chest as if inviting the challenge. Her fist begins trembling with contained energy. Pencil construction lines flicker around her forearm; small scraps of yellow paper lift from the wet ground and orbit her knuckles. The city ambience drops away. Hold a tiny anticipatory pause, letting her expression shift into a mischievous smile.

6–9 seconds: She drives forward and lands one exaggerated uppercut against the robot’s lower chest. Show the contact clearly in profile. At impact, insert three rapid graphic frames: black ink on white, a red halftone silhouette, then a bright yellow painted burst. Return immediately to the rooftop as the robot launches upward. The recoil sends a circular ripple through the puddles and flips her jacket collar over her face.

9–12 seconds: Track the airborne robot through three successive visual transformations within the same trajectory: dimensional metal becomes a folded cardboard puppet, then a charcoal drawing on textured paper, then a chunky clay figure. Each transformation lasts long enough to register. Preserve its silhouette, amber eye, momentum, and direction.

12–15 seconds: The robot returns to cel animation and drops headfirst into the empty water tank, leaving its legs sticking out. Cut to the fighter pulling her collar down. One loose metal bolt lands beside her shoe. She gives it a tiny dismissive kick. End on her deadpan expression.

Sound: isolate punch impact, metallic crashes, clothing movement, rooftop wind, and a short energetic percussion score into distinct layers. Give the impact a moment of silence beforehand, followed by a huge comic bass hit.
