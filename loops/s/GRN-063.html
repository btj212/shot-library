Prompt

1925 silent film comedy, black and white, a busy city street and a park,
sequence of cuts.

characters, fixed for the whole film:
the young man: around twenty-five, tall and skinny, clean-shaven, big ears,
checkered flat cap, striped jersey, suspenders, baggy trousers. no mustache,
no cane, no bowler hat.
the kitten: a small fluffy white kitten.

geography: the young man always carries one very long wooden ladder
horizontally on his right shoulder, until he puts it against the tree.

0-3s: iris opens on a wide static shot of a busy 1920s city street, the young
man walks in from the left carrying the long ladder on his shoulder.

3-7s: cut to wide static shot, he turns around and the back of the ladder
swings behind him, a gentleman in a top hat ducks just in time, his top hat
flies off.

7-10s: cut to wide static shot, a horse-drawn carriage passes, the young man
lifts the ladder high above his head and the carriage drives right under it.

10-14s: cut to wide static shot, a lady in a hat with a huge ostrich feather
walks by, the ladder sweeps over her and snips off the feather. he bows to
apologize, turns, and the ladder swings just over the heads of two painters
in white overalls, who duck.

14-17s: cut to wide static shot of a park, a fluffy white kitten sits on a
tree branch meowing, the young man leans the ladder against the trunk.

17-21s: cut to medium static shot, he climbs up heroically and reaches for the
branch, the kitten calmly walks down the trunk on the other side and strolls
away.

21-24s: cut to close-up of the young man's face, static, completely deadpan,
he slowly looks down.

24-27s: cut to medium static shot, the ladder slides sideways and falls to the
ground, he grabs the branch and hangs from it with both hands.

27-30s: cut to wide static shot, the young man dangles from the branch, the
kitten sits on the grass below and looks up at him. the iris slowly closes.

audio: a solo out-of-tune upright piano accompaniment in 1920s ragtime style,
bouncy and quick, tremolo chords at moments of danger, a descending comic
run when the ladder falls, a soft meow. no dialogue, no sound effects except
the piano and the meow.

style: shot on 35mm orthochromatic black and white film, 1.33:1 frame, soft
grey tones, bright white sky, heavy grain, scratches, dust specks, flicker,
dark vignette edges, iris-in and iris-out transitions, slightly fast jerky
hand-cranked motion typical of 1920s films, static wide camera.

constraints: black and white only, no color, no slow motion, no intertitles,
no readable text, no signs with letters, no logos, nobody is hurt, no
children, only one kitten, the ladder stays the same length, preserve the
young man's face and wardrobe across all cuts, no morphing hands.
