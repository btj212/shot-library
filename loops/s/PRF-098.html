The plush who doesn't want to be picked: a girl hides among claw-machine toys in a melancholy short (Seedance 2.5)

A 25 s surreal short, one prompt: a woman plays a claw machine in a laundromat while a small girl in a pink plush suit lies among the stuffed toys, trying not to be chosen. The performance is all in stillness and tiny reactions seen through the glass; references lock each face and wardrobe.

Prompt

Cinematic surreal short film, vertical 9:16, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, soft film grain. Tense yet whimsical, dreamy mood with a touch of melancholy. Smooth, slow, handheld-feeling camera.

- The tiny girl inside the machine is @Image2: keep her face and messy dark hair. She is the size of a plush toy and wears a fluffy pink plush teddy-bear costume with a removable bear head. Keep the costume design from the reference.
Do not copy backgrounds or lighting from the references; take them from the scene description below.

Location: an empty self-service laundromat with mint-teal walls, a pink stripe along the wall, a row of washing machines with round glass doors, fluorescent tube lights on the ceiling, and a black-and-white checkered floor. In the center stands a big glass claw machine with a butter-yellow base, lavender metal frame and a silver joystick panel. Inside, it is packed to the top with soft plush toys: teddy bears in brown, pink and yellow, a white bunny with pink cheeks, green frogs, ducks, hearts, and large felt balls in red, yellow and teal.

Story: the tiny girl lives among the plush toys inside the machine, dressed like one of them. When the woman starts playing, the girl panics, pulls the bear head of her costume over her own head to disguise herself as a toy, and scrambles across the pile to escape the descending claw so she won't be caught.

0-3s: Low-angle shot from inside the claw machine, plush toys in the foreground. @Image2 lies curled up between the toys, the bear head resting beside her. The giant face of @Image1 appears above, looking down through the glass, close-up, curious eyes.

6-9s: The metal claw starts to descend over the pile. The girl grabs the bear head and quickly pulls it on, instantly looking like a plush toy. Slow push-in.

9-12s: Extreme close-up of @Image1 in profile, reflection on the glass, pastel teal background, focused, quiet expression.

12-17s: Inside view looking up: @Image1 gazes down through the glass, the claw sweeps overhead. @Image2, wearing the bear head, crawls and scrambles over the toys, dives behind a big yellow bear, freezes whenever the claw passes close, then moves on.

17-20s: Wide static shot from the dark silhouette of an arcade cabinet in the foreground: @Image1 stands at the claw machine in the empty laundromat, long shadows on the checkered floor.

20-25s: The claw closes on a different toy and lifts it away. @Image2 stays hidden, then slowly lifts the bear head up, exhales and settles back against a large brown teddy bear next to the white bunny, eyes closed, red and yellow felt balls blurred in the foreground. She rests peacefully, subtle breathing movement.
