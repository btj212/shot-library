Prompt

Photorealistic cinematic sci-fi drama short film, shot like a high-budget feature on anamorphic lenses with natural film grain, believable zero-gravity physics (floating tools, drifting tears) and no gore. Opens with an instant hook, no establishing shot. Setting: a small deep-space relay station orbiting a blue gas planet, cramped corridors, glowing consoles, a pod bay, a large observation window. No real logos, brands or agency insignia. CHARACTER LOCK: Commander Eli is a man in his 40s, short graying hair, stubble, plain white suit with blue shoulder patches. Mara is a woman in her late 20s, dark hair tied back, same suit with orange shoulder patches. Both keep identical faces and suits in every shot. Cold blue space light against hot red alarm light, realistic skin texture, natural performances with no over-acting. Sound in vacuum scenes is muffled and silent. No text overlay, no watermark.
