3D previs to live action: tell Seedance 2.5 the blockout is a "badly compressed copy of the film" and ask it to recover the original

REA-107RealismSeedance 2.5 (previs made in CozyClay)by @Yun_HDY2026-11-26

A reusable trick for turning a rough 3D previs (here built in CozyClay) into photoreal footage without losing the blocking: the reference video is described as compression damage ("square pixel blocks and blur are compression damage, not part of the scene. Recover the original film behind it: same camera moves, same cuts, same timing"), and the untextured set is described as placeholders the damage "wiped out", so the model rebuilds a real, lived-in place. The creator feeds 1 mosaic frame + 3 blur frames on loop as the reference. Template prompt with [brackets] for setting, shots, lenses and light; ends with "Real live-action footage shot on 35mm film". The clip is a side-by-side grid of previs vs result. Ideal for car, night-street or chase-style spots that were previsualised first.

Prompt

[your video ref] is a badly compressed low-resolution copy of the film. Its square pixel blocks and blur are compression damage, not part of the scene. Recover the original film behind it: same camera moves, same cuts, same timing, and the same actions of [who].
The damage also wiped out most of the [place] around them, so the [buildings, props] in the copy are only rough placeholders; fill the [place] in fully as a real, lived-in place.

[setting with concrete texture, time of day]

The film has [N] shots, and the camera keeps moving inside each one.
Shot 1, [lens]: the camera [move] while [action]. Cut to shot 2, one continuous move: ...

Real live-action footage shot on 35mm film, [light], visible film grain.
