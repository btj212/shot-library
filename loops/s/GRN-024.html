Prompt

RAINY UNIVERSITY MORNING 

Create a realistic 30-second handheld lifestyle vlog using my reference image as the identity reference for the main character only. Keep her facial features, hair, skin tone, and overall appearance consistent throughout the entire video, but do not copy the reference image’s composition, outfit, setting, or poses.

SCENE 01 — A SLOW RAINY START

Early morning. Soft grey daylight fills a quiet bedroom while rain taps against the window.

The girl wakes up naturally, checks her phone for a moment, then looks toward the rainy window. Keep the acting subtle and unperformed — sleepy eyes, messy hair, small movements, no exaggerated expressions.

She is wearing simple cream sleepwear with muted peach details.

SCENE 02 — GETTING READY

A quick, natural getting-ready sequence.

She changes into a muted sage-green rain jacket, off-white top, dark charcoal straight-leg trousers, and white sneakers, carrying a simple neutral canvas shoulder bag.

She casually packs her phone, earbuds, water bottle, and notebook before leaving.

Keep everything imperfect and lived-in — small hand movements, natural pauses, realistic clothing motion.

SCENE 03 — WALKING THROUGH THE RAIN

She steps outside with a transparent umbrella and starts walking toward her university.

Wet sidewalks reflect the overcast sky. Cars pass in the background, people move around with umbrellas, rainwater gathers along the pavement, and her sneakers occasionally step through shallow puddles.

The camera follows her like a friend casually filming her morning — handheld, slightly imperfect, intimate, and unplanned.

SCENE 04 — ARRIVING AT UNIVERSITY

She reaches the university campus.

Show realistic campus details: wet pathways, students walking between buildings, bicycles, umbrellas, glass windows covered with rain droplets, and a quiet morning atmosphere.

She walks beneath a covered walkway, briefly looks toward the camera, gives a small natural smile, and continues toward the building.

No staged posing.

SCENE 05 — COFFEE & RAIN

End inside a cozy university café.

She sits beside a large rain-covered window with a warm cup of coffee. Outside, the campus remains grey and rainy.

She takes a sip, looks outside for a moment, then casually glances back toward the camera.

End on this quiet, unforced moment.

VISUAL STYLE

Authentic early-2000s consumer MiniDV / home-video aesthetic.

Handheld 4:3 framing, slight camera shake, imperfect composition, occasional focus hunting, subtle exposure shifts, natural DV noise and compression, soft overcast daylight, and warm practical lighting indoors.

The footage should feel found rather than filmed — like a real personal university vlog captured on an ordinary rainy morning.

SOUND

Use only natural environmental audio:

Rain against windows
Umbrella and footsteps
Distant traffic
Campus ambience
Doors opening and closing
Soft café sounds
Cup placed on the table

No background music.
No narration.
No dramatic sound effects.

CONTINUITY & REALISM

Keep the same woman, face, hairstyle, body proportions, outfit, umbrella, and accessories consistent across every scene.

Natural human movement is essential. Avoid plastic skin, beauty filters, exaggerated expressions, perfect camera movement, or overly cinematic shots.

Negative prompt: CGI, cartoon, anime, artificial skin, beauty filter, face drift, identity change, distorted hands, extra fingers, warped anatomy, floating objects, excessive bokeh, cinematic commercial look, drone shots, gimbal-perfect movement, oversaturated colors, fake rain, unrealistic reflections.
