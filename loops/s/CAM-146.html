Prompt

One continuous 30-second low-altitude chase shot, a single unbroken take with no cuts, shot at greyhound speed. On a vast muted-green lawn a distinguished older man in a cream golf outfit lines up a putt at a banana lying on the grass; a slender grey greyhound wearing a knitted cream balaclava snatches the banana at the exact moment of the putt and bolts; the camera drops to ten centimeters off the ground and chases the dog flat-out for the rest of the film.
0-5s: the theft: club swings, the dog's jaws close on the banana mid-stroke and it accelerates away in three strides, ears flat inside the balaclava; the golfer watches it go without any expression; the camera whips down and after it, grass streaking past the lens.
5-11s: across the lawn at full sprint, weaving between motionless cream sheep who do not react; the dog leaps a low hedge, the camera vaulting it a half-beat behind.
11-17s: through a butter-yellow doorway into a whitewashed coastal street, claws skittering on cobblestones, laundry lines whipping overhead; the dog cuts left as a red city bus sweeps past behind it as one long streak of motion blur.
17-23s: out of the street straight into a blush-pink world flooded ankle-deep with glassy water: the dog charges through in sheets of spray, past an elegant woman standing in a red paper boat who turns her head slowly to watch it pass, spray hanging in pink light.
23-27s: up an ochre steppe slope, petals and dust kicked up in its wake, the camera swinging wide alongside as the dog bounds onto a single rounded black boulder at the top.
27-30s: the dog places the banana carefully on the very top of the boulder like a trophy, sits bolt upright beside it, perfectly still, and stares into the lens, deadpan, balaclava slightly askew. Far below on the lawn, the tiny figure of the golfer raises his putter once in salute.
Audio: the chase scored by galloping paws on grass, then cobbles, then water, then dust - each surface changing the rhythm's texture; wind rush, one bus whoosh, one splash crescendo, sheep silence; a sprinting string ostinato that locks to the paw rhythm and cuts dead at 27s, leaving only wind and the dog's breathing.
Lens 35-50mm, stabilized low tracking, level horizon even at speed. Generate a single shot, one continuous take.

Style: editorial, refined, fashion-forward, deadpan surrealist fashion film with high kinetic energy. Grade: slightly desaturated, lifted blacks, compressed milky highlights, low contrast while colors stay legible, light 35mm film grain, faint halation on highlights only, matte print-like finish, warm cream as the neutral instead of pure white. ABSOLUTELY NO on-screen text, no titles, no captions, no logos, no typography of any kind anywhere in the frame. Negative: no harsh contrast, no HDR, no crushed blacks, no clipped whites, no neon saturation, no fisheye, no dutch angle, no smiling or mugging at the camera, no CGI gloss.
