Prompt

A little squirrel peeks its head out of a hole, its eyes moving curiously as it gently lifts its head and looks from side to side. It then raises one front paw, slowly bringing a small torch up beside its face and holding it upright. The squirrel tilts its head slightly, blinks naturally, and briefly closes its eyes with a cheerful smile before opening them again to look ahead, its mouth slightly open. Its body remains largely in place, with subtle, natural changes in head movement, gaze, and facial expression. All movements flow smoothly and continuously.

【Subject】The small squirrel exactly as in the first frame, peeking out of the hole.
【Action】Its eyes dart curiously left and right as it lifts its head; it raises one front paw and brings a small lit torch up beside its face, holding it upright; it tilts its head, blinks, closes its eyes briefly with a cheerful smile, then opens them and looks ahead with its mouth slightly open. The body stays in place; only the head, eyes and face change.
【Camera】Static close-up at the hole's height; no camera movement.
【Visuals】The torch flame becomes the warm key light on its face and fur against the dark burrow; natural fur detail.
【Sound】A soft crackle of the torch flame, tiny rustles of earth, one small squeak.
