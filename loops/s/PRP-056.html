Prompt

Part 1💥

SEEDANCE 2.5 — 16:9 — 30s — PART 1 of 2 — "할매 만두 (Granma's Dumplings)" — Korean horror-fantasy short, multi-shot cinematic, Korean dialogue, no BGM, natural ASMR ambience only.

REFERENCES
Granma — character sheet (≈90-year-old tiny woman, silver bun with red cat-head binyeo hairpin, plum quilted vest, cream blouse, white apron with red dumpling pocket, charcoal skirt, black slippers, green jade bangle on LEFT wrist).
YoungMan — character sheet (24, messy black fringe, black bomber with orange lining + reflective sleeve strips, grey hoodie, navy cargo joggers, white-grey sneakers, matte black helmet with clear visor).
Cat — character sheet (large solid-black tomcat, amber-gold slit eyes, notch on LEFT ear, red braided cord collar with small brass bell).
Stall — exterior location (tiny red hanok-roof stall, 「할매 만두」 sign, six red lanterns, steamer stack with steam on the left, indigo split curtain with white dumpling symbol as the only entrance, stool with cat figurine right of the curtain, white parking box on the right).
Hotel — interior location (vast dark-gold palace-hotel hall, three crystal chandeliers in a line, red dragon columns, red carpet to a double staircase, full-moon round window at the back, ONE round white-cloth table with one red velvet chair, gold-rim plate, silver chopsticks).

PROP LOCKS
- PROP-BIKE: small matte-black 125cc city scooter-motorbike. Parks inside the white parking box at frame right of Stall and stays there.
- PROP-HELMET: matte black helmet, clear visor. He takes it off outside and carries it in his RIGHT hand. When he sits, he sets it on the table at his LEFT elbow, and it stays there.
- TABLE SET: the one table holds only the gold-rim white porcelain plate (empty), silver chopsticks and spoon on a rest, and the helmet. NO food appears in Part 1.
- The curtain symbol, the 「할매 만두」 sign text and the chandeliers stay identical to the refs. No new signs, no extra text.

STAGING LOCK
- Outside: stall on the LEFT, parking box on the RIGHT, street recedes to the right with one cool street lamp. YoungMan arrives from frame right.
- Cat outside: sits upright on the small wooden stool to the RIGHT of the curtain, beside the cat figurine.
- Inside: the entrance curtain is at the front; the table is about 6 m ahead on the red carpet. YoungMan sits in the ONE red velvet chair. Granma comes down the staircase side (back of the hall) and stands on his RIGHT side of the table. Cat walks in and sits on the red carpet on his LEFT, beside the chair leg.
- No other people, guests, staff or animals anywhere. Only one cat, only one table.

LOOK / LIGHTING LOCK (keep identical tone in every shot)
- Exterior: cool deep-blue night, wet asphalt, light drizzle, warm red-gold lantern glow on the stall, light mist.
- Interior: warm rich gold chandelier key light with cool-blue moonlight fill from the round window, deep shadows in the far corners, light haze. Same exposure and grade in every interior shot.
- Ultra-photorealistic live action, low saturation, filmic grain. Horror-fantasy mood: beautiful but too still and too perfect.

SOUND
No music at all. Natural ASMR only: scooter engine idling then clicking off, drizzle and drips, steamer hiss outside, one long sad cat meow, the curtain fabric rustling, then near-silence inside. Footsteps echo on marble in the huge hall, crystals faintly tinkle, the chair creaks softly.
Voices: ONLY Granma speaks in Part 1, in a soft, slow, sweet old woman's voice. YoungMan never speaks; he only breathes and gives one stunned exhale. Cat only meows.

PACING
Slow and atmospheric. Each beat holds; silences breathe. No rushed cuts, no fast-forward feel.

BEATS
0–4s — Wide Stall at night in the drizzle, steam drifting from the steamers, lanterns swaying slightly. PROP-BIKE rolls in from the right and stops in the parking box; the engine clicks off. Quiet drizzle.
4–7s — Medium: YoungMan pulls off PROP-HELMET, shakes his damp fringe, looks at the glowing tiny stall. He breathes in the steam and gives a small, tired, hungry smile.
7–11s — He walks up to the curtain, helmet in his right hand. On the stool to the right of the curtain, Cat slowly turns his head toward him. Close-up of Cat: ears lower, amber eyes soften with pity, and he gives one long, low, sad meow.
11–13s — YoungMan glances down at the cat, chuckles softly under his breath, and parts the indigo curtain with his LEFT hand. Warm gold light spills across his face, and he steps through. Behind him, Cat hops off the stool and pads in after him.
13–19s — THE REVEAL. Reverse shot from inside: YoungMan stops just past the curtain. The camera cranes slowly up and back to reveal the vast Hotel hall: chandeliers, dragon columns, staircases and the moon window, impossibly bigger than the tiny stall. He turns slowly in place, looking up, lips parted, stunned. One shaky exhale. Cat walks calmly past his sneakers down the red carpet without looking back.
19–24s — Echoing footsteps. Wide: Granma appears on the staircase side and walks slowly down the red carpet toward him, small steps, hands folded on her apron, sweet warm smile. Medium close-up of her, clearly on screen, mouth in sync, she bows slightly and speaks:
   Granma: 「어서 와요, 손님. 오래 기다렸어요.」
   Reverse: YoungMan bows back awkwardly, unsure.
24–27s — She gestures with an open palm to the single table. He walks to it and sits in the red velvet chair, setting the helmet on the table at his left elbow. Cat sits down on the carpet at the left side of his chair, upright and still. Granma, standing at his right, smiles a beat too long, then turns and walks slowly away toward the staircase, out of frame.
27–30s — END SHOT (no characters visible): a slow, gentle push-in on the table top in close-up, showing only the empty gold-rimmed white plate and the silver chopsticks on their rest under the chandelier glow, with haze drifting in the light. No hands, no people, no cat in frame. Hold in silence.

NEGATIVE
No background music, no score, no stingers. No extra speaking voices: no narrator, no voiceover; YoungMan does not speak; Cat does not talk in Part 1, only one meow. No dialogue other than Granma's one line. No other people, guests, waiters or crowds; no second cat. No text, subtitles, captions, watermark or UI; no changes to the 「할매 만두」 sign. No food or dumplings on the table yet. No demonic or black eyes, no glowing eyes, no monster features in Part 1. No change to the lighting tone, no daylight, no interior flicker. No wardrobe drift; the helmet is never on his head inside. No floating objects, magic sparkles or particles. No jump cut through the reveal, no shaky handheld, no fast-forward look. Final 3 seconds: no characters in frame.

Part 2💥

SEEDANCE 2.5 — 16:9 — 30s — PART 2 of 2 — "할매 만두 (Granma's Dumplings)" — Korean horror-fantasy short, multi-shot cinematic, Korean dialogue, no BGM, natural ASMR ambience only.

REFERENCES
Frame — FIRST FRAME (start exactly on this image): top-down close-up of the table showing a large gold beaded-rim charger plate with a white-and-clear glass plate on it, a gold fork and gold chopsticks resting on a small gold rest to the right, a warm taupe-brown tablecloth, and soft window-light shadow patterns across the cloth. No people in frame.
Granma — character sheet (≈90-year-old tiny woman, silver bun with red cat-head binyeo hairpin, plum quilted vest, cream blouse, white apron with red dumpling pocket, charcoal skirt, black slippers, green jade bangle on LEFT wrist).
YoungMan — character sheet (24, messy black fringe, black bomber with orange lining + reflective sleeve strips, grey hoodie, navy cargo joggers, white-grey sneakers).
Cat — character sheet (large solid-black tomcat, amber-gold slit eyes, notch on LEFT ear, red braided cord collar with small brass bell).
Hotel — interior location (vast dark-gold palace-hotel hall, crystal chandeliers, red dragon columns, red carpet to a double staircase, full-moon round window at the back).
Eyes — demonic eye reference (solid glossy pitch-black eyes: the entire eye, iris and white, is filled black).

CONTINUITY FROM PART 1
- He is already seated in the single red velvet chair at this table. His matte black helmet sits on the table at his LEFT elbow.
- Cat sits upright on the red carpet at the LEFT side of his chair.
- Granma walked away toward the staircase at the end of Part 1 and returns from that direction.

PROP LOCKS
- TABLE SET (match Frame exactly): gold beaded-rim charger, white-and-clear glass plate, gold fork, gold chopsticks on a small gold rest, taupe-brown tablecloth. Do NOT turn it into a white cloth, silver cutlery or a different plate.
- PROP-STEAMER: ONE small round natural bamboo steamer with a woven bamboo lid. Granma sets it on the table just above the plate. When she lifts the lid, there are SIX identical plump white pleated wangmandu dumplings with steam rising. The count stays six until he takes one, then five. No other food appears.
- He eats with the GOLD chopsticks from Frame.
- Helmet stays at his left elbow the whole time.

STAGING LOCK
- YoungMan is seated in the chair, facing the moon-window axis.
- Granma stands on his RIGHT side of the table for the rest of the clip once she arrives.
- Cat hops from the carpet onto the table on his LEFT side, then sits upright on the cloth beside the helmet, facing him, about 50 cm away. He stays there.
- No other people, guests, staff or animals. Only one cat.

LOOK / LIGHTING LOCK (identical in every shot, matching Frame and Part 1)
Warm rich gold chandelier key light, soft window-shadow patterns, cool-blue moonlight fill from the round window, deep shadows in the far corners, light haze. Same exposure and grade throughout, including the final eye reveal. Ultra-photorealistic live action, low saturation, filmic grain. Horror-fantasy mood: beautiful but too still.

SOUND
No music at all. Natural ASMR only: slow slipper footsteps echoing on marble, crystals faintly tinkling, bamboo steamer set down with a soft knock, lid lifted with a hiss of steam, chopsticks clicking, a juicy bite, a soft paw-thud as the cat lands on the table, the cat's slow wet paw-lick, a hard gulp. The final 3 seconds are near-silent (room tone only).
Voices: ONLY two spoken lines. Granma speaks in a soft, slow, sweet old woman's voice. Cat speaks in a deep, slow, majestic, regal adult male voice. YoungMan never speaks; he only breathes and gulps. The two voices are clearly different people and never merge.

PACING
Slow and tense. Each beat holds. Silences breathe. No rushed cuts.

BEATS
0–3s — Start exactly on Frame: the empty plate and gold chopsticks, haze drifting in the light. Echoing slipper footsteps approach. Granma's wrinkled hands (green jade bangle on her LEFT wrist) enter the frame and set the bamboo steamer down just above the plate with a soft knock.
3–7s — Medium shot, Granma clearly on screen at his right, warm sweet smile, mouth in sync:
   Granma: 「천천히 드세요. 우리 집 만두는… 한 번 먹으면 못 떠나요.」
   She lifts the lid. Steam bursts up and reveals six plump dumplings. YoungMan smiles politely, slightly uneasy at the words.
7–10s — Wide-ish two-shot: Cat hops from the carpet onto the table at his left with a soft thud, settles upright beside the helmet and stares at him with a stone-cold face. He glances at the cat, then back to the food.
10–14s — Close-up: he lifts ONE dumpling with the gold chopsticks, blows on it once, takes the first bite, juice and steam. His eyes widen at how good it is; he chews slowly, savoring.
14–18s — Close-up of Cat, clearly on screen and still, staring at him, amber eyes unblinking. A beat of silence. Then the cat's mouth moves in sync, deep majestic male voice, completely deadpan:
   Cat: 「…어떤가, 젊은이.」
18–21s — AFTER the line, Cat slowly raises his right front paw and licks it calmly, eyes half-closed, utterly indifferent, then lowers it.
21–25s — Close-up of YoungMan frozen mid-chew, the dumpling still in the chopsticks. His eyes dart from the cat to the giant empty hall and back, and fear creeps in. He swallows hard (audible gulp) and gives two small, awkward nods. No words.
25–28s — He slowly turns his eyes up and to his right toward Granma. Medium close-up of Granma looking down at him, her sweet smile unchanged. Slow push-in on her face.
28–30s — Her eyes turn completely into Eyes: solid glossy pitch-black from lid to lid, with no iris and no white. Her smile slowly widens into a devilish closed-lip grin. Hold 1 second in silence. Cut to black.

NEGATIVE
No background music, score or stingers. No extra speaking voices: no narrator, no voiceover; YoungMan does not speak. No dialogue other than the two lines above. Cat licks his paw ONLY AFTER his line, never during or before it, and his mouth moves only on his line. Granma's and Cat's voices never merge. No other people, guests or waiters; no second cat. No text, subtitles, watermark or UI. Do not change the table set from Frame: no white tablecloth, no silver cutlery, no extra plates. No food other than the six dumplings. No glowing, red or white eyes; only Granma's eyes change, only in the last 2 seconds, and they become fully black like Eyes. The cat's eyes stay amber. No fangs, monster transformation or face distortion. No lighting change, interior flicker or daylight. No wardrobe drift; the helmet is never on his head. No floating objects or magic particles. No fast-forward look, shaky handheld or jump cuts.
