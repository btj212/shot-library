Prompt

Nano Banana Pro, one prompt written as JSON, and it hands back a two-frame 90s Japanese fashion editorial.

Nobody laid out those frames. The prompt just says two overlapping photographic frames at different zoom levels, and the model decides where the wide shot sits behind the close-up.

The rest of it is a wardrobe list. Rugby pullover with lime panels, khaki cargos, olive socks, orange cap, waffle cone. Every one of those lands in the render.
