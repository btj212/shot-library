Prompt

WHITEOUT EXPRESS — 30s CINEMATIC ACTION

FORMAT: Exactly 30 seconds, 16:9, 60fps where supported. Photorealistic Hollywood action thriller, realistic skin, frosted clothing, icy steel, cold blue-white daylight, drifting snow, natural motion blur.

HERO: image1 is the only hero. Preserve his exact face, skin tone, hair, beard, age, physique, black outfit and accessories in every shot. No morphing, duplicates or costume changes. Unarmed combat only.

LOCATION: A freight train races screen-right through a vast frozen desert. Locomotive ahead; boxcars and black tankers behind. Five enemies approach from screen-left, always from the rear. Never reverse direction.

ENEMIES: Scout with steel baton; massive Brute with fur collar and bare fists; Twin-Blade with two short blades; Chain Man with weighted chain; Leader in a long dark coat, arriving last. Distinct appearances and white-grey winter gear.

TIMELINE:
00–05s: Aerial train reveal. Low tracking shot as the hero sprints across the roof, glancing back while five enemies pursue.
05–07.5s: Scout attacks with baton. Hero ducks, traps his arm, elbows his ribs and sweeps him off the train.
07.5–10s: Brute throws a heavy punch. Hero kicks his thigh and ribs, but Brute grabs him and slams him onto the roof.
10–12.5s: Hero hooks Brute's leg, rolls him over an open roof hatch and pushes him off the train.
12.5–15s: ONLY slow-motion sequence. Hero leaps across the gap between cars, orbiting camera, suspended snow, then lands in a roll. Twin-Blade follows.
15–17.5s: Hero deflects both blades, slides beneath a low steel footbridge beam; Twin-Blade is swept off the train.
17.5–22.5s: Chain Man traps the hero's arm. Hero slips over the tanker edge, catches a handrail, kicks the attacker's knee, and uses the tangled chain to pull him off.
22.5–25s: Hero climbs back onto the icy tanker roof and faces the Leader ten meters ahead.
25–28s: Intense martial-arts duel. Hero traps the Leader's arm, lands a palm strike, spinning back kick and final elbow, knocking him off the roof.
28–30s: Hero stands alone, breathing heavily, frost on his clothes. Camera rises to a wide aerial as the train disappears into the snowy wilderness.

CAMERA & AUDIO: Dynamic aerials, 24mm tracking, 35mm combat orbits, 85mm close-ups and one Dutch angle. Original 150 BPM percussion, pulsing synth bass, bowed-metal drone, wind, rail clacks, locomotive rumble, chain rattles and realistic impacts. Each defeat hits a drum accent. Near-silence during the leap, explosive final impact, then fading wind.

CONTINUITY: Readable one-on-one fights, unique defeats, fallen enemies stay gone, consistent identities and geography, escalating tension, decisive hero victory.
