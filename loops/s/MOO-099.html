Prompt

30-second, photorealistic live-action horror short, cinematic supernatural thriller, realistic human characters, authentic skin texture, natural facial expressions, practical lighting, subtle handheld camera movement, dark atmospheric bedroom, muted earthy colors, cold blue moonlight mixed with a dim warm bedside lamp, realistic film grain. Consistent character throughout: a young woman in her mid-20s, shoulder-length dark brown hair, wearing a simple oversized white T-shirt and dark trousers. The mysterious man is tall and extremely pale, with messy dark hair, wearing an old black shirt, unsettling smile, and motionless posture. No text, no subtitles, no lip-sync.

[0–4s] Close-up of her browsing through old objects at a flea market. She notices a dusty vintage film camera sitting on a table and picks it up.

[4–8s] Nighttime. She stands in her bedroom, examining the old camera. She raises it and takes a photograph of herself. A bright camera flash briefly illuminates the entire room.

[8–13s] Close-up of a photograph slowly developing in her hands. The image reveals her standing in her bedroom, but a strange pale man is clearly visible directly behind her. Sarah's smile disappears.

[13–17s] She quickly turns around, scanning the empty bedroom. The camera follows her movement, revealing nobody behind her. Only the curtains move slightly in the cold breeze.

[17–22s] Nervously, She raises the camera again and takes another photograph toward her bedroom. A sudden flash fills the screen.

[22–26s] Close-up of the second photograph developing. The same strange man is now standing beside her bed, much closer to the camera, staring directly forward.

[26–30s] Extreme close-up of the photograph. The man's expression slowly reveals an unnatural, wide smile. Cut to her horrified face. In the background, the bedroom appears empty. Final shot: the camera flash suddenly goes off by itself. Cut to black.

Maintain strict character and location consistency, realistic physics, believable camera handling, natural movement and suspenseful pacing. The mysterious man must look photorealistic, not like a CGI monster. Make the photographs visually clear and recognizable as physical printed photographs.
