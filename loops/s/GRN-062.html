Prompt

AN ORDINARY DAY IN 2003

Duration: 30 seconds
Aspect ratio: 16:9
Style: Authentic early-2000s MiniDV home video.

MASTER IDENTITY INSTRUCTIONS

Use the uploaded Rosie storyboard as the visual reference for the character and scenes.

Rosie must remain the exact same woman in every scene:

- Same facial identity and facial proportions.
- Same natural freckles.
- Same long, dark, wavy hair and wispy bangs.
- Same skin tone, eyes and facial features.
- Same body proportions.
- Same cream floral puff-sleeve dress.
- Same gold hoop earrings and pendant necklace.

Do not redesign, recast or beautify Rosie between scenes.

IMPORTANT: The storyboard is a sequence reference. Do NOT reproduce the 3×3 grid in the output. Generate a continuous video with individual full-frame scenes, following the storyboard's order.

SCENE-BY-SCENE DIRECTION

00:00–00:03 | The Garden

Rosie stands outside her house in the morning. She casually looks towards the camera, then turns towards the flowers. A gentle breeze moves her hair. The camera operator adjusts framing imperfectly.

00:03–00:06 | Pink Flowers

Rosie bends towards a cluster of pink flowers, gently smells them and smiles. Her hair falls naturally over her shoulder. The camera moves closer, slightly losing focus.

00:06–00:09 | Making Breakfast

Inside the modest kitchen, Rosie stirs something in a small cooking pot. She briefly looks towards the camera while continuing to cook.

00:09–00:12 | Morning Coffee

Rosie holds a floral ceramic mug with both hands, takes a sip and looks thoughtfully out of the kitchen window.

00:12–00:15 | Phone Call

Rosie sits comfortably on an old sofa, holding a cream-coloured corded landline telephone. She listens, smiles and suddenly laughs.

00:15–00:19 | Neighbourhood Walk

Rosie walks along a quiet residential sidewalk carrying her woven shoulder bag. She turns her head towards the camera while walking. The operator follows her casually.

00:19–00:22 | Hanging Laundry

In the backyard, Rosie reaches up to hang a white bedsheet on the clothesline. Wind gently moves the fabric and her hair.

00:22–00:26 | The Cat

Rosie crouches near a fluffy cream-coloured cat outside her house. She gently pets it, laughs and looks down affectionately.

00:26–00:30 | Sunset

A close handheld shot of Rosie sitting outside during sunset. The sky is pink and orange. She looks over her shoulder towards the camera, gives a small genuine smile and waves.

The recording abruptly cuts to black.

CAMERA AND IMAGE QUALITY

Authentic early-2000s consumer MiniDV camcorder footage.

- Extremely raw handheld recording.
- Organic camera shake and micro-movements.
- Imperfect framing and accidental cropping.
- Natural autofocus hunting.
- Slight exposure fluctuations.
- Occasional motion blur.
- Faded, slightly desaturated colours.
- Soft highlights and faded blacks.
- Subtle digital noise and compression artifacts.
- Natural changes in lighting throughout the day.
- Occasional delayed camera reactions.

The footage should feel like a friend casually documenting Rosie's ordinary day, not a professional film crew.

AUDIO

No soundtrack. No background music.

Only natural environmental sounds:

- Birds in the garden.
- Gentle wind and leaves.
- Kitchen utensils and cooking.
- Coffee mug touching the table.
- Telephone conversation and laughter.
- Footsteps on the sidewalk.
- Laundry moving in the breeze.
- Cat purring.
- Evening crickets.

STRICT NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS

No identity changes.
No face morphing.
No hairstyle changes.
No outfit changes.
No extra people appearing.
No artificial facial expressions.
No model posing.
No fashion-commercial aesthetic.
No cinematic glamour.
No professional camera movement.
No modern colour grading.
No artificial transitions.
No split screens or storyboard grids.
No background music.

FINAL FEELING: A precious forgotten home video from April 2003. An ordinary day, a familiar neighbourhood, and Rosie being herself.
