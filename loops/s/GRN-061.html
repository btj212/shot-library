Seedance 2.5 "LOST FILMS #09 — Orbit (1978)": a never-released Soviet sci-fi reel with practical models, fixed geography and a no-Cyrillic rule

The creator's "Lost Films" series fakes a film that was never released, and this one is the clearest template for a period look. It is written in lower case as a production document: the format line first ("1978 soviet science fiction film … practical model effects"), then two characters "fixed for the whole film", fixed geography (the hatch with the porthole "right next to it, on its right"), eight timed static cuts, a score that "stops completely at 16s", and a style block ("shot on 35mm soviet svema color negative film, 1.66:1 frame, soft low contrast, muted colors with magenta and teal"). The constraints close it: "real-time speed, no slow motion, weightless floating only, no readable text … no cyrillic, no flags, no emblems", "the hatch stays closed from 8s to the end", and no morphing gloves. The output is a model-shot station, a tethered EVA, and a mustached colleague reading inside a porthole.

Prompt

1978 soviet science fiction film, open space near an orbital station,
sequence of cuts, practical model effects.

characters, fixed for the whole film:
the cosmonaut: around thirty-five, bulky white retro spacesuit with ribbed
accordion joints, round helmet with a clear visor, life-support backpack, a
safety tether. no lettering, no flags, no emblems on the suit.
the colleague: around forty-five, bushy mustache, brown knitted turtleneck
sweater, no spacesuit.

geography, fixed for the whole film: the cosmonaut is always outside the
station, the colleague is always inside. on the side of the station module
there is one round hatch with a wheel handle, and right next to it, on its
right, one round porthole window. the hatch stays closed after the cosmonaut
goes out.

0-4s: wide static shot of a model space station slowly drifting against
black velvet space with tiny painted stars, a painted blue earth below.

4-8s: cut to medium static shot, the round hatch swings open, the cosmonaut
floats weightlessly out of it on his tether, solemn and heroic, then the
hatch closes behind him.

8-13s: cut to medium static shot, the cosmonaut tightens a bolt on a dish
antenna with a huge wrench, thin wires holding him are faintly visible.

13-16s: cut to close-up of the helmet visor, static, the earth reflected in
it, the cosmonaut nods proudly, the job is done.

16-19s: cut to medium static shot, he pulls himself along a handrail back to
the hatch and turns the wheel handle: it does not move.

19-22s: cut to close-up of his gloved hands, static, he tries the wheel again,
then knocks on the hatch with his fist.

22-26s: cut to shot through the round porthole from outside, static: inside
the cozy cabin, lit by warm tungsten lamps, the colleague floats calmly,
reading a book and sipping tea from a squeeze tube, a small cassette player
floating next to him playing music. he does not notice anything.

26-30s: cut to medium static shot outside, the cosmonaut presses his helmet to
the porthole and taps the glass with the wrench, the wrench slips from his
glove and drifts away into the stars. he slowly turns his helmet and watches
it go, completely still.

audio: 1970s soviet sci-fi score: dreamy analog synthesizer melody, slow
pulsing bass, a wordless vocoder choir, solemn and heroic, the music stops
completely at 16s. from 22s a muffled cheerful 1970s pop melody plays faintly
from the cassette player behind the glass. foley: the cosmonaut's breathing
inside the helmet, the hatch clunk, the wrench clicking, muffled knocks on
metal and glass. no dialogue.

style: shot on 35mm soviet svema color negative film, 1.66:1 frame, soft low
contrast, muted colors with magenta and teal shift, heavy grain, slight gate
weave, practical cardboard and plastic model station, stars painted on black
velvet, faintly visible support wires, chunky toggle switches and round
dials inside the cabin, cold blue backlight outside, warm tungsten light
inside.

constraints: real-time speed, no slow motion, weightless floating only, no
readable text, no letters, no cyrillic, no flags, no emblems, no logos, the
hatch stays closed from 8s to the end, preserve both faces and wardrobe across
all cuts, no morphing hands, no morphing gloves.
