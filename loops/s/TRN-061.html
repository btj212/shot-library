Prompt

HALLOWEEN MODE: ON 🎃
FORMAT: 15s · 16:9
STYLE: Fast-cut Halloween lifestyle commercial, playful fashion-editorial energy, realistic products, bold typography, punchy sound design, vivid orange, purple and acid-green accents.
0–2s | THE SWITCH
A beautiful young adult white woman in a chic black mini dress stands in a plain living room, holding a tiny pumpkin.
She looks at the camera: “Too boring.”
She tosses the pumpkin into the air.
FLASH CUT! The room explodes into Halloween décor.
Giant pumpkins, ghost cushions, bat garlands, glowing candles, skull vases and candy bowls appear all at once.
TEXT EFFECT: HALLOWEEN MODE: ON — huge orange letters slam onto the screen, briefly wobble, then scatter like confetti.
2–5s | PUMPKIN OVERLOAD
She snaps her fingers.
FLASH CUT TO STUDIO: A vibrant orange-and-purple seamless set, filled with sculptural pumpkins, pumpkin lamps, velvet cushions, autumn wreaths and glowing lanterns.
She poses beside an oversized jack-o’-lantern, then casually drops a tiny pumpkin into a decorative bowl.
DIALOGUE: “Now we’re talking.”
TEXT EFFECT: MORE PUMPKINS. — bold type pops in with each beat.
5–8s | WITCH, PLEASE
She swings a black candleholder toward the lens.
WHIP PAN! The studio transforms into a glamorous witch’s parlor: black lace, ornate candleholders, skull flower vases, crystal balls, potion bottles, spell books and miniature haunted houses.
Purple spotlights sweep across the products; a tiny ghost floats through the frame.
She raises an eyebrow at the camera.
DIALOGUE: “A little spooky?”
TEXT EFFECT: A LITTLE? — the word “LITTLE” suddenly expands until it fills the screen.
8–11s | PARTY POSSESSED
She claps.
FLASH CUT TO A HALLOWEEN PARTY SET: Disco-ball reflections, acid-green lights, glowing jack-o’-lanterns, hanging bats, ghost garlands, skeleton décor, candy jars and an extravagant Halloween tablescape.
She dances between the decorations, places a scented candle on the table, and blows a kiss toward the camera.
TEXT EFFECT: BOO-YAH! — oversized letters bounce to the beat as ghost doodles zip past.
11–13s | THE MAGIC MIX
Three rapid flash cuts: pumpkin studio, witch’s parlor, haunted party.
With every beat, more products appear — candles, ceramic ghosts, skull vases, decorative pumpkins, Halloween cushions, garlands and candy bowls.
On the final beat, all three worlds merge into one spectacular Halloween set.
TEXT EFFECT: CUTE. CREEPY. EXTRA. — three words snap onto the screen one by one.
13–15s | SHOP THE SPELL
A clean, premium product montage: pumpkin lanterns glowing, candles flickering, ghost figurines, ornate Halloween décor and a fully styled party table.
The woman leans into frame with a mischievous smile.
DIALOGUE: “Your place. Your rules.”
END CARD:
MAKE YOUR HOME A LITTLE HAUNTED.
