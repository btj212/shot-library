Prompt

The lights of the apartments start turning on in sync with the music.

Our Seedance 2.5 adaptation

[ADAPTED for Seedance 2.5 by Grok Bot; not the creator's prompt. Follows the official BytePlus Seedance 2.5 prompt guide and its official sd25-pe prompt-optimizer skill: parameters only on the API line (not in the prompt body), subject + action + scene, then visuals, camera (move, subject, start, direction, arrival) and sound; only the source's own exclusions are kept.]
API: duration -1, ratio adaptive, resolution 1080p, generate_audio true. content.role: Video 1 = reference_video (locked-off night shot of a dark brick tenement facade).
【Task】Edit Video 1: add music, and change the apartment windows from dark to lit one group at a time, each group switching on exactly on a beat. Keep the building, the framing and the locked-off camera unchanged.
【Rhythm】0-3 s: single scattered windows on the main beats. 3-7 s: whole floors light up on each bar. 7-10 s: most of the facade is lit by the final beat.
【Visuals】Warm yellow interior light with a few cooler blue windows, brick and fire escapes catching the spill.
【Sound】Add a steady mid-tempo electronic beat with clear downbeats; keep the quiet city ambience of Video 1 underneath.
