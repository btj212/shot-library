Prompt

A RAINY WEEKEND AT HOME | KOREA, 1998 🌧️

Create a 30-second ultra-realistic live-action home vlog set in a traditional Korean family house in 1998. Make it look like authentic Sony MiniDV family footage.

CHARACTERS:

- Main girl: 21-year-old Korean woman with shoulder-length dark brown hair and bangs, wearing a cream knitted sweater and brown trousers.
- Mother: Short dark hair, floral apron.
- Father: Short black hair, grey sweater and dark trousers.

Keep faces, hairstyles and outfits consistent.

EXACT 8-SCENE SEQUENCE:

1. RAINY MORNING (0–4s): The girl sits beside a rainy window, holding warm tea and watching raindrops fall over the quiet Korean neighbourhood.

2. MAKING PANCAKES (4–8s): In the traditional kitchen, she mixes dough, adds brown sugar and cinnamon filling, and cooks golden homemade hotteok while her mother smiles nearby.

3. BREAKFAST (8–11s): She enjoys a freshly cooked pancake and tea beside the rainy window.

4. CLEANING (11–15s): She folds blankets, arranges cushions, wipes the wooden table and sweeps the floor while her mother folds laundry.

5. OLD CASSETTE TAPES (15–19s): She discovers handwritten cassette tapes in an old drawer, inserts one into a vintage cassette player and listens with a nostalgic smile.

6. FAMILY TEA TIME (19–22s): Her father joins with a newspaper. The girl serves tea, and all three share smiles and laughter.

7. PREPARING DINNER (22–26s): Mother prepares Korean rice, kimchi, doenjang-jjigae and grilled fish. The daughter helps arrange everything on the low dining table.

8. COZY EVENING (26–30s): The family enjoys dinner together on floor cushions. They chat and laugh naturally. The girl smiles at the camcorder. End with rain falling outside the window.

VISUAL STYLE:
Photorealistic live-action, authentic 1998 Sony MiniDV footage, soft resolution, subtle tape noise, faded warm colours, handheld camera shake, imperfect framing and natural exposure changes.

AUDIO: Rain, cooking sounds, cassette player clicks, family conversations, laughter and dinner ambience. No background music, narration or subtitles.

STRICT CONTINUITY: Same characters, outfits, house and objects throughout. Pancakes before breakfast, cleaning after breakfast, cassette listening before dinner. Rain continues from morning to evening.

No modern phones, smart TVs, teleportation, duplicated characters, outfit changes, distorted hands or artificial CGI.

FINAL GOAL: A peaceful, cozy and nostalgic family memory that feels like a genuine rainy weekend recording from Korea in 1998. Include all 8 scenes in exact order within 30 seconds.
