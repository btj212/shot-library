Prompt

Create a finished 30-second realistic social-media UGC advertisement for a fictional AI camera app called LENSO.

The entire video must look like an authentic creator-made smartphone advertisement that could realistically be posted on TikTok, Instagram Reels, or X.

It must NOT look like a polished traditional commercial, movie, music video, CGI demonstration, or AI-generated video.

MAIN CREATOR:
A fictional attractive Korean woman in her mid-to-late 20s.
Naturally beautiful, realistic Korean facial features, healthy natural skin texture, expressive dark brown eyes, dark medium-length straight hair, subtle natural makeup, neat eyebrows, natural lips, warm and confident personality.

She should look attractive and stylish without looking artificially perfect or resembling any real celebrity or identifiable person.

WARDROBE:
Bold contemporary Korean fashion styling:
a fitted black leather-look cropped jacket over a rich burgundy fitted top, high-waisted charcoal wide-leg trousers, tasteful silver earrings and a minimal necklace.
Modern, confident, fashionable and eye-catching, but completely appropriate for a lifestyle creator.
The outfit should look like something a real Korean fashion/tech creator would wear.

SETTING:
A beautiful but believable modern apartment in Seoul-style urban surroundings.
Natural daylight coming through a large window.
Real furniture, books, plants, coffee cup and ordinary lived-in details.
No luxury mansion.
No futuristic interior.
No visible brands or logos.

CAMERA STYLE:
The creator is filming herself primarily with a realistic modern smartphone.

Natural handheld smartphone framing.
Small natural hand movements.
Subtle autofocus adjustments.
Realistic exposure changes.
Natural smartphone dynamic range.
Occasional tiny framing imperfections.
Natural facial expressions and blinking.
Natural hair movement.
Realistic skin texture.

Do NOT make the footage excessively stabilized or cinematic.
Do NOT use impossible camera movements.
Do NOT make it look like a professional film crew shot it.

The result must feel like a real person spontaneously recording a recommendation for her followers.

PRODUCT:
LENSO is a fictional AI camera app that transforms ordinary smartphone footage into polished cinematic-looking footage while keeping the original subject and environment realistic.

No recognizable existing app interface.
No Apple, Samsung, Google, Instagram, TikTok or other real brand references.
Do not generate complex readable UI text.
The phone screen can show a simple believable generic camera interface with a single obvious LENSO capture control.

STORY:

0–5 SECONDS — HOOK

The creator holds her smartphone in selfie mode and speaks naturally directly to the camera.

She gives a small surprised smile.

DIALOGUE:
"Okay, I didn't expect this to actually work."

Natural conversational delivery.
No exaggerated influencer acting.

She quickly turns the phone toward herself and the apartment.

5–10 SECONDS — DEMONSTRATION

She says:

"I've been testing LENSO on completely normal phone footage."

She records herself walking a few steps toward the window.

The footage initially looks like ordinary smartphone video.

Natural room ambience.

10–16 SECONDS — RESULT

She taps the fictional LENSO capture control.

Show a believable visual transition into the processed result.

The exact same woman, clothing, apartment and lighting remain consistent.

The footage becomes noticeably more cinematic through:
better natural-looking exposure,
subtle depth separation,
cleaner motion,
beautiful but realistic color,
natural highlight rolloff,
and more polished camera movement.

Do NOT change her face, clothing, apartment layout or identity.

The transformation must look like realistic computational photography, not magic.

She reacts naturally:

"Look at that."

16–22 SECONDS — SECOND TEST

She turns the phone toward herself again and walks toward the apartment window.

She records a second short shot.

The result shows her standing near the window with natural daylight, realistic hair movement and subtle cinematic depth.

She looks genuinely impressed rather than overly excited.

DIALOGUE:
"And that's literally just my phone."

22–26 SECONDS — CREATOR REACTION

Return to authentic handheld selfie framing.

She looks directly into the camera.

Natural smile.

DIALOGUE:
"If you're making content, you need to try this."

Her delivery should sound spontaneous, friendly and believable.

26–30 SECONDS — SIMPLE CTA

She raises the phone slightly toward camera.

Natural close-up.

She says:

"LENSO. Make your everyday footage look cinematic."

End naturally with her smiling and lowering the phone.

Do NOT create a giant advertising end card.

Do NOT use floating graphics.

Do NOT use holograms.

Do NOT use futuristic effects.

AUDIO:
Clear natural female Korean-English-accented conversational voice speaking fluent natural English.
The voice must belong to a completely fictional person and must not imitate any real person or celebrity.

Natural apartment ambience.
Very subtle original background music beginning around 5 seconds.
Soft, modern, social-media-friendly music that remains underneath the dialogue.
Natural phone handling sounds.
Subtle room tone.
No copyrighted or recognizable music.

DIALOGUE MUST BE SYNCHRONIZED:
The woman's lips must naturally match every spoken word.
No overlapping dialogue.
No duplicated words.
No missing words.
No unnatural pauses.
No robotic voice.
No exaggerated facial expressions.

CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS:
Keep the same woman throughout.
Keep her face, hairstyle, outfit, body proportions and accessories consistent.
Keep the same apartment layout throughout.
Keep the same smartphone throughout.
Keep LENSO branding consistent.
The phone must remain physically believable in her hand.
Hands and fingers must remain anatomically correct.
No extra fingers.
No warped hands.
No duplicated objects.
No disappearing objects.
No changing clothing.
No changing hairstyle.
No sudden background changes.
No inconsistent lighting.
No distorted phone.
No unreadable fake text dominating the screen.

IMPORTANT:
This is a UGC advertisement, not a cinematic commercial.

The strongest feeling should be:
"She genuinely discovered a useful product and is showing it to me."

Keep the performance natural, conversational and slightly imperfect while maintaining professional visual quality.

The final 30-second video must feel completely believable as authentic creator content and be ready to publish directly without external editing.
