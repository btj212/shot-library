Prompt

Image prompt: experimental editorial artwork: one frontal close-up portrait of a young black man with short textured hair, distinctive features and a neutral, confident expression. divide the continuous face into an exact modular grid of equal squares with extremely thin black borders. align every facial feature across the grid; do not combine different faces. assign each square a distinct treatment: x-ray bones, thermal heat map, fluorescent risograph and grain, photographic negative, infrared, 3d wireframe, rgb separation, black-and-white photocopy, posterized red/cobalt/magenta, analog scan distortion or natural photographic skin. a few squares may stay white for negative space. swiss modernist structure, bold color contrasts, experimental fashion editorial and mixed-media textures. no text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere.

Video prompt: animate the provided portrait into an intense scream: mouth opens wide, teeth and tongue appear, eyes narrow, eyebrows tense and facial muscles contract naturally. treat the grid as a fixed screen mask. every panel must retain its exact screen position, size, border and assigned filter throughout. animate one synchronized face beneath all panels; features move through the stationary filter regions. filters must not follow the face, switch treatments or shift position. keep x-ray, thermal, wireframe, risograph, negative, halftone, rgb and photographic regions recognizable. locked frontal close-up with realistic skin and facial motion. one uninterrupted shot; no cuts, transitions, zoom, reframing or bending grid lines.
