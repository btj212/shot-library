Prompt

Cinematic 16:9 live-action Hollywood fantasy action short film, 30 seconds, 30fps. Shot like a scene from a big-budget fantasy blockbuster: real actress, real mountain gorge location, photorealistic CGI dragons fully integrated into the plate. Shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with anamorphic lenses, epic scale, natural skin texture, real fabric and hair in strong wind, physically accurate fire, rock debris, mist and water spray. Late-afternoon golden sunlight cutting through mist, filmic color grade, subtle grain, natural motion blur. Intense, thrilling, then emotional and heartwarming. No cartoon, no animation, no Pixar or 3D-render look. No gore.  CONTINUITY LOCK (MANDATORY): - Screen direction never flips: the chase always moves from LEFT to RIGHT of frame. The giant dragon is always BEHIND (left of) and ABOVE her dragon, never in front. - No teleporting. Every change of position is shown on screen in continuous motion. No character can appear somewhere they were not seen moving to. - Fewer, longer shots. The camera follows the action with smooth motivated moves: tracking, drone follow, slow push-in. No random angle jumps. - She is always either on the bridge, falling, or seated on her dragon's back holding his neck spikes, and the transitions between these are shown fully. - Identical faces, outfit and dragon designs in every frame.  Characters: 1) A real young woman in her mid-20s with messy wavy blonde hair, thin round wire glasses, a cream linen blouse with rolled sleeves, a dark brown pinafore apron dress, and a small leather satchel full of herbs. Natural freckles, realistic skin, expressive brave face, believable acting. 2) HER DRAGON: a horse-sized real living dragon. Thick, textured white-cream scales with pink-magenta markings, a crest of soft pink flexible spikes, wide cream wings with pink-tinted membranes, wet glossy amber eyes with vertical pupils. Agile, fast, loyal, with real weight and mass. 3) THE GIANT: a wild dragon five times bigger than hers. Charcoal-black scales, jagged obsidian horns, tattered leathery wings, burning ember-orange eyes, smoke leaking between its teeth. Heavy, slow to turn, terrifying. Each keeps an identical design and scale in every shot.  Setting: A deep misty mountain gorge with a long old rope-and-plank bridge spanning it, a river far below, waterfalls on the cliffs, pine trees on the edges. Further along the gorge to the RIGHT, a narrow natural stone arch spans the canyon, just wide enough for her dragon.  0-3s (HOOK): Wide drone shot. She is halfway across the swaying rope bridge, herb satchel over her shoulder. A colossal black shadow rises out of the mist below the bridge. She freezes and looks down. The giant dragon's burning eyes open in the mist directly beneath her. 3-6s: The giant erupts upward through the mist and smashes through the middle of the bridge behind her. Planks explode into the air, ropes snap. Medium shot: the bridge splits and her half drops away under her feet. She grabs a rope with one hand, swinging, legs dangling over the drop. Her satchel falls; herbs scatter into the wind. 6-9s: Close-up on her hand slipping down the frayed rope, knuckles white. Far away on the RIGHT cliff edge, her dragon's head snaps toward the sound, eyes wide. He launches off the cliff, wings snapping open, diving toward her. 9-13s: The rope tears. She falls. One continuous falling drone shot: she tumbles down through mist and flying planks, hair and dress whipping. Her dragon dives into frame from the upper RIGHT, wings tucked, faster than her, passes underneath her, rolls, and she lands across his back. She scrambles to grab his neck spikes as his wings slam open and he pulls out of the dive just above the river, spray exploding off the water. 13-16s: Low tracking shot following them LEFT to RIGHT along the river at high speed. She clings to his neck, glasses crooked. Behind them on the LEFT and above, the giant dives after them, roaring, its huge wingbeats whipping the river into spray. 16-20s: The giant unleashes a massive blast of fire. Her dragon banks hard to the right and the fire scorches the cliff wall beside them, rock cracking and falling into the river. He weaves between falling boulders. Close-up: she looks back over her shoulder, terrified, then forward, and sees the narrow stone arch ahead. 20-23s: Wide shot. Her dragon folds his wings tight and shoots straight through the narrow stone arch with inches to spare, her hair brushing the rock. The giant, too big, slams its wings out and pulls up hard at the last second, its claws scraping the arch, chunks of stone falling. It roars in frustration, hovering, unable to follow. 23-26s: They burst out the other side of the arch into a wide sunlit valley and glide down toward a soft green meadow. Her dragon lands in a skidding run through the tall grass. Behind them, far away above the arch, the giant turns and flies away into the clouds. 26-30s: She slides down from his back and her legs give way; she sits in the grass, shaking, then laughing with relief. Her dragon lowers his big head to her, breathing hard. She throws her arms around his snout and presses her forehead to his, eyes closed. He purrs and gently curls his wing around her. Slow pull back to a wide golden shot: the two of them small in the meadow, the stone arch and the gorge behind them. Hold.  Style: Photorealistic Hollywood fantasy action blockbuster. Huge scale contrast between the two dragons, real wind and mist, real fire light on rock and water, golden sunlight through mist, cold misty blue in the gorge warming to golden in the meadow. No morphing, no extra limbs, no warped hands, stable glasses and face.  Audio: Quiet wind and creaking bridge ropes at the start, then a deep rumble and an earth-shaking roar. Splintering wood and snapping ropes, her scream, rushing wind during the fall, a heavy wing-boom on the catch, water spray, a huge fire blast, cracking rock. An epic orchestral score with drums and choir building through the chase, then a sudden release as they pass through the arch into soaring emotional strings. Her breathless laughter and her dragon's deep purr at the end.
