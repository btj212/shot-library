Prompt

Edit @Video1. Change the camera only: a full-body wide shot — show the subject from head to toe, with enough surrounding space to contain their entire original movement path and final pose.

Camera position: on a stationary tripod at natural standing eye level, facing the performance area straight-on. Choose a distance and fixed field of view that keep the subject’s complete body visible throughout their existing blocking.

A completely locked-off camera — no tracking, panning, tilting, zooming, reframing, or stabilization drift. The subject moves freely within the fixed composition and may change apparent size as they approach or move away. Do not alter their path to keep them centered.

Apply ONE single camera setup to the ENTIRE video: every shot and every cut must be reshot from this exact same fixed camera location, with the same lens, orientation, and field of view — one uniform camera across all shots. Every shot must visibly adopt this new camera setup. Do not retain the original framing.

Do not change: the subject’s performance, timing, expressions and blocking, their wardrobe and identity, the environment, the lighting, the original visual texture, or the original audio. Everything about the scene stays exactly as shot — only the camera is different.
