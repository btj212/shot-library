Prompt

One continuous 15-second cinematic shot, no cuts. A lone warrior in a dark cloak stands on a snowy cliff edge at dawn, wind whipping his cape. The camera slowly pushes past his shoulder toward a vast frozen valley. Suddenly the ice below cracks, and a colossal ancient dragon rises from the mountain, shaking off snow and rocks, wings unfolding across the sky. The camera tilts up as the dragon roars, golden sunlight breaking through the clouds behind it. Epic scale, volumetric light, drifting snow, film grain, anamorphic lens, ultra-realistic, blockbuster movie look.
