Prompt

Create a premium Hollywood-style live-action automotive commercial using realistic 35mm cinematography, natural lighting, organic grain, accurate reflections, dynamic tracking shots, and seamless transitions. Real-time speed only. No music.

CONTINUITY: Same silver-white 4.5m Roadster with “RDSTR” plate, identical woman and racer, and consistent locations. The racer wears a plain white-and-silver suit. The woman always drives from the left seat using the same yoke steering control. Every flight or hover uses exactly four thin white vapour jets pointing downward, without flames or glow. Preserve realistic physics, scale, shadows, and reflections.

SCENE 1 — INVITATION (0–7s): In an old stone courtyard, a low tracking shot reveals the Roadster. The man rests his hand on the roof: “Try this Roadster.” She replies, “It looks amazing!” She enters the left driver's seat, admires the yoke, and starts the car.

SCENE 2 — GETAWAY (7–8s): The Roadster accelerates past the camera on a leafy street with realistic tyre noise, rushing air, and brief tyre haze.

SCENE 3 — DESERT FLIGHT (8–12s): About 12 camels react to the approaching car; one groans. Four downward jets lift it above the dusty highway. It completes exactly one nose-to-tail barrel roll above the camels, returns upright, lands softly, and accelerates away.

SCENE 4 — LEAF MAGIC (12–15s): In an autumn suburb, the Roadster hovers beside a street sweeper in an orange safety vest. Four jets scatter dry leaves into a swirling cloud, revealing a clean sidewalk. The sweeper laughs; the woman smiles.

SCENE 5 — APPLE PICKING (15–17s): In a green orchard, the car hovers beside an apple tree. The woman reaches through the open window, picks a red apple, and smiles as the branch springs back.

SCENE 6 — THE DRIVER (17–18s): Interior side profile of the smiling woman driving one-handed with the yoke. Warm sunlight crosses her face, transitioning into sunset.

SCENE 7 — SKYBOUND (18–28s): On a runway, the Roadster accelerates beside an unbranded white private jet. Four jets activate as the car lifts through dust. Both climb toward golden clouds, but the Roadster pulls ahead. It passes through a dense cloud bank, emerges above glowing clouds, and flies among six to eight naturally moving white geese. A goose passes the open window as the woman smiles and waves. Finally, the Roadster tilts nose-down and begins descending through golden clouds as the camera pulls back to a vast cinematic sky. No text or logo.

SOUND: Natural dialogue, footsteps, door thunk, electric hum, tyre noise, wind, camel groan, vapour hiss, sand, landing, leaves, laughter, birdsong, apple snap, jet roar, and goose calls. No music.

NEGATIVE: No cartoon or artificial CGI appearance, flames, explosions, glowing exhaust, incorrect jets, changing car or plate, duplicate characters, wrong steering position, round steering wheel, logos, malformed anatomy, frozen animals, teleportation, blended scenes, extra rolls, flips, slow motion, captions, watermarks, or text.

FINAL DIRECTIVE: Exactly seven scenes, 28 seconds, original order, all essential actions preserved, consistent assets, realistic physics, natural dialogue, and seamless live-action transitions. Do not omit, repeat, or extend scenes.
