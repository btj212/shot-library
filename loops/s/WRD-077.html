Prompt

Create a 30-second, 1080p ultra-realistic cinematic commercial for a fictional premium travel luggage brand called VELA.
A stylish young Korean woman with long, sleek dark hair and natural glowing makeup travels through a modern international train station carrying one premium VELA hard-shell suitcase.
She wears a bold contemporary fashion look: a structured deep burgundy leather jacket worn over a fitted black asymmetric top, high-waisted wide-leg charcoal trousers with a dramatic tailored silhouette, pointed black ankle boots, sculptural silver earrings and a sleek black shoulder bag. The styling feels like a high-fashion Korean editorial while remaining realistic and wearable. Maintain the exact same woman, outfit and suitcase design throughout.
The commercial opens with a low cinematic tracking shot of the VELA suitcase rolling smoothly beside her as she walks confidently through the busy station. Her bold outfit immediately establishes a strong fashion presence.
The station feels completely real—natural travelers, luggage, footsteps, announcements, polished floors and authentic ambient movement.
She changes direction through a crowded passage, effortlessly pulling the suitcase beside her. Show a detailed close-up of the quiet multi-directional wheels moving smoothly across the floor.
She stops briefly near a seating area, places the suitcase upright and opens it. Inside, her clothing is neatly organized. She removes a folded piece of clothing, closes the suitcase and continues walking.
She approaches an escalator and pulls the suitcase naturally behind her.
Cut to a low tracking shot following the wheels as she moves toward a waiting modern train.
She reaches the train, extends the suitcase handle, lifts the luggage naturally into the storage area and secures it.
Show refined macro shots of the wheels, handle mechanism, textured shell, zipper and premium hardware.
Inside the train, she sits beside the window with the VELA suitcase beside her. The train begins moving through the city.
She looks toward the passing skyline, then down at her suitcase with a subtle confident smile.
End with a cinematic hero shot of the same VELA suitcase, with the woman softly out of focus behind it.
Voiceover
“Travel doesn't always go as planned.
That's why we designed for everything in between.
Smooth when you move. Strong when you need it.
Made to keep up with you.
VELA. Ready for the journey.”
Style: Ultra-photorealistic premium fashion-travel campaign, cinematic live-action, realistic Korean woman, bold contemporary styling, natural skin and hair, authentic human movement, physically accurate luggage physics, premium materials, realistic reflections, sophisticated lighting, smooth tracking shots, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, luxury editorial cinematography.
Negative: No outfit changes, no identity drift, no suitcase morphing, no changing luggage design, no duplicated people or luggage, no impossible physics, no floating objects, no CGI-looking environment, no futuristic effects, no holograms, no excessive slow motion, no distorted anatomy, no subtitles, no captions, no random text, no watermark.
