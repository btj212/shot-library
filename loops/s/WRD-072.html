Prompt

Create a 15-second ultra-realistic 16:9 landscape video featuring the same naturally attractive 24-year-old Korean woman with long loose dark hair, minimal makeup, an oversized maroon hoodie, and loose casual pants. Preserve her exact facial identity, hairstyle, and body proportions throughout.

Setting: A cozy apartment on a chilly winter morning. Soft natural daylight enters through the window. Authentic early-2000s Sony MiniDV home-video aesthetic, filmed by another person, with slight handheld shake, imperfect framing, subtle autofocus adjustments, faded colors, and natural lighting.

0–3 seconds: She stands near the apartment window, rubbing her hands together. She suddenly shivers, pulls her shoulders inward, and wraps her arms around herself. Her expression suggests that the room is much colder than expected.

3–6 seconds: She looks toward a jacket hanging on a nearby coat rack. She walks over, still slightly shivering, reaches for it, and pulls it off the hanger.

6–10 seconds: She puts the jacket on over her maroon hoodie, slips one arm into a sleeve, then struggles briefly to get the other arm through. She wiggles her shoulder to settle it properly and gives a small amused smile.

10–13 seconds: She pulls the jacket closed, adjusts the collar, and rubs her hands together inside its warm sleeves. Her body relaxes as she finally feels comfortable.

13–15 seconds: She looks toward the camera with a satisfied smile, gives a tiny playful nod, and walks away feeling warmer.

Motion must be smooth, natural, and physically accurate. Realistic shivering, fabric movement, sleeve interactions, and natural walking. Maintain consistent character identity and clothing. No stuttering, judder, duplicated frames, distorted hands, or sudden transitions.

Audio: Quiet indoor ambience, faint wind outside, subtle footsteps, jacket fabric rustling, and natural clothing sounds. No music or narration.
