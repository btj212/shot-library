WHIP PAN 🎥

A whip pan is a rapid camera rotation on a horizontal axis that creates a deliberate motion blur.

Cinematographers use it very concisely to:
• Convey rapid passage of time or a change of location within a single, continuous motion.
• Connect two distinct actions or characters in different parts of a room, establishing a fast-paced spatial relationship.
• Inject energy and urgency into a scene, often used to mirror a character's sudden shock or distraction.
• Hide a hidden cut by using the heavy motion blur to seamlessly transition between two entirely different shots.
