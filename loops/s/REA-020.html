Create a 30-second one-take ultra-realistic UGC video filmed on an Apple iPhone front camera. A woman in a beige sweater slices a highly realistic cake shaped like a cute cat in a bright modern kitchen, while the real fluffy cat sits in the background. Natural smartphone exposure, slight handheld movement, realistic depth of field, casual everyday UGC feel. No image reference, no text, subtitles, logos, or watermark.

[0–6s]

[6–12s]
As the knife cuts into the cake, the cat suddenly jumps onto its hind legs, opens its mouth wide, and reacts as if screaming in exaggerated horror. Its facial expressions and movements become comically dramatic while keeping the cat photorealistic.

[12–18s]
The woman immediately stops cutting, looks at the cat, and bursts out laughing. The cat continues its ridiculous reaction, waving its paws and staring at the cake as if desperately trying to stop her.

[18–24s]
She looks between the cake and the cat, laughing harder. The cat keeps dramatically “screaming” and bouncing on its hind legs, creating an absurdly funny reaction.

[24–30s]
The cat drops back onto all fours and stares suspiciously at the cake. The woman laughs toward the phone camera. Just before the video ends, the cat gives one final exaggerated scream and she completely loses it laughing.
