Prompt

Create a 30-second ultra-realistic live-action video featuring a naturally beautiful young Korean woman in her early 20s spending time with a friend in an older residential neighborhood in Korea on a warm late-summer afternoon.

The video should feel like genuine footage casually recorded on an early 2000s consumer digital camcorder. It should not look like a commercial, K-drama, music video, staged production, or cinematic film. Keep everything simple, candid, engaging, and natural.

**Character Consistency**

The main character has shoulder-length dark brown-black hair with soft, natural waves, subtle makeup, realistic skin texture, and a relaxed, expressive face.

She wears:
- A fitted white short-sleeve top.
- Light blue straight-leg jeans.
- White sneakers.
- A small dark brown shoulder bag.
- A delicate silver necklace.

Maintain perfect character consistency throughout the entire video. Her facial features, hairstyle, clothing, accessories, body proportions, and the same bottled drink must remain identical in every shot.

**Location and Environment**

A quiet, older Korean residential neighborhood featuring:
- Narrow concrete alleys and small side streets.
- Low-rise brick villa-style apartment buildings (빌라) and small apartment complexes.
- Brick and concrete walls, with potted plants and small rooftop or doorstep gardens.
- Parked bicycles.
- Utility poles with tangled overhead electrical and cable wires.
- A small neighborhood mini-mart or convenience store with an open front and a drink refrigerator.
- Shaded walkways, narrow stairways, and apartment entrances with metal gates.

The environment should feel lived-in, ordinary, and authentic. Avoid recognizable landmarks, identifiable brands, readable signage, advertisements, or commercial activity. Any signs or labels should be blurred, generic, or out of focus.

**Visual Style and Camera Direction**

Authentic handheld early 2000s consumer DV camcorder footage.

Use:
- Natural handheld camera shake.
- Imperfect framing and spontaneous camera movements.
- Occasional autofocus breathing and focus hunting.
- Slight automatic exposure fluctuations.
- Soft digital image detail.
- Faded, slightly washed-out colors.
- Subtle digital video noise.
- Natural motion blur.
- Occasional accidental zooming.

The person filming should behave like a friend casually recording everyday moments. The characters should react naturally to the camera.

Absolutely no gimbal shots, drone footage, stabilized camera movements, slow motion, beauty filters, or polished cinematic aesthetics.

**Storyboard and Timeline**

**00:00–00:05, Leaving the Apartment**
She walks out of her apartment building through a metal gate, adjusts her shoulder bag, notices the camera, and smiles naturally. Her friend casually follows behind while filming.

**00:05–00:10, Buying a Cold Drink**
She buys a bottled cold drink from a small neighborhood mini-mart. She gently presses the cold bottle against her cheek, laughs as the camera zooms in on her face, and casually says in Korean: "오늘 진짜 덥다." (It's so hot today.)

**00:10–00:15, Walking Through the Neighborhood**
The two friends walk along a quieter residential alley. A gentle breeze moves her hair. She turns back toward the camera, looks directly into the lens, and smiles naturally.

**00:15–00:20, A Playful Moment**
She sits on a low wall beside an apartment garden. Her friend notices a tiny leaf caught in her hair and points it out. She removes the leaf herself and bursts into natural laughter.

**00:20–00:25, Sharing a Drink**
They continue walking together. She briefly takes the bottled drink from her friend, takes a casual sip, hands it back, and smiles toward the camera.

**00:25–00:30, Saying Goodbye**
At the apartment entrance, she turns toward the camera, waves casually, and says in Korean: "내일 봐." (See you tomorrow.)

She walks inside the apartment building. Her friend continues recording for one additional second as the door closes. The footage then abruptly cuts to black.

**Audio and Sound Design**

Use only authentic environmental sounds recorded naturally within the scene:
- Footsteps on concrete.
- Distant passing traffic.
- Bicycle sounds.
- Summer cicadas (매미) and other insects.
- Ambient residential neighborhood sounds.
- Mini-mart refrigerator humming.
- Bottle handling and movement.
- Rustling leaves.
- Faint conversations in Korean and distant neighborhood chatter.

Include only natural character dialogue. No background music, voiceover, artificial sound effects, subtitles, captions, explanatory text, logos, watermarks, or random text.

**Realism and Physical Accuracy**

Every movement must feel spontaneous and physically believable.

Ensure realistic:
- Walking and sitting movements.
- Drinking and handling the bottle.
- Hair movement in the breeze.
- Facial expressions and hand gestures.
- Natural interactions between friends.

Avoid exaggerated acting, identity drift, wardrobe changes, distorted hands, extra fingers, duplicate characters, teleportation, CGI-like appearances, or physically impossible movements.

**Final Direction:** The finished video should look like a genuine personal memory captured by a friend on an inexpensive early 2000s digital camcorder in Korea. It should feel imperfect, nostalgic, intimate, spontaneous, and completely believable, as if someone found an old home video from the summer of 2003.
