Prompt

DURATION: 15 SECONDS  
ASPECT RATIO: 9:16 VERTICAL  
MODE: TEXT TO VIDEO  
GENRE: Neo-noir anime / Gun-fu / Crime action

VISUAL STYLE:

Spectacular high-budget 1990s Japanese seinen anime OVA. Authentic hand-drawn 2D cel animation, gritty urban noir atmosphere, sharp expressive ink linework, dramatic shadows, richly painted backgrounds, sophisticated character animation and explosive sakuga action.

A stylish combination of grounded martial arts, realistic gunplay and dynamic cinematic editing. Deep midnight blues, warm amber lighting, crimson accents, subtle film grain and beautifully animated muzzle flashes.

SETTING:

A narrow, dimly lit apartment hallway in Tokyo at night. Flickering fluorescent lights, wooden doors, scattered debris, rain visible through a window at the far end.

CHARACTERS:

The protagonist is a lean Japanese hitman in his thirties, with messy black hair, sharp facial features, a black suit, loosened tie and white shirt. He carries a black semi-automatic pistol.

Two armed assassins wearing dark tactical clothing confront him inside the corridor.

0–3s — THE FIRST SHOT

Extreme close-up of the protagonist's narrowed eyes.

A sudden gunshot tears through the silence.

Smash cut to a dramatic low-angle shot: the protagonist twists sideways as a bullet shatters the wooden doorframe beside his head.

He immediately raises his pistol and fires twice toward the first attacker.

Brilliant muzzle flashes illuminate the narrow corridor.

3–6s — CLOSE-QUARTERS GUN-FU

The second attacker rushes toward him.

The protagonist deflects the attacker's pistol arm with his left forearm, steps inside his guard and delivers a brutal elbow strike to his face.

Without breaking the movement, he grabs the attacker's jacket, pivots and throws him against the corridor wall.

Dynamic lateral tracking shot, sharp anime impact frames, convincing physical contact.

6–9s — THE COUNTERATTACK

The first attacker fires again from the far end of the corridor.

The protagonist ducks behind the fallen attacker, then slides sideways across the wooden floor.

A spectacular low-angle tracking shot follows him as he fires two precise shots while sliding.

Bullets strike the wall behind his opponent, sending wood splinters flying.

The first attacker staggers and falls out of frame.

9–12s — THE FINAL EXCHANGE

The second attacker recovers and lunges at the protagonist.

A rapid sequence of beautifully choreographed close-ups: a wrist grab, a blocked punch, a sharp knee strike.

The protagonist spins behind his opponent, forces the attacker's gun arm downward and delivers one final close-range shot toward the floor, narrowly missing him.

The attacker drops his weapon and collapses to his knees.

12–15s — THE SILENCE

Sudden silence.

Low-angle close-up of the protagonist, breathing heavily, his black hair falling across his eyes.

Smoke rises from his pistol.

The camera slowly pushes toward his face as he adjusts his loosened tie with his free hand.

Behind him, the corridor lights flicker and finally go dark.

A final flash of lightning illuminates his silhouette.

Cut to black.

CINEMATOGRAPHY AND EDITING:

Extremely dynamic cinematic montage with aggressive whip pans, dramatic low angles, extreme close-ups, fast lateral tracking shots and powerful perspective changes.

The 9:16 vertical composition must be used creatively: dramatic full-body shots, strong foreground-background separation, narrow architectural framing and intense vertical camera movements.

Fast, readable action with exceptional timing, momentum and visual clarity.

No slow motion. Every second must feel energetic and purposeful.

ANIMATION DIRECTION:

Premium hand-drawn sakuga animation with convincing body mechanics, expressive facial acting, sharp silhouettes, powerful impact frames, realistic weapon recoil and fluid transitions between shooting and martial arts.

Every physical interaction must have clear cause and effect.

AUDIO:

Sharp, powerful gunshots with realistic indoor echoes, metallic shell casings hitting the floor, heavy footsteps, brutal physical impacts, splintering wood and intense industrial percussion.

The soundtrack stops abruptly for the final three seconds.

No dialogue.

CRITICAL:

Strict 9:16 vertical framing throughout.

Exactly three characters: one protagonist and two attackers.

Maintain consistent character designs, clothing, weapons and spatial relationships.

The protagonist carries exactly one pistol throughout the entire sequence.

No duplicated characters, no extra limbs, no teleportation, no disconnected strikes, no floating weapons.

Authentic traditional 2D Japanese anime only. No CGI, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no text, no subtitles.
