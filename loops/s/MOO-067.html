Prompt

Create a ultra-realistic cinematic short film featuring a young adult woman with long, naturally messy dark brown hair, wearing a simple light-colored sleeveless nightdress.

SCENE 1 (0–3 seconds):
A cinematic overhead shot of a young woman lying alone on an unmade bed in a dimly lit bedroom at night. Clothes are scattered across the floor, creating a realistic messy atmosphere. Teal light filters through window blinds, creating dramatic shadows across the room. She slowly sits up, looking exhausted and lost in thought.

SCENE 2 (3–6 seconds):
The camera smoothly transitions into a closer overhead angle as she gets out of bed and walks toward the bathroom. Her movements are natural, subtle and realistic. Maintain consistent character identity, facial features, hairstyle and clothing.

SCENE 3 (6–10 seconds):
Inside a small, slightly messy vintage bathroom, she stands in front of an old mirror, holding a cigarette. Soft greenish bathroom lighting contrasts with warm light coming from the bedroom. Capture intimate cinematic details, natural facial expressions and realistic reflections.

SCENE 4 (10–15 seconds):
Transition into a small vintage kitchen. She stands near the kitchen counter, holding her cigarette, looking quietly into the distance. The camera slowly pushes toward her face, capturing subtle expressions, natural skin texture and cinematic smoke movement.

VISUAL STYLE:
Ultra-realistic live-action cinematography, dark moody atmosphere, teal and warm amber color grading, natural skin textures, practical lighting, realistic environmental details, shallow depth of field, cinematic camera movements, atmospheric smoke, subtle film grain, professional movie production quality.

IMPORTANT:
Maintain exactly the same woman's face, hairstyle, body proportions and outfit throughout all scenes. Natural human movements, realistic hands and fingers, physically accurate reflections, no face morphing, no character changes, no artificial animation look, no sudden transitions.

FORMAT: Vertical 16:9
