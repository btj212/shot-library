Prompt

Create realistic handheld smartphone street vlog footage. An ordinary person discovers that touching everyday objects causes instant magical transformations. Natural daylight, real pedestrians, authentic street sounds, imperfect handheld movement and natural autofocus.

0-3 seconds: The person touches a dry brown leaf on the sidewalk. A brief white flash. The same leaf becomes translucent green glass with a tiny living forest visible inside its veins. The camera moves closer in surprise.

3-6 seconds: They tap an orange traffic cone. A flash. The same cone transforms into a miniature red and white lighthouse with a rotating light. It stays in exactly the same position.

6-9 seconds: They touch a metal street drain. A flash. The same grate becomes a clear underwater window revealing a giant manta ray swimming through a turquoise ocean beneath the pavement. The street remains dry.

9-12 seconds: They touch a bus stop glass panel. A flash. A beautiful green and violet aurora appears beyond the glass, above distant snowy mountains. Real street reflections remain visible.

12-15 seconds: They touch a round manhole cover. A flash. Its surface becomes a miniature galaxy surrounded by a glowing blue ring. Suddenly, the stars form the exact shape of the street being filmed. The person freezes, the camera jerks backward, and the video cuts to black.

Keep every transformation instant, clean and visually clear. Preserve object positions, scale, perspective and background continuity. Magic stays contained within each object. No duplication, melting, smoke, random effects, distorted hands, extra fingers or warped buildings. Natural human reactions, no exaggerated acting, no background music. Use realistic smartphone footage, not glossy CGI or cinematic color grading.

Priority: realism, clean transformations, visual surprise and a powerful ending.
