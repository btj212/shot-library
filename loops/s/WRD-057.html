A quiet, cinematic three-shot sequence, 12 seconds, 16:9, shot on medium-format film with soft overcast light, using the attached references. The woman is from image 3: same face, hair and outfit. The man is from image 5: same face, coat and silhouette, with everything he wears in the same pale cream as the city. The world is built from the architecture in images 1, 2 and 6: the curved stacked ramp tower, the vast concrete stairways and the interlocking monolithic blocks, all raw board-marked concrete in monochrome cream and beige under a pale sky. She is the only colour in the world. SHOT 1, CLOSE-UP (0-3s): Tight on her face and shoulders against a board-marked concrete wall. She looks down, then slowly lifts her eyes and turns her head to look off to the right, as if she has noticed something. SHOT 2, LOW-ANGLE MEDIUM-WIDE, THEN ORBIT (3-10s): On a broad concrete terrace in front of the vast stairways and the curved ramp tower, the man stands completely still, head tilted back, looking up at the sky. She walks up beside him and lightly touches his coat sleeve. In that instant his coat becomes a rich deep plum and the trousers underneath a deep maroon. He lowers his head and turns to look at her as she walks on past him and out of frame to the right. The camera slowly orbits around him, low and close, the ramp tower and concrete blocks wheeling behind him, as he stands watching where she went. SHOT 3, WIDE (10-12s): A vast, still, futuristic plaza of monumental cream concrete blocks, cantilevered slabs and stepped terraces, the curved ramp tower at the left edge of frame. Two tiny figures stand far apart on the empty paving: him, a dark spot of plum, and her, a small spot of baby blue. Nothing else moves. Fabric moves with weight, steps land with weight. No one speaks. No music; only soft footsteps on concrete, the brush of her hand on his sleeve, and a light wind.
