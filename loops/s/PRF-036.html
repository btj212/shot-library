Prompt

Create an ultra-realistic comedic video set at an American international airport passport-control counter. A young adult American woman with long light-brown hair arrives after a 12-hour flight, looking exhausted, with messy hair, smudged makeup, and wrinkled travel clothes. She approaches the passport officer and hands over her passport.

The officer compares her passport photo with her tired appearance, looking increasingly confused. She checks the photo herself and gasps, saying, “Girl, who is that?!”

Determined to look presentable, she opens her carry-on bag right beside the counter and performs an exaggerated travel makeover. Show quick, funny close-ups as she changes into a fresh cream-colored sweater, brushes her hair into soft waves, applies foundation, mascara, blush, and glossy lipstick, and checks her appearance in a compact mirror.

The officer watches patiently as she adjusts her hair one final time, sprays a little perfume, and confidently presents her passport again. The officer compares the photo with her refreshed appearance, pauses, and finally stamps her passport with a subtle nod.

She smiles triumphantly and says, “See? Same person. Better lighting!”

She walks away confidently while the officer stares after her in disbelief.

Photorealistic live-action comedy, realistic airport lighting, natural handheld camera movement, detailed makeup application, believable expressions, accurate lip synchronization, authentic airport ambience, and consistent character identity. Keep the makeover playful and non-suggestive. No magical transformations, no face swapping, no subtitles, no logos, and no watermarks.
