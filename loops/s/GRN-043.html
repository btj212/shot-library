Prompt

Create a 15-second ultra-realistic 16:9 landscape video featuring the same naturally attractive 24-year-old Korean woman with long loose dark hair, minimal makeup, an oversized maroon hoodie, and loose casual pants. Maintain identical facial identity, hairstyle, outfit, and proportions throughout.

**Shot 1 — 0–3s:** Handheld medium-wide shot on a quiet neighborhood sidewalk in late afternoon. She exits a small bookstore carrying a tall, uneven stack of paperbacks reaching just below her chin. She adjusts her grip and walks carefully, looking pleased.

**Shot 2 — 3–6s:** Side-angle handheld tracking shot. The books shift as she walks. She tilts her head to see around the stack and uses her chin to steady a sliding book.

**Shot 3 — 6–9s:** Low-angle close-up of her hands and the books. One paperback slips from the middle; she catches it against her elbow, causing the top book to slide forward. She freezes, struggling to stabilize the stack.

**Shot 4 — 9–12s:** Quick reframe to a medium shot. She regains control, but the top paperback slips, lightly lands on her head, and slides back onto the stack. She blinks and looks upward in confusion.

**Shot 5 — 12–15s:** Slightly crooked medium-wide shot beside a bench. She carefully lowers and straightens the books, brushes back her hair, and gives the camera a sheepish, triumphant smile. Before leaving, she picks up one final book and hugs it to her chest.

**Visual style:** Authentic early-2000s Sony MiniDV home-video footage filmed by another person, with subtle handheld shake, imperfect framing, spontaneous reframing, occasional autofocus hunting, slight exposure shifts, faded colors, soft contrast, realistic skin texture, and natural afternoon light. Playful, relatable physical comedy without exaggerated slapstick.

**Motion quality:** Smooth, continuous real-time movement with believable book weight, gravity, friction, and hand coordination. Maintain consistent book count, colors, and positions. Every slipping book must move continuously and visibly. No teleportation, floating, duplicated objects, distorted fingers, self-correcting stacks, stuttering, judder, frame skipping, slow motion, or excessive cinematic movement.

**Audio:** Natural neighborhood ambience, footsteps, paper covers rubbing, books lightly shifting and thumping, and her quiet amused laugh. No music, narration, or artificial sound effects.
