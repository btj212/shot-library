Create a 30-second ultra-realistic cinematic FPV destruction sequence through a dense modern city during a catastrophic collapse. The camera moves at extreme speed from ground level, weaving through traffic, falling concrete, shattered glass, dust, fire, and collapsing structures. Relentless momentum, realistic destruction physics, cinematic scale, dynamic motion blur, dramatic lighting, 4K HDR. No text, logos, subtitles, or watermarks.

[0–5s] — THE COLLAPSE
The FPV camera races inches above a crowded city street. Cars brake and swerve as buildings suddenly begin collapsing on both sides. Concrete fragments and glass rain down while the camera aggressively weaves between vehicles and debris.

[5–10s] — NARROW ESCAPE
A huge section of concrete crashes directly ahead. The camera sharply banks around it, then ducks beneath collapsing scaffolding as metal beams fall behind. Dust rapidly fills the street while the camera maintains extreme speed.

[10–15s] — THE ALLEY
The camera shoots into a narrow alley between towering buildings. A massive shockwave erupts behind, sending fire, dust, papers, and debris racing toward the lens. The camera accelerates harder as the alley begins collapsing around it.

[15–20s] — GROUND BREAKS
The asphalt suddenly splits open beneath the camera. The FPV view rolls violently as cracks race across the ground and vehicles fall sideways. The camera briefly flips almost upside down, narrowly clears the collapsing pavement, then stabilizes.

[20–25s] — THROUGH THE CHAOS
The camera bursts through the end of the alley into a wider boulevard engulfed in destruction. Buildings crumble in the distance, fires burn between abandoned vehicles, and enormous clouds of dust sweep across the street.

[25–30s] — FINAL BURST
The camera continues forward at maximum speed, weaving between overturned cars and falling debris. A final building collapses behind it, creating a gigantic wall of dust and smoke. The camera breaks through into an open intersection, revealing the full devastated skyline before rapidly pulling upward into a wide cinematic shot.
