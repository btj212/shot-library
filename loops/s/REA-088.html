Prompt

A single continuous vertical 9:16 shot, 15 seconds, photorealistic, shot like a viral smartphone/drone-style action video in bright desert sunlight. A motocross rider in a white full-face helmet, a neon-yellow riding jersey and pants, white riding boots and gloves, on a blue dirt bike, rides straight up a gigantic, almost vertical red-orange sandstone wall. A huge crowd stands along the rim at the very top, hundreds of people with phones raised, cheering. Clear deep-blue sky, harsh midday sun, strong warm red and orange tones.

Action, in sequence:
0–1s: Close behind-the-rider view. He sits on the bike at the base of the cliff, back to camera, revving, bright yellow jersey against the red rock. Another rider on a blue bike waits in the far left background.
1–2.5s: He launches forward and hits the base of the wall. The rear wheel spins hard and throws a thick, billowing cloud of orange dust. The camera pulls back as he goes up the rock.
2.5–5s: He rides straight up the wall, getting smaller as he climbs. A long fiery-orange dust trail streams down behind him along the rock face. The top rim comes into view with a thin line of spectators looking down.
5–10s: The camera tilts up and tracks him from below as he climbs, bike nearly vertical, back to camera, arms slightly out, wheel kicking up bursts of orange dirt that tumble down the rock. The crowd along the top edge grows bigger and clearer as he gets closer.
10–12.5s: The rim fills the top of the frame. Hundreds of spectators lean over the edge with phones held up, shouting and waving. The rider keeps steady throttle, closing the last few meters, dust still streaming below him.
12.5–15s: He reaches the top. The front wheel crests the edge, the crowd surges forward and grabs him and the bike, pulling them over the edge into the crowd, a mass of hands, phones and raised arms around him.

Camera: Starts at close shoulder height behind the rider, then pulls back and tilts up with fast, slightly shaky handheld energy, following him up the cliff from below at a steep low angle. Ends at a low-angle shot of the crowd swallowing him at the rim against the blue sky. Wide-angle phone lens, sharp focus on the rider, natural motion blur on the dust.

Lighting and look: Strong direct sunlight, saturated orange-red rock, deep blue sky, high contrast, neon-yellow jersey popping against the red wall, glowing backlit dust clouds, crisp texture on the rock face and bike.

Audio: A high-revving two-stroke or four-stroke dirt bike engine, tire spin and rocks skittering down, the crowd's roar growing louder toward the top, ending in screams and cheers as he is pulled over. No music.

Avoid: cuts, text overlays, logos or readable brand names, cartoon style, distorted bike or body, extra riders in the main action, watermark.
