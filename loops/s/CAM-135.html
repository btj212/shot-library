SOL Y SOMBRA — A SPANISH TRAVEL DIARY

[ENGINE DIRECTIVE]
Create an exactly 30-second, 16:9 photorealistic cinematic travel film, 60fps if supported, styled like a premium Spanish tourism campaign. Natural Mediterranean lighting, realistic skin, detailed architecture, subtle film grain, smooth motion and warm cinematic color grading.

HERO LOCK: @image1 is the ONLY hero. Preserve his exact face, hairstyle, beard, skin tone, body proportions and outfit in every shot. No identity drift, clones or costume changes. He carries one compact mirrorless camera on a neck strap.

[TIMELINE]
00:00–00:05 | SEVILLE: Aerial crane reveal of Plaza de España, its curved canal, tiled bridges and brick towers in soft morning light. Transition to a low-angle 35mm tracking shot as the hero walks across the bridge, admires the architecture and smiles. At 00:05, he photographs a colorful tiled alcove.

00:05–00:10 | CÓRDOBA MEZQUITA: A motivated rightward whip-pan reveals symmetrical red-and-white striped arches. The hero stands beneath them, looking upward in wonder as warm light rays illuminate floating dust.

00:10–00:15 | ARCHWAYS: Smooth 35mm gimbal tracking follows him walking between ancient columns, gently touching the stone. At 00:13, show an over-the-shoulder camera-screen close-up as he reviews his photograph and smiles.

00:15–00:20 | PATIO CAFÉ: Macro 85mm shot of café con leche, rising steam, ceramic saucer and spoon on a rustic table surrounded by colorful flowers. Cut to the hero sipping coffee, briefly closing his eyes and smiling peacefully.

00:20–00:25 | ALHAMBRA: A smooth 180-degree orbit follows him beside a reflecting pool at golden hour. At 00:23, crane upward to reveal palace towers, Granada and distant mountains.

00:25–00:30 | SAN NICOLÁS: Sunset side-profile shot with the glowing Alhambra across the valley. He raises his camera and takes a final photograph at 00:28. Slowly push into his face as he lowers the camera and smiles warmly. Hold the final frame until exactly 00:30.

[AUDIO]
Original uplifting Spanish nylon-string guitar, light cajón and handclaps, synchronized with location ambience: fountains, birds, footsteps, stone echoes, café sounds, water ripples, wind and distant bells. Shutter clicks at 00:05, 00:13 and 00:28; bell at 00:23. End with a warm sustained musical chord fading to silence.

[CONSTRAINTS]
Natural human movement, anatomically correct hands, stable camera and realistic reflections. Sparse blurred tourists only. No readable text, logos, subtitles, extra heroes, warped architecture, floating objects, plastic skin or excessive bloom. Maintain continuous lighting progression from morning to sunset.
