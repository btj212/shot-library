A short, reusable motion-transfer prompt. @Image1 sets the character and location (an astronaut on a sunset rooftop), and @Video1 is limited to one job: "defines ONLY the body movement and its timing: copy that exact movement sequence", followed by the sequence in words (arms out, full turn, one step, wide wave) so the model has both the clip and a text check. It then excludes everything else in the clip ("Do not copy the man, his clothes or the white studio from @Video1"), and ends with Camera (full-body wide, static) and Sound lines. The posted video is the creator's side-by-side test of the same prompt in Seedance 2.5 (top) and Kling 4.0 (bottom).

Prompt

@Image1 is the astronaut: white spacesuit, helmet visor raised, a friendly woman in her 30s with short dark hair, standing on a flat city rooftop at sunset. @Video1 defines ONLY the body movement and its timing: copy that exact movement sequence (she raises both arms out to the sides, turns a full circle on the spot, steps forward once, and finishes with a wide wave of her right hand). Do not copy the man, his clothes or the white studio from @Video1. The astronaut performs the movement on the rooftop from @Image1, the sunset skyline behind her.
Camera: full-body wide shot, static.
Sound: light wind and distant city hum. No dialogue. No music.
