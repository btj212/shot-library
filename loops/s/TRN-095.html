Crimson Knight vs a 10 m cyclops: a 30 s per-second battle beat sheet that links beats by continuous camera moves and locks screen direction (Seedance 2.5)

TRN-095TransitionsSeedance 2.5by @tokyo_Valentine2026-11-21

A Seedance 2.5 action prompt written as thirty one-second beats, each opening with a camera verb (glide, crane up, push in, pull back, tilt up, track, pan, whip, drop, rise). Two global rules do the transition work: "Action-movie camerawork continues every second throughout the entire video" and "Where no cut is specified, link adjacent beats through continuous camera movement", so only the beats marked "cut" become hard cuts and the rest flow as one moving camera (the double leap off the horse and onto the giant's knee is tracked sideways without a cut). It also fixes a lineup with screen direction ("From behind, the elf is on frame left; from the front, the elf is on frame right"), gives scale in metres, caps how often each @ Image character may appear, and keeps the violence bloodless.

Prompt

30 seconds, 16:9, photoreal live-action fantasy. The plain before a white stone fortress gate is in bright, clear daylight. Warm morning sun rims the grassy hill.
Action-movie camerawork continues every second throughout the entire video.
Where no cut is specified, link adjacent beats through continuous camera movement, especially the double leap.

@ Image1 — Crimson Knight. Dark hair, stubble, dark plate, torn crimson cloak, longsword. He rides a dark bay. The only red figure on the battlefield.
@ Image2 — Holy Shield Knights. Cavalry in engraved closed helms, steel plate and weathered ivory cloaks.
@ Image3 — Horned War Brutes. An army of 3 m gray brutes with wide horns and shaggy fur, carrying clubs.
@ Image4 — Cyclops. One 10 m giant, measured to its horn tips. One orange slit-pupil eye, ivory brow, ring marking on belly. Spiked iron ball on a rigid mace shaft.
@ Image5 — Elf archer. Female, blonde braids, pointed ears, green cloth, wooden bow and quiver.
@ Image6 — Dwarf warrior. Short and broad, gray braided beard, steel helm, ochre tunic, fur mantle, broad axe.
Keep faces, scale, costumes and weapons consistent. Only @ Image2 and @ Image3 appear in ranks. @ Image1, @ Image4, @ Image5 and @ Image6 each appear only once.
Fixed lineup: @ Image5 elf always stands at @ Image1 Crimson Knight's left side, @ Image6 dwarf at his right side. From behind, the elf is on frame left; from the front, the elf is on frame right.

00–01s Glide low behind @ Image5 elf, @ Image1 Crimson Knight and @ Image6 dwarf standing on the sunlit crest. From frame one, the plain below is already filled with @ Image3 horned brutes surrounding the white fortress, and @ Image4 cyclops towers among them.
01–02s Crane up over their shoulders. @ Image3 horned ranks encircle the white walls and fill the plain to the horizon. @ Image4 cyclops stands at their center.
02–03s Push in as @ Image4 cyclops roars. Frame one @ Image3 brute beside its knee and the gate behind it for scale.
03–04s On a horn blast, cut head-on to the faces of @ Image5 elf, @ Image1 Crimson Knight and @ Image6 dwarf, same lineup. Rows of mounted @ Image2 Holy Shield Knights fill the crest behind them.
04–05s Pull back as @ Image1 Crimson Knight swings onto his waiting dark bay. @ Image2 ivory cloaks ripple down the ranks.
05–06s Tilt up with @ Image1 Crimson Knight's raised longsword. @ Image2 cavalry surges downhill at his signal.
06–07s Track beside pounding hooves. Alongside the cavalry, @ Image5 elf and @ Image6 dwarf run on foot down the slope into the fight.
07–08s Hold broadside as @ Image2 cavalry crashes into the @ Image3 horned front rank. Show contact before dust rises.
08–09s Pan with @ Image3 brutes flying backward. They tumble out of sight into dust.
09–10s Cut straight overhead. Glide over @ Image2 ivory knights and @ Image3 gray brutes intermingling across the whole plain.
10–11s From above, follow @ Image1 Crimson Knight's red cloak as he cuts a straight path through the melee toward @ Image4 cyclops.
11–12s Drop beside one @ Image2 Holy Shield Knight knocked off his horse by an @ Image3 brute. His closed helm rolls away, revealing a young face. The @ Image3 brute raises its club over him.
12–13s Frame @ Image5 elf and the club-bearing @ Image3 brute in profile. Her arrow pierces the brute through its fur mantle. It drops the club and collapses into dust.
13–14s Whip low as @ Image6 dwarf sweeps a different @ Image3 brute's legs out with his axe. It topples sideways into dust.
14–15s @ Image6 dwarf grabs the young helmetless @ Image2 knight's hand and hauls him upright.
15–16s A round shadow crosses the riders. Tilt up to @ Image4 cyclops lifting its spiked iron mace.
16–17s Drop with the iron ball. It slams into bare earth. Cracks race outward from the impact.
17–18s Track the shock wave flinging @ Image2 horses and riders sideways. It also knocks the young helmetless @ Image2 knight onto his back.
18–19s Cut to his ground-level POV. His gauntlet rises into frame. @ Image4 cyclops bends low, its single orange eye looming over him.
19–20s Hold his POV. The iron ball of @ Image4 cyclops rises again at frame edge. The din drops to a single breath.
20–21s Cut over @ Image5 elf's bow on the twang. Follow her arrow into @ Image4 cyclops's single eye. The lid snaps shut and the mace stalls.
21–22s Whip down as @ Image6 dwarf slashes @ Image4 cyclops's wrapped ankle with his axe. Its foot gives way. In the background, @ Image1 Crimson Knight gallops straight toward the giant.
22–23s Pull wide in profile. @ Image4 cyclops drops to one knee and bows its head. @ Image1 Crimson Knight on his dark bay draws level with the raised knee.
23–24s Track sideways without a cut. @ Image1 Crimson Knight leaps off his horse's back. The riderless dark bay gallops on beneath him.
24–25s Keep tracking. @ Image1 Crimson Knight's boot lands on @ Image4 cyclops's raised knee, takes his weight, then pushes off into a second, higher leap.
25–26s Rise beside @ Image1 Crimson Knight in slow motion. His crimson cloak fans out against the white stone walls as his longsword rises above @ Image4 cyclops's bowed head.
26–27s Still in profile and in slow motion, @ Image1 Crimson Knight drives the longsword into @ Image4 cyclops's ivory forehead above its eye. A dry crack brings back real time.
27–28s Widen as @ Image1 Crimson Knight pulls the blade free and lands clear. @ Image4 cyclops collapses into billowing dust. The @ Image3 horned army breaks and scatters.
28–29s Ease back through the dust. @ Image5 elf, @ Image1 Crimson Knight and @ Image6 dwarf stand shoulder to shoulder on foot facing us, same lineup. Fleeing horns recede behind them.
29–30s Keep their faces clear on the pullback. Behind them, the young helmetless @ Image2 knight is the first to raise his sword. Then all @ Image2 Holy Shield Knights raise theirs with one wordless victory roar.

Sound effects only. Wind, field horn, hooves, armor, bowstrings, splitting earth, the cyclops's roar and the final cheer. No BGM, dialogue or narration. Bloodless impacts. Fallen enemies collapse into dust. No visible wounds, deaths, captions, logos or reference-sheet panels.
