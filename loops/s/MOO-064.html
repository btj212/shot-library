Prompt

A young Korean woman in a black leather jacket and denim skirt waits alone on a nearly empty Seoul subway platform. Suddenly, her eyes turn milky white, dark veins spread across her face, and she begins laughing uncontrollably. When a train arrives, she drops to all fours and crawls unnaturally toward terrified passengers. A commuter runs into a service corridor, but she suddenly appears behind him, dragging her fingers along the walls. The station lights flicker out as the last train departs. In the darkness, her pale eyes appear inside the train’s final window.

STYLE: Photorealistic Korean zombie horror, cinematic 4K, handheld camera, realistic movements, eerie laughter, flickering fluorescent lights, suspenseful sound design, natural film grain.

NEGATIVE: Cartoon, anime, obvious CGI, excessive gore, inconsistent characters, subtitles, text, or watermark. No music or narration.

Tags: short prompt, story paragraph, style line, negative line, handheld horror, flickering fluorescents, natural film grain, no music
