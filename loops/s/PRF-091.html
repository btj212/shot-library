Prompt

Vertical phone video, filmed by a friend, in a small sunny kitchen. A man in his early thirties with a short beard stands at the stove and cracks an egg into a hot pan with one hand. The yolk lands whole and the white starts to sizzle. He looks up at the camera, grins and says, "One hand. No shell." Natural sound: the crack, the sizzle and his voice.
