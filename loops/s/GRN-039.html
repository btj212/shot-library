Prompt

Create an ultra-realistic early-2000s personal vlog filmed by a friend on a consumer MiniDV camcorder in South Korea, 2003. A Korean woman in her early 20s buys her first compact digital camera, struggles with its controls, takes a blurry selfie, and proudly carries it home.

Character continuity: Same woman throughout: soft oval face, natural skin, shoulder-length dark brown hair with side-swept bangs, pastel zip-up cardigan, fitted top, dark jeans, sneakers, and small shoulder bag.

Six scenes:

1. 0–5s: She arrives at a Korean electronics shop, spots a silver digital camera in the window, smiles excitedly, and enters.
2. 5–10s: At a glass display counter, she examines the camera, presses its physical buttons, and receives guidance from the salesperson.
3. 10–15s: She accidentally opens menus and playback mode, looks confused, then learns the shutter controls.
4. 15–20s: She attempts a self-timer selfie, gets a blurry photo, laughs, tries again, and proudly shows the tiny LCD.
5. 20–26s: She pays cash, receives the camera, battery, memory card, wrist strap, manual, and cardboard packaging, then packs her purchase into her shoulder bag.
6. 26–30s: Outside, she briefly admires the camera, secures it in her bag, smiles at the camcorder, and walks away as the footage cuts off naturally.

Camera continuity: One identical early-2000s silver compact camera throughout, with rectangular metal body, small lens, physical buttons, tiny rear LCD, wrist strap, and removable memory card. Preserve its identity and physical dimensions from display to purchase. Keep the transaction and packaging physically consistent.

Visual style: Genuine 2003 consumer MiniDV footage, 16:9 standard-definition softness, mild video noise, limited dynamic range, imperfect autofocus, exposure shifts, shaky handheld framing, accidental zooms, fluorescent shop lighting, period-authentic Korean storefronts, electronics, signage, and packaging. Natural ambient street traffic, footsteps, Korean conversation, button clicks, and shop sounds.

Strict realism: Natural expressions and movements, consistent face, outfit, hairstyle, bag, and props. Authentic low-resolution photographs and slow camera menus. No smartphones, touchscreens, front-facing displays, modern electronics, cinematic transitions, 4K sharpness, heavy grading, VHS overlays, face morphing, duplicated fingers, or disappearing objects.

Mood: Candid nostalgia, progressing from curiosity and confusion to laughter and genuine excitement. Must feel like a real personal memory, not a polished cinematic recreation.
