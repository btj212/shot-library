Prompt

Create a high-energy, realistic 15-second User Generated Content (UGC) product advertisement featuring an Indonesian man in his mid-to-late 20s unboxing and testing a premium smart desk fan.

The video should feel like authentic smartphone footage captured at home, not a traditional commercial. Use rapid jump cuts, satisfying ASMR sounds, natural reactions, realistic movements, and cinematic product close-ups.

PRODUCT: BREEZEO TOWER PRO FAN.

A premium compact tower fan featuring a matte-ivory-white body with brushed matte-gold accents, a bladeless slim-profile design, a touch-sensitive control panel on top with illuminated icons, five wind-speed modes, an auto-oscillation function, a small LED speed indicator, a quiet brushless motor, a slim remote control, a braided power cable, and a soft fabric dust cover.

SHOT 1: THE ARRIVAL (0-2s)

[Medium shot, eye-level]

The man sits on his living room floor with a tall delivery box branded "BREEZEO TOWER PRO" in front of him. He tears the sealing tape open in one satisfying motion.

SFX: Loud, crisp tape ripping sound.

SHOT 2: THE REVEAL (2-4s)

[Medium shot, slightly low angle]

He opens the box flaps, removes the protective foam, and lifts the fan out of its dust cover to reveal the matte-ivory-white body and gold accents. He looks genuinely impressed.

Dialogue: "Okay, this finally came!"

SFX: Cardboard movement, foam sliding, fabric cover rustling.

SHOT 3: THE CLOSE LOOK (4-7s)

[Full shot, clean jump cut]

The fan now stands in the corner of the room. He peels the protective film off the control panel, runs his thumb along the gold trim, and taps the touch panel, which glows with illuminated icons.

Dialogue: "Look at this! It's so slim and clean."

SFX: Protective film peeling, soft touch beeps, power-on chime.

SHOT 4: THE AIRFLOW TEST (7-11s)

[Close-up, 45-degree angle]

He plugs in the fan, cycles through the speed modes, and switches on oscillation. A strong, smooth stream of air ruffles a sheet of paper and the curtains behind him. He holds his hand in front of the airflow, then raises his eyebrows, clearly impressed by how quiet it is.

SFX: Plug inserting, soft airflow whoosh, gentle touch beeps, paper fluttering.

SHOT 5: THE FINAL EXPERIENCE (11-13s)

[Medium shot, eye-level]

He closes his eyes, lets the cool breeze wash across his face, opens them, and smiles directly at the camera.

Dialogue: "Wait... I think my room just got ten degrees cooler!"

SHOT 6: HERO PRODUCT SHOT (13-15s)

[Slow cinematic close-up]

The BREEZEO TOWER PRO stands in the corner beside its open box, dust cover, and remote, with the curtains gently swaying. Soft natural window light reflects across the matte-ivory-white body and gold accents.

Final dialogue: "This is officially my new favourite upgrade."
