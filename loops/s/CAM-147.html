Prompt

Edit @Video1. Change the camera only: an extreme wide shot — the subject small against the environment, the location doing the storytelling, horizon carefully placed on a third.

Camera position: at natural eye level — the lens at the subject's own eye height, straight-on and human, the way a standing observer would see the scene.

Locked off — the camera is bolted down: absolutely ZERO camera movement of any kind, no drift, no push, no sway, no zoom; every frame's composition identical to the first, as if shot on a locked tripod.

Apply ONE single camera setup to the ENTIRE video: every shot and every cut must be reshot with exactly the same framing, the same camera position and the same movement described above — one uniform camera across all shots. The composition of every shot must visibly change to this new camera; do not keep any shot's original framing.

Do not change: the subject's performance, timing, expressions and blocking, their wardrobe and identity, the environment, the lighting, or the original audio. Everything about the scene stays exactly as shot — only the camera is different.

Establish the enormous gallery; woman small in the frame, full column heights and floor visible. Preserve exact same adult woman, red veil and long dress, action timing, blue-grey gallery, photo grain. No text or dialogue; one continuous shot.
