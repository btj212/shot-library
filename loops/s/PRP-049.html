Prompt

Photorealistic live-action miniature adventure, cinematic macro lens, ultra-shallow depth of field, realistic convenience store, energetic camera movements, practical lighting, physically accurate interactions, intricate textures, premium commercial film quality, 4K.

Create a 30-second cinematic action sequence starring one athletic miniature woman, approximately 1:5 scale. Preserve her facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, backpack, body proportions, and miniature size consistently throughout the video.

She begins by sprinting across towering biscuit shelves, leaping between packages as a giant bag of chips bursts open beside her. She dodges tumbling snacks, jumps onto a rolling cookie, and uses it to cross a dangerous gap. Next, she scrambles across a gigantic juice carton, swings from its straw, and lands on a dangling sale banner. A moving shopping cart carries her toward checkout, where she vaults over the scanner and races along the conveyor belt between enormous grocery items. In the chilled aisle, she slides across frosty packaging, narrowly escapes a closing refrigerator door, and climbs its handle to safety. For the finale, she reaches the top of a towering promotional display and surveys the bustling supermarket below. The camera slowly pulls away, revealing her tiny scale against the gigantic everyday world.

CAMERA & STYLE: Ground-level macro tracking, dramatic close-ups, sweeping camera arcs, handheld energy, strong foreground parallax, realistic motion blur, detailed reflections, natural shadows, cinematic focus transitions, and seamless action continuity. Every jump, landing, and object interaction must feel physically believable.

AUDIO: Detailed footsteps, rustling wrappers, rolling snacks, creaking plastic, shopping-cart rattles, checkout beeps, conveyor motors, refrigerator ambience, rushing air, and impactful cinematic sound design. No narration.

ENDING: Finish with a dramatic wide shot and elegant cinematic typography:
“ONE TINY HERO. AN EPIC ESCAPE.”
“THE SUPERMARKET IS HER PLAYGROUND.”

NEGATIVE PROMPT: Cartoon, anime, CGI-looking character, toy-like textures, artificial skin, duplicated person, inconsistent facial identity, changing wardrobe, incorrect scale, unrealistic physics, floating objects, distorted limbs, abrupt cuts, narration, subtitles, or unwanted text.
