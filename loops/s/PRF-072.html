Prompt

Create a 30-second vertical 9:16 cinematic 3D animated comedy set in a sunny backyard garden with a small pond, flowers, muddy grass, a wooden fence, and a large tree. Use warm daylight, soft shadows, detailed environments, and polished family-animation quality.

Main Character: Maintain the exact same cute baby raccoon throughout the video: fluffy gray fur, black eye mask, round ears, tiny paws, chubby body, and highly expressive cartoon facial expressions.

Scene 1 (0–3s): A baby raccoon sits beside an open picnic lunchbox containing a shiny frosted donut. He excitedly reaches for it, but the lid suddenly snaps shut, startling him.

Scene 2 (3–7s): He grabs the lunchbox and struggles comically with it, tumbling and rolling across muddy grass. He finally sits upright, proudly holding the donut.

Scene 3 (7–10s): Medium close-up. The raccoon smiles proudly, then slowly looks upward as he realizes someone is standing over him.

Scene 4 (10–13s): Low-angle POV reveals a large, angry tan-and-white bulldog with a wrinkled face and heavy body. The lunchbox lid is stuck on its head like a helmet. Hold on the intimidating expression.

Scene 5 (13–19s): The frightened raccoon runs away. The bulldog immediately chases him across the backyard. Use fast side-tracking camera movement, bouncing bodies, flying dirt, exaggerated leg movement, and motion blur. The raccoon eventually flies out of frame toward a large tree.

Scene 6 (19–22s): Wide shot of the quiet garden. Reveal the raccoon sitting near the tree, dazed but unharmed.

Scene 7 (22–27s): The raccoon notices a small yellow-and-black striped bee hovering near a flower. He curiously watches it, then tries to catch it with his mouth. The bee buzzes around his face while he fumbles comically.

Scene 8 (27–30s): The bee escapes safely. Cut immediately to an extreme close-up of the raccoon with enormously swollen, bright-red, glossy cartoon lips from the sting. His eyes are wide with shock and embarrassment. Hold on this ridiculous expression as the final punchline.

Animation & Camera Direction:
Expressive eye animation, squash-and-stretch body movement, believable weight, cinematic framing, smooth camera tracking, quick reaction cuts, realistic motion blur, playful slapstick timing, detailed environmental lighting, and clear visual storytelling.

Consistency Rules:
Maintain identical character design, fur, proportions, and facial features in every shot. Keep the environment consistent. No dialogue, text, subtitles, logos, or watermarks.

Negative Prompt:
Inconsistent character design, changing appearance, extra limbs, malformed paws, duplicate characters, warped faces, broken anatomy, incorrect animal proportions, random objects, floating objects, camera jitter, flickering textures, changing environment, poor lip shape, stiff movement, low-detail backgrounds, text, subtitles, logos, and watermarks.
