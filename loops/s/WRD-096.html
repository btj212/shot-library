Prompt

Create a photorealistic fashion video with a humorous ending. Duration: 18 seconds. Vertical 9:16, 24 fps. No dialogue, voiceover, captions, or on-screen text.

The main character is a 28-year-old woman with expressive features and shoulder-length chestnut hair. Maintain her facial identity, age, and body proportions throughout the video.

The first four scenes take place in the spacious entrance hall of a modern private home: light walls, wood accents, a large mirror, and soft lighting. The woman showcases elegant outfits. After a brief black-screen pause, reveal what she actually wears when leaving the house.

0–3 SECONDS — TAILORED BUSINESS SUIT

She wears a charcoal trouser suit: a fitted blazer, long tailored trousers with pressed creases, a light-colored top, and black pointed-toe heels. Neatly styled hair and understated professional makeup.

Start immediately with action: she adjusts her blazer lapels and confidently raises her gaze.

The camera starts with a full-body frontal shot and smoothly orbits approximately 60 degrees to the right around her. Toward the end of the scene, it gently decelerates and stops at a three-quarter angle. Briefly hold the outfit before cutting.

3–6 SECONDS — MINI SKIRT, TIGHTS, AND STILETTOS

A clean cut on a musical beat. The new shot begins from the exact camera position where the previous scene ended.

She now wears a black mini skirt, a tucked-in white shirt, sheer black tights, and black stiletto heels. Her hair is down, styled in soft waves.

She adjusts her cuff and slightly turns her head to follow the camera.

The camera continues orbiting in the same direction for approximately another 60 degrees, then smoothly slows down and stops at a side angle. Keep her entire silhouette and footwear visible.

6–9 SECONDS — WHITE SKIRT LOOK

Another clean cut while the camera is stationary.

She wears a white midi skirt, a fitted light-colored top, and elegant beige heels. Minimal gold earrings and neatly styled hair complete the look.

The camera smoothly reverses its orbit, returning to the frontal angle. She shifts her weight onto the other leg, allowing the skirt to sway naturally.

At the end, the camera comes to a complete stop and briefly holds a full-body view.

9–12 SECONDS — EVENING GOWN

A clean cut. The camera remains in the frontal position.

She appears in a floor-length evening gown in deep burgundy, paired with elegant high heels. Her hair is arranged in a sophisticated evening updo. Professional fashion-editorial makeup: defined eyes, sculpted cheekbones, and polished lips, while preserving natural skin texture.

She touches an earring and takes a small step forward. The hem moves softly, briefly revealing her shoes. The camera remains stationary, keeping her entire body in frame.

12–13 SECONDS — COMEDIC PAUSE

A completely black screen for one second. The music cuts off abruptly. Silence.

13–18 SECONDS — WHAT SHE ACTUALLY WEARS OUT

The camera is outside the house, facing the front door. A slightly wider shot includes the doorway and porch. Soft, natural daylight.

A lock clicks. The door opens, and the same woman steps outside wearing wide-leg gray jeans, an oversized gray hoodie, and comfortable sneakers.

The hoodie is loose, with dropped shoulders and the hood down. The jeans are wide throughout the leg and slightly bunch over the shoes. Her clothes look comfortable and well cared for.

Her hair is gathered into a messy bun at the back of her head, with loose strands framing her face. Natural makeup and a relaxed expression.

She closes the door, tucks a loose strand behind her ear, slips her hands into her hoodie pockets, and casually walks past the camera with a faint, contented smile. The camera pans slightly to follow her. Her walk is natural and relaxed, without runway posing.

CAMERA AND VISUAL STYLE

Cinematic photorealism, Sony FX3 visual reference, 35 mm lens. Realistic skin, natural clothing folds, and clearly defined textures in the suiting fabric, satin, denim, and cotton.

In the first three scenes, the camera physically moves along an arc around the woman, producing natural changes in background perspective. Use stabilized movement with smooth acceleration and deceleration. No shaking or tilted horizons. Maintain a consistent distance and keep her entire body, including her shoes, visible. She stays in place and does not spin around her own axis.

Outfits change only through clean cuts while the camera is stationary. No morphing or clothing transformations on her body.

MUSIC AND SOUND EFFECTS

Accompany the first four looks with stylish, rhythmic electronic music. Align the cuts with musical accents.

During the black screen, use complete silence. In the final scene, introduce a light, relaxed beat, with audible door movement, the lock clicking, footsteps, and subtle outdoor ambience. Music must not overpower these sounds.

Convey the humor through the changes in clothing, mood, and behavior. No exaggerated facial expressions, dialogue, advertising, or logos. Avoid changes to her face, distorted hands or shoes, incorrect reflections, and objects appearing unexpectedly.
