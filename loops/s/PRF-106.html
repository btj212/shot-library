Prompt

FORMAT
15-second native video, 9:16 vertical, 5 shots, real-time speed, no slow motion. 3D animated family-feature style: soft global illumination, warm rim light, stylized proportions with big expressive eyes and rounded shapes, rich detailed textures of knitwear, winter jackets, cardboard, metal, glass and screens. Cinematic animated short, smooth character animation with strong comedic timing and big readable action.

REFERENCES
Image 1 = GRANNY face reference. Image 2 = GRANNY full-body reference. Use both only for GRANNY's face, hair, glasses, outfit, handbag and cane. Not for background, lighting or pose.
Both references in reference mode, not first frame.

REALISM
Stylized 3D animation with grounded physics: real weight and momentum. Cartoon slapstick chaos: people pull, shove and tug, but nobody punches, nobody is hurt, no blood. Every item moves and lands with real weight. GRANNY moves in total calm through the chaos, the contrast is the joke. No superpowers, no magic, no glowing effects.

CHARACTERS
GRANNY: about 80, tiny, 1.5 m tall, white hair in a tight bun, small round glasses, purple knitted cardigan over a white blouse with a collar, long grey skirt, black comfortable shoes, a small black handbag with a gold clasp on her left forearm, a wooden walking cane with a curved handle and a small four-foot base in her right hand. Calm, unhurried, a tiny knowing half-smile the entire video. Walks slowly at the same pace the whole video, never runs, never hurries, never looks stressed.
BIG GUY: huge broad man about 35, 2 m tall, red beanie, puffy black jacket. Furious, red-faced.
SECOND GUY: tall thin man about 30, green puffer jacket, grey beanie. Furious, red-faced.
CROWD: about twelve other adult shoppers of mixed ages in colourful winter jackets, scarves and beanies, frantic and wild. Among them TWO WOMEN in pink and yellow jackets.
CASHIER: young woman about 20 in a plain blue store vest with no logo, behind the checkout counter, holding a handheld barcode scanner, appears only in Shot 5.
No children, no animals.

ENVIRONMENT
Huge, packed home electronics store at 6 a.m. on Black Friday, full of detail in every shot. Outside: dark blue early morning.
SIGNAGE: exactly three large black banners with the words BLACK FRIDAY in big bold white capital letters: one above the entrance doors, two hanging from the ceiling over the main aisle. The words BLACK FRIDAY are the only text in the whole store, always spelled exactly BLACK FRIDAY, large and clean.
DECOR AND PRODUCTS: arches of red, black and gold balloons over the aisles, gold and red confetti on the shiny floor. A wall of switched-on flat-screen TVs near the entrance showing colourful abstract nature scenes, no text and no logos on the screens. Pyramids of stacked product boxes on display islands. Shelves packed with boxed microwaves, vacuum cleaners, blenders and speakers, headphones hanging on hooks, a shelf stacked with pillows. Red plastic shopping baskets and metal shopping carts everywhere. Price tags are plain red stickers with no text. All product boxes are plain colours with simple product pictures, no text and no logos.
TV AISLE: one last big flat-screen TV box, plain grey cardboard with no text and no logo, 1.2 m tall, standing upright on a low flat metal store cart with four wheels and a vertical push handle. The cart stands in the middle of the aisle, its push handle facing the camera. The shelves around it are empty.
CHECKOUT: one counter at the end of the store, frame RIGHT in Shot 4, with a small balloon arch over it.
