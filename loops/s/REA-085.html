Prompt

Ultra-Realistic Cinematic Wildlife Documentary – The Little Bird's Mud Home

Create a 30-second ultra-realistic wildlife documentary showing a small warm-brown bird carefully building a rainproof mud nest on a natural tree branch, one tiny mud pellet at a time.

SCENE 1 – Collecting the Mud (0–4 seconds)

Begin with a cinematic macro close-up of a small brown-orange bird standing on a rough tree branch in a peaceful green natural environment. The bird carefully carries a round, moist ball of dark reddish-brown mud in its beak and places it onto the branch. Show the bird's delicate beak movements and realistic feather details. The mud must look wet, heavy and naturally textured.

SCENE 2 – Building the Foundation (4–9 seconds)

The bird repeatedly brings small mud pellets and carefully attaches them together. Show realistic construction behavior as it presses, shapes and joins each piece with its beak. Gradually, the mud develops into a thick, rounded nest foundation attached firmly to the tree branch. Use detailed close-up shots to capture the natural building process.

SCENE 3 – Shaping the Nest (9–16 seconds)

Show the bird continuing to add layers of wet mud, gradually raising the walls and shaping a rounded, enclosed dome. The bird carefully smooths the exterior with its beak, leaving subtle natural cracks, uneven textures and tiny pieces of dried grass embedded in the mud.

A small circular entrance gradually forms on the front side of the dome. The nest must look handmade by a real bird, not manufactured or artificially perfect.

SCENE 4 – The Completed Mud Homes (16–23 seconds)

Transition into a wider cinematic wildlife shot revealing two neighboring rounded mud nests attached to the same natural tree branch. Both structures have thick mud walls and small circular entrance holes.

The little brown bird inspects its completed nest, approaches the entrance and carefully moves its head inside. Show realistic bird movements, natural balance and delicate interactions with the nest.

SCENE 5 – Rainproof Shelter (23–30 seconds)

The atmosphere gradually changes. Dark grey rain clouds gather behind the tree branches. A gentle breeze moves the surrounding green leaves.

Rain begins falling naturally, becoming a steady rainfall. Show realistic water droplets running down the rough exterior surfaces of both mud nests. The two birds remain safely visible inside their respective circular entrances.

End with a beautiful cinematic wide shot of both rounded mud homes on the branch during rainfall, emphasizing the contrast between the wet natural environment and the sheltered birds.

VISUAL STYLE & REALISM

- Photorealistic wildlife documentary cinematography.
- Natural bird anatomy, accurate proportions and authentic feather textures.
- Physically realistic wet mud, individual mud pellets and natural surface imperfections.
- Realistic beak movement and believable nest-building behavior.
- Soft natural daylight transitioning into overcast rainy weather.
- Shallow depth of field in close-up shots.
- Smooth, professional camera transitions.
- Natural green forest background with subtle environmental movement.
- Highly detailed 4K visual quality.
- Authentic wildlife documentary color grading.
- Realistic rain physics and water droplets.

CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Maintain the same bird appearance, feather color, body proportions, tree branch, mud texture and environmental setting throughout the video. The nest must develop gradually through visible construction actions. Preserve the circular entrance design and the final two-nest composition.

NEGATIVE PROMPT

No cartoon appearance, no animation, no CGI-looking birds, no artificial plastic textures, no oversized birds, no extra limbs, no deformed beaks, no unnatural wing movements, no floating mud, no instant nest construction, no disappearing objects, no changing bird colors, no inconsistent nest shapes, no random background changes, no unrealistic rain, no text, no subtitles, no logos and no watermarks.

OUTPUT: 30 seconds, 16:9 landscape, 24 FPS, ultra-realistic cinematic wildlife documentary.
