Prompt

Photorealistic Hollywood fantasy comedy, 16:9, 4K, shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with 35mm anamorphic lenses, 24fps cinematic film look, soft bright winter morning sunlight with gentle falling snow, clean low-noise image, creamy shallow depth of field with oval anamorphic bokeh, ultra-sharp detail, natural film grain, rich cool-and-warm color grade, smooth stable camera, fast comedic hard cuts. Setting stays the same in every shot: a frozen lake in a snowy valley with thick crystal-clear ice that never cracks, a small wooden hut with a chimney on the shore, a wooden fence, snow-covered pine trees, a big soft snowbank at the far edge of the lake, and soft white hills. Match Daisy and giant dragon references. The dragon is the same size in every shot, about as tall as the hut; show its full body, wings, legs and tail in wide shots. The dragon is eager, clumsy and sweet. Daisy wears her usual dress with only an added knitted hat, scarf and small ice skates. The dragon wears two huge brown leather skates with long steel blades, each as long as a rowing boat, with thick laces, looking the same in every shot. Natural English dialogue for Daisy only, accurate lip-sync. The dragon NEVER speaks; it only makes giggles, huffs, whimpers, gulps and purrs. Only Daisy and the dragon appear: no animals, no insects, no extra characters, and nothing stuck on the dragon's face.

0-3s: Hook, slow dolly-in at eye level: Daisy glides backward gracefully across the ice, then stops and points at the dragon, who stands at the shore in its huge skates with sparkling eyes. "Rule number one: knees bent."

3-6s: Macro close-up with shallow focus: the dragon's claws pull the thick laces tight on its skate. Cut to a medium shot: it steps onto the ice with its legs wobbling like jelly and its wings spread for balance, eyes wide. Daisy gives a thumbs-up. "Perfect!"

6-9s: Low tracking shot at ice level: the dragon starts to slide forward with its legs slowly splitting wide apart, whimpering, while Daisy skates beside it and holds one claw tip. "I've got you!"

9-12s: Wide static shot: Daisy does one graceful spin. The dragon copies her and spins like a top with its wings out and its tail whipping, a swirl of snow flying around it, then stops with spiral dizzy eyes, wobbling, wings drooping.

12-15s: Wide shot: the dragon slides toward the big snowbank, and Daisy skates after it. "Brake!" The dragon has no idea how, and crashes headfirst into the snowbank with only its tail sticking out, wiggling, while a cloud of snow falls down.

15-18s: Medium shot: Daisy grabs the tail and pulls the dragon out of the snow with a loud "pop". Snow covers its horns like a crown, and it giggles, shaking its head.

18-21s: Smooth side-tracking shot: Daisy holds one claw tip, and the two of them skate in a slow circle. The dragon, tongue out in concentration, glides smoothly, and its tail draws a perfect spiral in the snow on the ice.

21-24s: Wide static shot: the dragon tries a big jump with its wings open, lands flat on its belly and slides like a sled across the whole lake while Daisy skates beside it laughing.

24-27s: Close shot: the dragon slides to a stop with its snout exactly at Daisy's skates. She bows politely, and the dragon nods back solemnly, then stands up carefully.

27-30s: Wide golden shot, slow dolly-out: Daisy and the dragon skate side by side, and their blades leave crossing tracks on the ice that form a crooked heart. Daisy, to the camera, deadpan: "Next time... brakes." The dragon slowly slides away backward out of the frame, waving one claw. Freeze on the empty heart on the ice. Hold this shot until the end.

Realistic ice, snow, steel blade and fabric physics, expressive acting, cinematic scale. No slow motion, modern objects, subtitles or watermark.
