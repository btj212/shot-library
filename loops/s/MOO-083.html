Prompt

CHERRY BLOSSOM PICNIC IN KOREA 🌸 | A PERFECT SPRING DAY

Create an exactly 30-second ultra-realistic live-action travel vlog set in Seoul, South Korea, during peak cherry blossom season.

MAIN CHARACTER: A young Korean woman with long wavy dark brown hair, natural makeup and realistic skin. She wears the same cream cardigan, white top, pastel pink long skirt, white sneakers and beige shoulder bag throughout the video. Maintain perfect face and outfit consistency.

SCENE 1 (0–3s) — SPRING MORNING
Inside a cozy Korean apartment, she opens the curtains and smiles at the beautiful cherry blossoms outside.

SCENE 2 (3–6s) — FOOD PREPARATION
In the kitchen, she prepares homemade gimbap, washes strawberries and packs the food neatly into a lunchbox.

SCENE 3 (6–8s) — PACKING
She packs the lunchbox, strawberries, sandwiches, lemonade and camera into a woven picnic basket, then leaves home.

SCENE 4 (8–11s) — JOURNEY
She walks along Seoul streets lined with blooming cherry trees as pink petals gently fall around her.

SCENE 5 (11–14s) — PICNIC SETUP
She finds a peaceful spot beneath a large cherry blossom tree, unfolds a pastel picnic blanket and arranges her basket.

SCENE 6 (14–17s) — PHOTOGRAPHY
She photographs the blossoms with her digital camera, then smiles as a friend takes her picture.

SCENE 7 (17–21s) — PICNIC LUNCH
She opens her lunchbox, enjoys gimbap, strawberries and lemonade, smiling naturally.

SCENE 8 (21–24s) — SPRING MOMENT
She relaxes on the blanket, looking up at the blossoms as petals drift gently around her.

SCENE 9 (24–27s) — EVENING WALK
She packs her belongings and walks along a quiet blossom-covered pathway in warm golden sunlight.

SCENE 10 (27–30s) — FINAL SHOT
She turns toward the camera with a gentle smile, then walks away as pink petals float through the evening light.

VISUAL STYLE: Authentic handheld travel vlog footage, photorealistic 4K quality, natural expressions, realistic skin and movement, beautiful spring lighting, detailed food shots and natural camera focus. It must feel like a real day filmed by a friend, not an advertisement or AI video.

AUDIO: Only natural sounds such as birds, kitchen activity, footsteps, gentle wind, park ambience and camera shutter. No narration, dialogue or background music.

STRICT CONTINUITY: Keep the same woman, face, hairstyle, outfit and accessories in every scene. Maintain the exact story order and realistic progression from morning to evening. Keep the picnic basket, food and blanket consistent. No teleportation, duplicate people, outfit changes, distorted hands, artificial transitions or cartoon effects. Include all ten scenes within exactly 30 seconds.
