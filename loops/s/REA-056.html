Prompt

30-SECOND VIDEO — 16:9

Create one continuous 30-second ultra-realistic smartphone video filmed from inside a moving car on a wet rural highway during daytime. Natural handheld shake, windshield raindrops, autofocus hunting, realistic motion blur, exposure shifts and imperfect digital zoom. No cuts.

0–4s — DISCOVERY The car approaches a stopped farm truck carrying enormous watermelons. Its rear gate suddenly swings open, and dozens of giant watermelons begin rolling onto the wet highway. The camera zooms toward the unfolding chaos.

4–8s — THE GIANT CATCH A huge, heavyset man in a yellow raincoat runs into view carrying an oversized empty laundry basket. He struggles to catch a massive rolling watermelon, which drops into the basket and nearly pulls him off balance.

8–12s — UNEXPECTED CRACK He places the watermelon on the road and attempts to crack it open by dropping another watermelon onto it. The fruit bursts apart, splashing juice and seeds across the asphalt. He slips slightly but regains his balance.

12–16s — DOMINO EFFECT Several loose watermelons roll downhill, collide with one another and bounce off the roadside barrier. Their chain reaction sends a giant watermelon bouncing toward a parked roadside fruit stall.

16–20s — FRUIT EXPLOSION The giant watermelon crashes into stacked melons, triggering a spectacular cascade of rolling fruit. The startled vendor jumps aside as melons tumble across the shoulder. The camera operator instinctively jerks the phone toward the commotion.

20–24s — THE UNLIKELY LAUNCH One enormous watermelon rolls beneath the man's feet. He steps onto it, loses his balance and is launched upward in a believable comedic tumble as the watermelon shoots away beneath him. His yellow raincoat flaps, and his sandals fly off separately.

24–27s — MID-AIR CHAOS The man travels forward in a realistic ballistic arc toward a slowly moving delivery van with its rear cargo doors open. The camera tilts and zooms shakily to follow him.

27–30s — PERFECT LANDING He lands harmlessly in a towering pile of soft empty cardboard boxes inside the van. The boxes collapse around him, the van rocks slightly, and the driver turns around in complete disbelief. One sandal drops onto the road behind the van.

AUDIO: Natural highway traffic, rain, truck gate rattling, heavy fruit rolling, watermelon impacts, splashing juice, cardboard crashing, rushing air and authentic surprised reactions. No music.

REALISM RULES: Maintain one uninterrupted take with continuous physical cause and effect. Realistic gravity, momentum, object weight and collision physics. No teleportation, instant movement, duplicated people, disappearing objects or impossible trajectories. Keep the comedy non-graphic and injury-free. No cinematic cuts, slow motion, subtitles, captions, text overlays, logos, watermarks or artificial CGI appearance.
