Prompt

Create a 30-second ultra-realistic live-action Korean historical film set in a Joseon-era village in the late 1800s. High-budget Korean cinema style, natural acting, realistic environments and emotional storytelling.

CHARACTERS:
Mother: 68-year-old Korean woman, wrinkled gentle face, grey-black hair in a traditional low bun, faded beige jeogori and dark-brown chima.

Son: 28-year-old Korean man, lean face, short black hair, light stubble, exhausted eyes, worn dark-blue Joseon soldier robe, traditional fabric armor and muddy shoes.

SCENE 1 (0–5s): At dusk, the elderly mother sits outside her traditional wooden house holding an oil lantern, staring at the empty village road, waiting for her son who left for war. Autumn leaves blow gently in the wind.

SCENE 2 (5–10s): She walks slowly to the village entrance, searching the deserted dirt road. Seeing no one, she presses a folded piece of cloth to her chest and returns home with tearful eyes.

SCENE 3 (10–15s): Inside her dimly lit house at night, she prepares to extinguish her oil lamp when she hears exhausted footsteps outside. She freezes, turns toward the wooden door and notices a shadow beneath it.

SCENE 4 (15–20s): She slides open the door and finds her long-lost son standing outside, exhausted and covered in dust. He whispers emotionally in Korean, “Eomeoni…” (Mother). She stares at him in disbelief, her eyes filling with tears.

SCENE 5 (20–26s): She touches his cheek with trembling fingers, realizes he is real and embraces him tightly. He rests his head on her shoulder, silently crying. She whispers, “I knew you would come home.” Warm lantern light illuminates their emotional reunion.

SCENE 6 (26–30s): Inside the house, mother and son sit together sharing a simple bowl of warm rice. She holds his hand and rests her head against him, smiling through tears. The camera slowly pulls back through the doorway as the lantern flickers against the cold autumn night. End on this peaceful reunion.

VISUAL STYLE: Ultra-photorealistic live-action, authentic Joseon architecture and clothing, realistic skin texture, natural facial expressions, cinematic 35mm film look, subtle film grain, realistic depth of field, cool moonlight contrasting with warm lantern light, smooth camera movements and consistent character faces throughout.

AUDIO: Gentle wind, distant village ambience, footsteps on dirt, sliding wooden door and subtle emotional orchestral music. Natural, clear Korean dialogue.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: Cartoon, anime, CGI look, plastic skin, modern objects, exaggerated crying, artificial expressions, slow motion, shaky camera, changing faces, inconsistent clothing, deformed hands, extra fingers, duplicated characters, unrealistic lighting, oversaturated colours, subtitles, text, logos, watermarks.

Tags: period drama, restraint, lantern light, reunion, Korean dialogue, no slow motion
