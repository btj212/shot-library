Prompt

IMPORTANT VIDEO INSTRUCTION:
Every interaction must show the complete physical action clearly. Do not skip object handling. Whenever she buys or uses something, visibly show her reaching for the object, picking it up, holding it, exchanging money, receiving the object and physically carrying it afterward.

“Bye bye!”
