Prompt

Ultra-detailed 4K superhero transformation sequence, an original hero with an original costume design. Shot on a high-end cinema camera with premium macro and prime lenses, razor-sharp focus, rich colors, high dynamic range, extremely fine material textures, no film grain, no motion blur on the costume details.

Character: a strikingly handsome man in his mid-20s, athletic and well-built with broad shoulders, sharp jawline, high cheekbones, light stubble, warm brown skin, intense dark brown eyes, thick dark eyebrows, and short textured black hair swept back. Confident, calm and focused expression. The same man in every shot.

Setting: a dark, empty studio with a single beam of soft white light from above, faint dust particles floating in the beam, and cool teal and warm copper rim lights outlining the figure.

Costume design: a deep teal suit woven from fine fabric threads, fitted to his athletic build, with overlapping hexagonal ceramic scales on the chest and shoulders, copper plates on the knuckles and forearms, a small copper emblem shaped like a sprouting seed in the center of the chest, and a smooth matte teal mask with thin copper filigree lines and two softly glowing amber eye lenses.

Shot 1 (0-4s): Extreme macro close-up of his bare hand, palm open, veins and skin texture visible. Fine teal threads emerge from the wrist and quickly weave themselves around each finger, forming a glove. The woven pattern, individual fibers and tiny stitches are clearly visible as they tighten into place. Slow push-in.

Shot 2 (4-7s): Macro close-up of the gloved hand. Thin copper plates slide out and lock into place over each knuckle with small precise clicks, their brushed metal surface catching the light. The hand clenches into a fist, the fabric creasing naturally.

Shot 3 (7-11s): Close-up tilting up from his forearm to his chest. The woven fabric ripples across his body, then hexagonal ceramic scales flip open one by one across the chest and shoulders, each catching the light at a slightly different angle. The copper seed emblem forms last in the center of the chest.

Shot 4 (11-16s): Close-up of his face, unmasked, lit by the teal and copper rim lights. He lifts his chin and looks straight into the camera with intense, confident eyes and a slight smirk. Then the smooth matte mask flows upward from his jaw and covers his face, thin copper filigree lines etch across its surface, and the two amber eye lenses flicker on and glow softly. Slow push-in.

Shot 5 (16-20s): The camera pulls back from a low angle to reveal the full costumed hero standing tall in the beam of light, a short teal cape lifting gently in a breeze so its woven texture is visible. Dust particles drift through the light around him. He holds a calm, confident pose until the end.
