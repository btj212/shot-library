Prompt

@Image 1 efines the [BRAND] running shoe: its exact shape, the bone white midsole and the moulded white logo mark on the lateral side. Ignore its black studio background. defines the runner and the visual style only: the man, his face, build, sweat-darkened long sleeve, shorts and stride, and the cold-warm dawn grade. Ignore its environment entirely: ignore the wide multi-lane avenue, its tall buildings, its shuttered shops and its street layout. The setting of this film is described in Shot 5 and is an upscale American suburban neighbourhood, not the street in Image 2. He is already wearing the shoes from Image 1. Use exactly one runner and one pair of shoes. Shot 1: lateral tracking backward along a warm dim apartment at first light, soft golden dawn low across wooden floors. Through a bedroom doorway, held in passing: an unmade bed, duvet thrown back in one motion, pillow still dented. The track keeps moving. End state: the doorway slides away toward the kitchen. Shot 2: the same lateral track carries past the kitchen table in the foreground: an untouched breakfast, coffee full in its cup catching the warm light, a chair left angled. The track does not stop. End state: the table slides away toward the hallway. Shot 3: the track reaches the front door and eases to a hold: coats on hooks, an empty key dish, the cat already sitting in the hallway facing the door, tail curled around its feet. Off screen a phone starts ringing, unanswered into the warm rooms. End state: the cat holds its watch on the door as the second ring sounds. Shot 4: hard cut on the third ring: exterior, extreme close up in pure profile on the shoe from Image 1 mid stride, the white moulded logo mark filling frame, brick sliding out of focus behind it. As the sole meets the asphalt the motion ramps into slow motion for one readable instant, the midsole compressing, grit skipping off the surface, first sun raking the logo mark, then speed releases. Shot 5: cut to the frontal medium shot of the runner from Image 2, camera low ahead of him, travelling backward at his pace: he runs down the middle of a quiet street in an upscale American suburban neighbourhood at dawn, wide manicured lawns on both sides, large colonial and craftsman houses set back behind them, every window dark, white porches, mature trees arching over the street, a sprinkler running alone on one lawn, newspapers lying on driveways, a basketball hoop over a garage, clean parked cars on the driveways, the low sun flaring between the trees behind him. He runs through two full breath cycles, and a small private half smile arrives and settles, the expression of a man winning something no one is watching. The camera eases to a stop as he runs on into the flare. Warm golden dawn interior, honeyed low light, against the cool early street outside with its warm sun flare. Shot on 35mm anamorphic, tungsten-balanced 500 ISO colour negative, photochemical grain, natural halation, physically based lighting, real fabric, brick and asphalt response. No person ever appears inside the apartment and nothing moves there except the cat's tail. The apartment track is lateral and one directional, never reversing. No phone is ever shown, it is heard only. The street stays free of people and moving vehicles, parked cars only, every business closed. The only branding in the film is the logo mark on the shoe. Keep the film entirely free of on-screen text and subtitles. Audio: NO MUSIC. Raw diegetic SFX only
