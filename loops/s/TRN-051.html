Prompt

One continuous shot. The camera steadily follows a person in a black coat (refer to Image 1) moving from left to right through six connected rooms, each with a different color palette and atmosphere. Every room has the same structure: white walls, light herringbone wood flooring, French double floor-to-ceiling windows, and white sheer curtains, based on Image 2, but the view outside and the interior atmosphere are completely different. The protagonist walks at a constant pace throughout and passes through every open doorway in the walls. 0-5 seconds: The first room has an American comic-book fight theme. The protagonist enters and fights the character in Image 3; the character is defeated. 5-10 seconds: The second room has a warm, felt-crafted style. Outside the windows is a sunflower field from Image 4. Warm orange soft light fills the room, where a painter is painting sunflowers from Image 5. After entering, the protagonist also transforms into a felt-crafted style. 10-15 seconds: The third room has a sad theme. The entire scene uses a black-and-white comic stop-motion style. Rain falls outside, the interior lighting is cold gray and subdued, and a person sits alone on the floor in the center of the empty room, head lowered and knees hugged, with a phone beside them displaying an unanswered call. After entering, the protagonist turns off the light and immediately turns it back on. The room changes to color, and flowers instantly grow throughout it. 15-20 seconds: The fourth room has a joyful theme and is completely submerged in the sea, based on Image 6. The protagonist swims into the room, surrounded by beautiful coral reefs and schools of fish. 20-25 seconds: The fifth room has a surprise theme. Outside is a night sky filled with fireworks, based on Image 7.
